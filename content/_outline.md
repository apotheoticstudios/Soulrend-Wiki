## Origins

Races and classes you pick at the start of the game.

- [[origins|Origins]]
  - [[origins/races|Races]] — The Race layer: your body and its innate powers.
    - [[origins/races/human|Human]] — No benefits or drawbacks.
    - [[origins/races/shulk|Shulk]] — Armored shell-dweller: defence, impact, teleport, lava immunity, growing storage; slow and hungry.
    - [[origins/races/pixie|Pixie]] — Tiny flier with Flight Power, glide, hover and dash; low defence, villagers won't trade.
    - [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]] — Magic-focused: Mystic Affinity, Highborne (+50 mana, +15 regen); −4 HP, weak to physical damage.
    - [[origins/races/plague-victim|Plague Victim]] — Undead plague carrier: Foul Aura, Contagious Hex, cheats death every 5 minutes; frail, no trading.
    - [[origins/races/valkyrie|Valkyrie]] — Winged healer: free flight, Divine Smite every 3rd attack, +1 holy spell power.
    - [[origins/races/revenant|Revenant]] — Undead summoner: raises up to 8 undead (incl. Kamath), commands them; villagers refuse.
    - [[origins/races/forest-elf|Forest Elf]] — Archer: piercing arrows, extra arrows per craft, wolf/bear summons, eagle and golden wolf mount; vegetarian.
    - [[origins/races/goblin|Goblin]] — Gold-loving and lucky: better loot, faster; small, −8 HP.
    - [[origins/races/moon-elf|Moon Elf]] — Stealthy rogue: dagger, sai and cutlass bonuses, To the Shadows opener; −10 HP.
    - [[origins/races/siren|Siren (Race)]] — Water-dweller: amphibious, fast regen underwater, Sirens' Song; weak to fire.
    - [[origins/races/faded|Faded]] — Ghostly mage: +50% mana, +30% spell power, block phasing, Pocket Dimension; −90% melee, −50% HP. Mage classes only.
    - [[origins/races/keres|Keres]] — Soul-eating flier: Souls resource, lifesteal, bleeding fangs, reel-in; no natural regen, burns in daylight.
    - [[origins/races/ungoliant|Ungoliant]] — Spider: wall climbing, webs, grapple, poison; −30% HP, hungry.
    - [[origins/races/yeti|Yeti (Race)]] — Frost tank: Glacial Slam, +12 HP, slows nearby mobs; double fire damage.
    - [[origins/races/dwarf|Dwarf]] — Miner: 2× mining speed, +10 HP, +5 armor, Brew heal; blinded by daylight, sinks.
    - [[origins/races/orc|Orc]] — Brute: +25% damage, +50% HP, Bloodlust, Leap & Shockwave; slower, less luck.
    - [[origins/races/corrosid|Corrosid]] — Acid bird: flight, wing dash, acid fireball, poison immunity; fragile, bread only.
    - [[origins/races/feral-beast|Feral Beast]] — Momentum hunter: speed that builds while sprinting, Pounce, Blood Scent. Class locked to Wildheart.
  - [[origins/melee-classes|Melee Classes]] — Martial classes (the in-game Melee tab).
    - [[origins/melee-classes/warrior|Warrior]] — +15% attack damage, +30% crit damage. Starts with chainmail and a wooden greatsword.
    - [[origins/melee-classes/berserker|Berserker]] — +20% max HP, +25% crit chance. Starts with chainmail and an iron axe.
    - [[origins/melee-classes/rogue|Rogue]] — +20% dodge, silent footsteps. Starts with leather and a flint knife.
    - [[origins/melee-classes/tank|Tank]] — +20% armor, +30% max HP. Starts with chainmail, shield and sword.
    - [[origins/melee-classes/samurai|Samurai]] — +50% katana damage, +20% attack speed. Starts with leather and a bokken.
    - [[origins/melee-classes/lancer|Lancer]] — +25% damage with lances and spears. Starts with a wooden spear.
    - [[origins/melee-classes/beast-master|Beast Master]] — +15% battlestaff damage, stronger summons. Starts with summon spells.
    - [[origins/melee-classes/brawler|Brawler]] — +100% glove damage, +1 attack speed. Starts with two gloves.
    - [[origins/melee-classes/blade-dancer|Blade Dancer]] — +25% with chakrams, twinblades and warglaives, +10 armor negation. Listed under "Other" in game.
    - [[origins/melee-classes/wildheart|Wildheart]] — Feral Beast only: fist weapons, Frenzy stacks, heal on fist kills.
  - [[origins/ranged-classes|Ranged Classes]] — The in-game Ranged tab.
    - [[origins/ranged-classes/hunter|Hunter]] — +30% arrow damage, extra arrows per craft, hide in leaves, Looting II. Starts with a bow and quiver.
  - [[origins/mage-classes|Mage Classes]] — Every mage gets +50% mana, +30% school spell power and a starter spellbook.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/warlock|Warlock]] — Blood school. Bloody Arrow, Blood Step.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/voidmancer|Voidmancer]] — Ender school. Magic Missile, Teleport.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/summoner|Summoner]] — Evocation school. Firecracker, Shield.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/pyromancer|Pyromancer]] — Fire school. Firebolt, Burning Dash.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/cryomancer|Cryomancer]] — Ice school. Icicle, Frost Step.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/electromancer|Electromancer]] — Lightning school. Ball Lightning, Lightning Bolt.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/eldritch-cultist|Eldritch Cultist]] — Eldritch school. Abyssal Blast.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/druid|Druid]] — Nature school. Poison Arrow, Oakskin.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/geomancer|Geomancer]] — Geo school. Dripstone Bolt, Crystalline Conduit.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/tideweaver|Tideweaver]] — Aqua school. Hydroshot, Tidal Grasp.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/bard|Bard]] — Sound school. Default Note, Birds.
    - [[origins/mage-classes/priest|Priest]] — Holy school. Wisp, Blessing of Life.
  - [[origins/fusion-classes|Fusion Classes]] — Hybrid martial and magic classes.
    - [[origins/fusion-classes/paladin|Paladin]] — +20% holy spell power and armor, +20% healing received. Starts with Fortify and Heal.
    - [[origins/fusion-classes/spellblade|Spellblade]] — +20% mana, +25% spell power, +15% attack damage. Starts with Magic Missile and Teleport.
    - [[origins/fusion-classes/reaper|Reaper]] — +25% blood spell power, +25% with scythes, heal on kill; weak to holy.

## Findings for the pack

Problems found while researching the outline. They are about the modpack, not the wiki.

- The Act IV gate does not match its text: "Eight Sins" says to defeat four Sins, but the real gate needs any five Legendary Eye quests (min_required_dependencies 5) plus entering the End.
- The Nightwarden is shown as Act IV's goal but the quest is marked optional.
- Five "Move to chapter" link quests point at chapters that do not exist: Simply NightFall, Dungeons and Combat, Soulslike Weaponry, Epic Knight, and one Act II link in the Prologue.
- The four zips in the instance's root `datapacks/` folder may not load: Paxi only reads `config/paxi/datapacks`.
- `kubejs/client_scripts/jei_remove_hidden.js` reads `global.deleted_items`, which is set in a server script, so it may be undefined on the client.
- Origin descriptions disagree with the data: Priest holy power (50% vs 30%), several starting kits, Orc, Valkyrie and Moon Elf.
- `soulrend_reforged` is missing from Paxi's `datapack_load_order.json`.
- Recipes made inside KubeJS scripts (flesh cooking, Emblem of the Past) are missing from `game-data/recipes.json`.

## Open questions

- The "spellbinding table" is still unidentified (no jar has it).
- Is `nightwielder23` (Apothic add-ons, Iron's Spellbooks Tweaks) on the pack team? If so those mods belong under Soulrend.
- Act placement of side-chapter bosses (Mowzie's, Soulslike, Aquamirae, Caverns) is by the chapter's unlock point; nothing stops players doing them later.
- The Gate of Disaster, Ancient Church and Tree of Light may also generate outside the Past.
