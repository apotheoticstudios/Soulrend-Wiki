Quality-of-life, interface, map and recipe-viewer mods. Most are client-side conveniences. A few change gameplay, and those are marked.

## Recipes, items and tooltips

- **Just Enough Items (JEI)** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei)) — shows every item and recipe, and is how players look up what a station crafts.
- **Just Enough Resources (JER)** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/just-enough-resources-jer)) — adds mob drops and ore generation to JEI.
- **JER Integration** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jer-integration)) — adds more mod support to JER.
- **Polymorph** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/polymorph)) — lets players choose the result when several recipes match the same ingredients.
- **Client Crafting** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/client-crafting)) — crafts on the client without waiting for the server.
- **Obscure Tooltips** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/obscure-tooltips)) — restyles tooltips with borders and animated effects.
- **Item Borders** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/item-borders)) — draws a colored border around items in slots by rarity.
- **Item Highlighter** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/item-highlighter)) — highlights newly picked-up items in the inventory.
- **Get It Together, Drops!** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/get-it-together-drops)) — sets how dropped items merge, through tags and config.
- **Mouse Tweaks** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mouse-tweaks)) — adds mouse shortcuts for moving and splitting stacks.
- **StackablePotions** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/stackablepotions-forge)) — lets vanilla potions stack.
- **Loot Beams: Relooted!** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/loot-beams)) — draws colored beams on dropped items.
- **Loot Journal** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/loot-journal)) — shows item pickups as animated notifications.
- **Controlling** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/controlling)) — adds a search bar to the keybinding screen.
- **Raw's Visual Keybinder** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/raws-visual-keybinder)) — adds a keybinding screen that draws the keyboard and mouse.

## HUD and information

- **AppleSkin** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/appleskin)) — shows hunger, saturation and food values in the HUD.
- **Overflowing Bars** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/overflowing-bars)) — wraps health, armor and toughness bars into extra rows so high values stay readable. This matters in Soulrend, where stats go far beyond vanilla.
- **Stylish Effects** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/stylish-effects)) — redesigns the status effect display and makes it show in more menus.
- **YDM's MobHealthBar** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ydms-mobhealthbar-mod)) — draws a health bar over mobs.
- **JeremySeq's Damage Indicators** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jeremyseq-damage-indicator)) — shows damage text and a direction marker when you are hit.
- **Jade** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jade)) — shows what block or mob you are looking at.
- **Jade Addons** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jade-addons)) — adds more mod support to Jade.
- **Merchant Markers** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/merchant-markers)) — shows each villager's profession as a marker above them.
- **Villager Names** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/villager-names)) — gives villagers names.
- **Traveler's Titles** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/travelers-titles)) — shows a title when you enter a biome or dimension.
- **Advancement Plaques** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/advancement-plaques)) — replaces the advancement popup with a larger plaque.
- **Better Advancements** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-advancements)) — redesigns the advancements screen.
- **Toast Control** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/toast-control)) — lets players hide the corner popups.
- **Chat Heads** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/chat-heads)) — shows player heads next to chat messages.
- **Craft Down Under Client Companion** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/craft-down-under-client-companion)) — adds tabbed chat channels and filters for Craft Down Under's servers.
- **Configured** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/configured)) — adds an in-game config screen for each mod.

## Maps and finding things

- **Xaero's Minimap** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/xaeros-minimap)) — shows nearby terrain, players and mobs, with waypoints.
- **Xaero's World Map** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/xaeros-world-map)) — adds a full-screen map of explored terrain.
- **Explorer's Compass Edited** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/explorers-compass-edited)) — an item that searches for a structure and points to it.
- **Nature's Compass** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/natures-compass)) — an item that searches for a biome and points to it.
- **WITS** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/wits)) — the `\wits` command names the structures at your position.
- **Waystones** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/waystones)) — adds teleporting between activated waystones and warp scrolls. See [[features/travel-and-storage/waystones|Waystones]].
- **Waystones Teleport Pets** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/waystones-teleport-pets)) — brings tamed pets along when you use a waystone.
- **Open Parties and Claims** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/open-parties-and-claims)) — lets players claim chunks and form parties, with Xaero map integration. See [[features/multiplayer|Teams & Claims]].
- **FTB Ranks** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ftb-ranks-forge)) — adds ranks that grant permissions and name formatting on servers.

## Movement, camera and combat feel

- **Better Mount Steering** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-mount-steering)) — gives mounts a free-look camera so WASD steers where you look.
- **Shoulder Surfing Reloaded** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/shoulder-surfing-reloaded)) — a configurable third-person camera.
- **Shoulder Surfing Reloaded: Camera Fixes & Additions** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/shoulder-surfing-reloaded-camera-fixes-additions)) — adds fixes for the camera with Epic Fight, Better Lock On and Iron's Spells.
- **Clean Swing Through Grass** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/clean-swing-through-grass)) — lets attacks hit mobs through tall grass.
- **Smooth Movement** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/smooth-movement)) — smooths entity movement under lag.
- **Just Zoom** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/just-zoom)) — adds a zoom key and spyglass improvements.
- **MmmMmmMmmMmm (Target Dummy)** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mmmmmmmmmmmm)) — adds a target dummy that shows damage numbers and can wear armor, for testing builds.

## Gameplay changes

- **Corpse** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/corpse)) — leaves a corpse holding your items when you die. See [[features/travel-and-storage/corpses|Corpses]].
- **Corpse x Cosmetic Armor Reworked Compat** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/corpse-x-cosmetic-armor-reworked-compat)) — puts cosmetic armor back into its slots when you collect a corpse.
- **Corpse x Curios API Compat** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/corpse-x-curios-api-compat)) — makes corpses hold Curios items too.
- **Second Chance** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/second-chance-forge)) — saves a player from a lethal hit once instead of killing them. Soulrend enables it for mob attacks and explosions, but not for falls, anvils, lightning, magic or elytra crashes.
- **StartInv** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/startinv)) — makes new players invulnerable for 30 seconds on first spawn.
- **Vein Mining** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/vein-mining)) — adds an enchantment that mines connected blocks, 50 blocks per level, with no required tool.
- **What Durability?** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/what-durability)) — removes item durability, so tools and weapons never break.
- **Trade Cycling** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trade-cycling)) — refreshes a villager's trades without breaking its workstation.
