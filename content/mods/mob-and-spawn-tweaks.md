Mods that change how and where mobs spawn, and what they drop. The values below are the pack's current configs.

- **You Shall Not Spawn!** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/you-shall-not-spawn)) — blacklists entities from spawning, globally or per dimension. Soulrend stops these from spawning naturally: creepers, Ice and Fire's hippocampi and cockatrices, Samurai Dynasty's kawauso and komainu, The Graveyard's skeleton creeper and Alex's Mobs' crocodiles and cosmaws.
- **Anti Mob Farm** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/anti-mob-cheese)) — makes mob farms inefficient. Each kill in a chunk lowers that chunk's drop chance by 2%, and the penalty recovers 2% per minute. Loot only drops if players did at least 50% of the mob's health in damage, and only for mobs a player killed. Animals are not affected.
- **Ageing Spawners** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ageing-spawners)) — makes spawners wear out after a set number of spawns. Soulrend runs it in blacklist mode, so every spawner ages unless it is listed, with a limit of 15 spawns.
- **DespawnTweaker** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/despawntweaker)) — decides whether mobs from spawners may despawn (they may), and includes Let Me Despawn's optimization for the equipment mobs drop when they despawn.
- **No Baby Zombies** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/no-baby-zombies)) — stops baby zombies, husks, drowned, zombie villagers and piglin zombies from spawning, and with `preventHostileBabies` on, baby versions of any hostile mob.
- **Mob Sunscreen** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mob-sunscreen)) — stops mobs from burning in daylight. In Soulrend that covers zombies, drowned, skeletons, strays and every Ice and Fire mob.
- **Configurable Extra Mob Drops** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/configurable-extra-mob-drops)) — adds extra drops to any mob, with a drop chance. The pack uses it for quest and progression items: Nether seal pieces from witches and evokers, the Nether Crystal from the Warden, Cursed Souls from the Grave Watcher and ghosts, and End Remastered eyes from the Yeti, Infernal Dragon and Wither Skeletons.
