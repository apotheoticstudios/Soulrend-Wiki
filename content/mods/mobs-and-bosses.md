Mods that add creatures, bosses and factions, plus the ones that change how those fights behave. Their mobs fill the wiki's [[bosses/act-ii/frostmaw|boss]] and enemy sections, and Soulrend Core's Auto Leveling scales their stats (see [[leveling/mob-levels|Mob Levels]]).

## Mob and boss content

- **Alex's Mobs** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/alexs-mobs)) — adds 85+ animals and creatures with their own behaviors. Most of its animals are on Auto Leveling's never-level list, so they keep their base stats.
- **L_Ender's Cataclysm** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lendercataclysm)) — adds structures with end-game bosses (Ignis, the Leviathan, Scylla, the Harbinger and others), plus their weapons and armor. Cataclysm Dimensions moves its structures out of the vanilla dimensions.
- **Mowzie's Mobs** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mowzies-mobs)) — adds powerful Overworld mobs and bosses such as the Frostmaw, Ferrous Wroughtnaut, Umvuthi and the Sculptor. The Fallen Myths quest chapter is built on them.
- **Bosses'Rise** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/bossesrise)) — adds five souls-like bosses, each in its own dungeon.
- **Souls like Bosses Reforged** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/souls-like-bosses-reforged)) — adds the Nightlord boss, its weapon drop and an arena that generates in ocean biomes.
- **NightFall: Invade** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nightfall-invade)) — adds hard bosses based on Epic Fight: Nightfall.
- **Friends&Foes** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/friends-and-foes-forge)) — adds the mobs that lost the Minecraft Live mob votes, such as the glare, copper golem and iceologer, plus new vanilla-style structures.
- **Aquamirae** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/aquamirae)) — adds a ship graveyard with deep-sea creatures and the Eel, Maze Mother and other bosses. It has its own quest chapter.
- **Ice and Fire: Dragons** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ice-and-fire-dragons)) — adds fire, ice and lightning dragons and other mythological creatures, plus dragon gear. See [[items/dragons|Dragons]].
- **Dragon Radars** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dragon-radars)) — adds a radar that finds Ice and Fire dragons, with a search screen for radius and stage.
- **The Graveyard** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-graveyard-forge)) — adds graveyard structures, undead mobs, bosses and blocks.
- **Guard Villagers** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/guard-villagers)) — adds armed guards to villages that fight hostile mobs. In Soulrend they open doors but don't keep their shields raised.
- **Demi's Sky Arena** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sky-arena)) — gathers strong mobs from many mods in an arena. It backs the Sky Arena quest chapter and its shop.
- **Valarian Conquest** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/valarian-conquest)) — adds Valarian soldiers with their own armor.

## Fight behavior

- **Improved Mobs** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/improved-mobs)) — makes hostile mobs smarter and harder, with configurable AI and equipment.
- **Awakened Mobs** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/awakened-mobs)) — an Epic Fight add-on that arms zombies and skeletons with weapons and armor. Soulrend sets the weapon chance to 0 and turns mob armor off, so it adds nothing visible here.
- **Entity Respawner** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/entity-respawner)) — respawns bosses after a delay. Soulrend uses a 36,000-tick timer (30 minutes) for 14 bosses: Ignis, Maledictus, the Leviathan, the Ancient Remnant, the Harbinger, the Netherite Monstrosity, Scylla, the Ender Guardian, the Ferrous Wroughtnaut, the Frostmaw, the Sculptor, Umvuthi, the Accursed Lord and the Draugr boss.
- **Boss Ultimatum** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/boss-ultimatum)) — heals bosses, or removes them, when players die during the fight. Soulrend's config applies it to Soulslike Weaponry bosses with at least 2,200 health.
