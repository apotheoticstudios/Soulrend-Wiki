These client mods change the screens players see before they enter the world. None of them affects gameplay.

- **Cherished Worlds** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cherished-worlds)) — lets players favorite worlds so they stay at the top of the list and can't be deleted by accident.
- **Default Options** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/default-options)) — ships default keybindings and options without overwriting a player's own. The pack's default keys (Inventory on I, for example) are set this way in `config/defaultoptions/keybindings.txt`.
- **Drippy Loading Screen** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/drippy-loading-screen)) — a FancyMenu add-on for customizing the loading screen.
- **Experimental Settings Disabler** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/experimental-settings-disabler)) — hides the "experimental settings" warnings when creating or loading a world.
- **FancyMenu** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fancymenu)) — customizes Minecraft's menus: title screen layout, buttons, backgrounds and animations.
- **Modern World Creation** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/modernworldcreation)) — redesigns the Create World screen with a cleaner layout.
