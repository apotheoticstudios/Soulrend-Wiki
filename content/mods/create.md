Create adds kinetic machines, contraptions and its own crafting chains. In Soulrend it is a crafting and building tool: the pack replaces some of its recipes through `removals.toml` (it drops Create's Andesite Alloy recipe, for example).

- **Create** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create)) — adds rotating shafts, gears, belts, presses, mixers and moving contraptions powered by water, wind or stress.
- **Create: Copycats+** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/copycats)) — adds copycat blocks (stairs, slabs, walls, fences, panels and more) that copy the look of any block placed on them, for builders who want custom shapes.
- **Create Mechanical Extruder** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-mechanical-extruder)) — adds a block that produces blocks or items from the fluids and blocks next to it, the Create way to run a stone or ore generator.
- **Create Contraption Terminals** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-contraption-terminals)) — lets Tom's Simple Storage terminals work on Create contraptions, so a moving train or ship can carry a storage terminal.
