Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks provides the pack's spellcasting, and every other mod here either adds spells and gear to it or adjusts its numbers. The wiki's Magic section covers the 12 spell schools these mods fill.

- **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/irons-spells-n-spellbooks)) — adds mana, spells, schools, spellbooks, scrolls, staves, mage armor and magic mobs. It is the base of Soulrend's magic: casting uses Soulrend's own recipes and stations, and the spell pages are generated from its registry.
- **Iron's Spellbooks Tweaks** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/irons-spellbooks-tweaks)) — adds TOML options Iron's doesn't expose, such as mana, cooldowns, spell power and rarity gating. Soulrend uses it to retune casting.
- **T.O Magic 'n Extras** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/to-tweaks-irons-spells)) — an Iron's add-on with new spells, bosses, weapons, armor, curios, spellbooks and staves, integrated with L_Ender's Cataclysm and other mods. Its weapons are the "Traveloptics" items in the pack.
- **GTBC's Geomancy Plus** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/gtbcs-geomancy-plus)) — an Iron's add-on with spells inspired by Mowzie's Mobs and original earth magic.
- **Dungeons And Combat x Iron's Spells** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dungeons-and-combat-x-irons-spells)) — adds spell power to Dungeons and Combat's items and a few new items and spells.
- **Ice and Fire: Spellbooks** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ice-and-fire-spellbooks)) — adds Iron's spells and spellbooks themed on Ice and Fire.
- **Alshanex's Familiars** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/alshanexs-familiars)) — an Iron's add-on that scatters magical familiars through the world, which can be tamed and summoned to help in a fight.
- **KubeJS Iron's Spells** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/kubejs-irons-spells)) — lets KubeJS scripts create new spells. Soulrend uses it for custom spells such as Cannibalism.
