Shaders, models, particles, music and sound. These change how the pack looks and sounds without changing the rules.

## Shaders and rendering support

- **Oculus** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/oculus)) — a Forge fork of Iris that loads shader packs.
- **Colorwheel** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/colorwheel)) — lets Iris-style shader packs work with Flywheel, Create's rendering engine.
- **[EMF] Entity Model Features** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/entity-model-features)) — loads OptiFine-format custom entity models from resource packs.
- **[ETF] Entity Texture Features** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/entity-texture-features-fabric)) — loads custom, emissive and randomized entity textures from resource packs, and player skin features.
- **Forge CIT** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/forge-cit)) — loads OptiFine-style custom item textures from resource packs.
- **Sodium/Embeddium Dynamic Lights** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dynamiclights-reforged)) — gives held and dropped light-emitting items dynamic light.

## Effects and animation

- **Fancy Trail** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fancy-trail)) — improves the slash trails from Epic Fight weapons.
- **Particle Effects** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/particle-effects)) — gives each vanilla status effect its own textured particles.
- **Subtle Effects** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/subtle-effects)) — adds small particle and sound details.
- **ItemPhysic Full** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/itemphysic)) — makes dropped items lie on the ground with physics.
- **Falling Leaves** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/falling-leaves-forge)) — drops leaf particles from trees.
- **Better Clouds Reforged** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-clouds-reforged)) — draws volumetric clouds in the vanilla style.
- **Ambient Environment** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ambient-environment)) — adds ambient sounds and details to the world.
- **Presence Footsteps** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/presence-footsteps-forge)) — gives footsteps sounds that depend on the surface and the player's gear.

## Music

- **Biome Music** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biome-music)) — changes the delay between tracks and makes modded biomes play music.
- **Medieval Music** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/medieval-music)) — adds medieval-style music.
- **Mob Battle Music** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mob-battle-music)) — plays battle music when fighting hostile mobs and players.

## Cosmetics

- **Cosmetic Armor Reworked** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cosmetic-armor-reworked)) — lets players wear one armor set for looks and another for function, and hide armor.
- **Icarus** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/icarus)) — adds wings worn in the Curios cape slot.
