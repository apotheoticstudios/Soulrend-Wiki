Cooking, fishing and farming mods. They give the pack its food items and some quality-of-life changes for gathering them.

- **Farmer's Delight** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/farmers-delight)) — adds cooking pots, cutting boards, skillets, crops and meals. Its Cutting Board is one of the crafting stations the wiki shows in recipes.
- **Delightful** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/delightful)) — a Farmer's Delight add-on that adds more foods and ties Farmer's Delight to other mods in the pack.
- **Chef's Delight** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/chefs-delight-forge)) — adds two villager professions, Chef and Cook, which trade Farmer's Delight food.
- **Aquaculture 2** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/aquaculture)) — expands fishing with biome-specific fish, new rods and fish-based recipes.
- **No Farmland Trample** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/notrample)) — turns off farmland trampling, so walking or jumping on crops no longer ruins them.
- **Panda's Falling Trees** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/pandas-falling-trees)) — felled trees fall over as a whole instead of leaving floating logs, and the whole trunk drops at once.
- **Passable Foliage** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/passable-foliage)) — removes the collision from leaves, so players and mobs walk through foliage instead of being blocked by it.
