These mods add dimensions or lock the Nether and the End behind extra steps. They set the pace of the main quest line.

- **Dimensions of Alex's Caves** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dimensions-of-alexs-caves)) — gives the Alex's Caves biomes dimensions of their own, so those caves can be entered through portals instead of only found under the Overworld.
- **Cataclysm Dimensions** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cataclysm-dimensions)) — generates each structure of L_Ender's Cataclysm in its own dimension rather than in the Overworld, Nether or End. It underlies the Cataclysm boss arenas that quests send players to.
- **End Remastered** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/endremastered)) — makes the End portal take 12 different Eyes instead of Eyes of Ender. Soulrend's config turns off Eyes of Ender in frames, eye removal, the Cryptic Eye from enchanting and the Evil Eye from Clerics, and the pack's Act IV gate needs five of the 16 eye quests plus entering the End. See [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]].
- **Nether Remastered** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nether-remastered)) — locks the Nether behind a Seal of the Underworld. The seal needs Bottled Nether Essence, which drops from listed undead mobs when killed with an Obsidian Infused Bottle in the off hand, and portal travel checks for the seal's advancement. The seal is not consumed.
