Mods made for Soulrend. Together they hold most of what makes the pack different from its parts: the leveling system, the origin mechanics and the pack-wide rules.

- **Soulrend Core** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/soulrend-core)) — the pack's central mod: custom scripts, screens and compatibility code. It runs the player leveling system (damage-based skill points, stats, titles and ranks, see [[leveling/index|Leveling]]), replaces Auto Leveling's mob level calculation ([[leveling/mob-levels|Mob Levels]]), applies the recipe removals in `removals.toml` that swap about 680 mod recipes for Soulrend's own, and implements several class mechanics such as Beast Master, Hunter and Orc Crush.
- **Soulrend Origins** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/soulrend-origins)) — the Java side of the origins: Pixie flight, Shulk Phase Step, Yeti Glacial Slam, Revenant squads, Forest Elf companions and the other native abilities that the datapack powers trigger. See [[origins|Origins]].
- **Soulrend Tweaks** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/soulrend-tweaks)) — a small add-on with a two-handed weapons item tag, an off-hand restriction mode (set to `INERT`, so it does nothing) and a gem loss chance setting (0.5). It also ships Fallen Gems trait data.
- **Soulrend Companion** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/soulrend-companion)) — a client-side extension for Quasar Companion that lets players on Craft Down Under's official servers pick a Soulrend realm and keep one shared character.
- **Feelix's Soulrend Optimizations** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/feelixs-soulrend-optimizations)) — a performance mod made for the pack. It speeds up world loading roughly twofold, cuts JEI's wait after joining from about 90 seconds to 10–16 seconds, lowers server tick time and adds graphics presets from Potato to Ultra. Every change can be switched off from `/fsoclient`.
- **Soulrend - Spanish Translation** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/soulrend-spanish-translation)) — translates over 276 of the pack's mods and resources into Spanish. It activates when the game language is Spanish.
- **The Config of Alexandria** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-config-of-alexandria)) — a config-driven mod for controlling items: disable, rename or change their attributes. It is a general item-tuning tool for pack authors.
