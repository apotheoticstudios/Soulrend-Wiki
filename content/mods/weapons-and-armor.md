Mods that mainly add weapons and armor. Their items fill the wiki's weapon and armor pages, and most of them get Epic Fight movesets from the [[mods/epic-fight|Epic Fight mods]].

- **Simply Swords** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simply-swords)) — adds spears, glaives, chakrams, katanas, greathammers, greataxes, rapiers and many other weapons, including unique weapons with special effects.
- **Simply Swords: Overhaul** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simply-swords-overhaul)) — reworks a few unique swords and fixes bugs. It changes the text of eight uniques, and the wiki's tooltips show the changed text.
- **Knaves' Needs** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/knaves-needs)) — adds more swords for Simply Swords.
- **Marium's Soulslike Weaponry** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mariums-soulslike-weaponry)) — adds legendary weapons and bosses, with abilities shown on Alt tooltips and numbers read from its config. Every shield gets its parry. Soulrend removes its guns (the Gatling Gun, Hunter Cannon, Hunter Pistol and Blunderbuss) and Mehrunes' Razor through Item Obliterator.
- **Epic Knights: Shields, Armor and Weapons** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/epic-knights-armor-and-weapons)) — adds medieval armor, shields and weapons. Item Obliterator is set up to remove its estocs, stylets, bastard swords, ranseurs and ahlspiesses, though the patterns may not catch them (see [[bugs|Known Bugs]]).
- **Epic Knights: Ice and Fire** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/epic-knights-ice-and-fire)) — adds Epic Knights gear made from Ice and Fire materials.
- **Fantasy Armor (Medieval Series)** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fantasy-armor)) — adds armor sets with 3D models in a medieval fantasy style.
- **Cataclysm Weaponery** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cataclysmweaponery)) — adds ignitium tools and weapons for L_Ender's Cataclysm.
- **Too Many Bows** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/too-many-bows)) — adds bows with unique abilities. Soulrend removes the recipes for a few of them.
- **Samurai Dynasty** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/samurai-dynasty)) — adds Japanese-style armor and tools with 3D models, and its own mobs.
- **Dungeons And Combat** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dungeons-and-combat)) — an adventure-RPG mod with weapons, armor sets, bosses and structures. Its set bonuses and weapon abilities are described on the armor and weapon pages.
