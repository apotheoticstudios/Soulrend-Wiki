The mods behind Soulrend's quest book, origins, skill trees, levels and stats. Most of the pack's own data sits on top of them.

## Quests

- **FTB Quests** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ftb-quests-forge)) — the quest book. Soulrend's main line is "The Exalted Path", with progression set to linear and each act ending in a completion quest that opens the next. See [[progression/act-i-awakening|the progression pages]].
- **FTB Teams** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ftb-teams-forge)) — lets players form teams that share quest progress.
- **FTB XMod Compat** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ftb-xmod-compat)) — links FTB mods to other mods.
- **Quests Kill Task Tweaks** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/quests-kill-task-tweaks)) — improves how kills are credited to FTB Quests kill tasks.
- **UIQuest** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/uiquest)) — a client-side add-on that gives FTB Quests a different interface.

## Origins and classes

- **Origins** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/origins)) — lets players choose an origin with passive abilities and drawbacks at the start of the game. Soulrend uses it for the 19 races and 26 classes in its origin selector.
- **Medieval Origins Revival** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/medieval-origins-revival)) — adds origins inspired by mythology and fantasy, such as the Siren and Moon Elf. Soulrend's Paxi packs override several of them.
- **Medieval** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/medieval-core-mod)) — the core library for the Medieval MC modpacks, required by Medieval Origins.

## Skills, levels and attributes

- **Passive Skill Trees** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/passive-skill-tree)) — adds skill trees where points unlock passive bonuses. Soulrend uses four trees (Warrior, Ranger, Rogue and Mage) with data from a Paxi pack.
- **Auto Leveling** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/auto-leveling)) — gives mobs levels that scale their health and damage. Soulrend Core replaces its level calculation.
- **Additional Attributes** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/additional-attributes)) — adds extra attributes, for gear and skills to use.
- **Attribute Modify** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/attribute-modify)) — changes the attributes on items, vanilla or Curios, through datapacks.
- **Attributizer** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/attributizer)) — defines custom item and mob attributes in JSON, with Curios support.
- **Paragliders** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/paragliders)) — adds a glider for descending. Soulrend turns on its heart containers and spirit orbs and leaves the stamina vessel disabled.
- **XP Tome** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/xp-tome)) — adds a book that stores a set amount of experience.

## Other

- **Patchouli** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/patchouli)) — the library behind in-game guidebooks.
- **Runelic** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/runelic)) — adds a runic font that other mods use for their text.
