Building blocks, furniture and small world details. None of them changes combat or progression, but Every Compat and Quark add wood and stone variants that show up in recipes and creative-style building.

- **Quark** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/quark)) — adds many small vanilla-style features: new blocks, building variants, tweaks to mobs and controls. Its modules can be turned off in its config. Its blocks and tweaks are what players meet from it.
- **Quark Oddities** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/quark-oddities)) — an add-on to Quark with larger "oddity" blocks such as the Crate, Backpack, Pipes, Magnets and Matrix Enchanting.
- **Supplementaries** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/supplementaries)) — adds functional vanilla-style blocks: jars, signposts, faucets, sconces, planters, flags, the quiver and more. The pack uses its quiver (Hunter's starter quiver) and its slingshot-style items.
- **Amendments** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/amendments)) — tweaks vanilla blocks and items: cauldron potion mixing, animated lanterns, carpeted stairs, double cakes and better lily pads.
- **Handcrafted** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/handcrafted)) — adds furniture such as tables, chairs, couches, shelves and wall decorations in every vanilla wood type.
- **Furnish** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/furnish)) — adds a second furniture set with kitchen, bedroom and garden pieces.
- **Twigs** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/twigs)) — adds natural building materials: twig and bamboo blocks, rhyolite and schist stone, paper lamps, and extra variants of vanilla stone.
- **Every Compat (Wood Good)** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/every-compat)) — generates matching wood types for other mods' blocks (furniture, chests, boards) from every installed wood, so Handcrafted, Quark and Twigs pieces come in all woods. It adds about 1,700 items, which is why the wiki's icons for them had to come from an in-game export.
- **Fetzi's Displays** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fetzis-displays)) — adds display blocks for showing items, such as shelves, pedestals and frames, with a sign-like text overlay.
- **Medieval Paintings** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/medieval-paintings)) — adds 60+ paintings in a medieval-fantasy style to the vanilla painting set.
- **Toni's Immersive Lanterns** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/immersive-lanterns)) — makes lanterns swing with physics and give off dynamic light, and lets them be worn with Accessories.
- **Immersive Weathering** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/immersive-weathering-forge)) — adds natural weathering: moss, rust, ice and snow build up on blocks, and fire leaves charred wood.
- **Double Doors** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/double-doors)) — opening one half of a double door, trapdoor or fence gate opens its matching neighbor too.
