Dungeons, towers, villages and other generated structures. They are where much of the pack's loot and exploration happens. Lootr makes their chests per-player, and Structurify, Structure Essentials and WITS help control and identify them.

## Dungeons and towers

- **When Dungeons Arise** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/when-dungeons-arise)) — adds large roguelike-style dungeons and structures, many of them hostile.
- **When Dungeons Arise - Seven Seas** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/when-dungeons-arise-seven-seas)) — adds ships and sea vessels to explore.
- **Dungeon Crawl** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dungeon-crawl)) — generates large underground dungeons in the Overworld, a successor to Roguelike Dungeons.
- **BrassAmber BattleTowers** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/brassamberbattletowers)) — continues Battle Towers, with towers that hold bosses and loot.
- **Towers of the Wild: Additions** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/towers-of-the-wild-additions)) — adds Nether and End towers to Towers of the Wild.
- **Realm RPG: Fallen Adventurers** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/realm-rpg-fallen-adventurers)) — scatters skeletons carrying treasure around the world.
- **Integrated Dungeons and Structures** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/idas)) — adds detailed structures built with blocks from other mods.

## Redesigned vanilla structures

- **YUNG's Better Desert Temples** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yungs-better-desert-temples)) — redesigns desert temples.
- **YUNG's Better Dungeons** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yungs-better-dungeons)) — redesigns dungeons.
- **YUNG's Better Mineshafts** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yungs-better-mineshafts-forge)) — redesigns abandoned mineshafts.
- **YUNG's Better Nether Fortresses** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yungs-better-nether-fortresses)) — redesigns Nether fortresses.
- **YUNG's Better Ocean Monuments** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yungs-better-ocean-monuments)) — redesigns ocean monuments.
- **YUNG's Better End Island** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yungs-better-end-island)) — redesigns the main End island for a more interesting Ender Dragon fight. Soulrend's config makes every dragon kill drop an egg.
- **Integrated Stronghold** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/integrated-stronghold)) — replaces the vanilla stronghold with a detailed one.
- **Integrated Villages** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/integrated-villages)) — overhauls villages with blocks from other mods.
- **Integrated Cataclysm** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/integrated-cataclysm)) — rebuilds L_Ender's Cataclysm structures with blocks from other mods.
- **Luki's Grand Capitals** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lukis-grand-capitals)) — rebuilds vanilla villages and illager structures.
- **Luki's Woodland Mansions** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lukis-woodland-mansions)) — adds new woodland mansions.
- **Better End Cities - Vanilla** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-end-cities-vanilla)) — makes End Cities more intricate and loot-rich, with vanilla blocks.

## Added structures

- **YUNG's Bridges** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/yungs-bridges)) — generates bridges across the world.
- **Abridged** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/abridged)) — adds bridges of all sizes across rivers and valleys.
- **MVS - Moog's Voyager Structures** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/moogs-voyager-structures)) — adds 160+ vanilla-style structures with loot, enemies and villagers.
- **MES - Moog's End Structures** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/moogs-end-structures)) — fills the End with vanilla-style structures.
- **Towns and Towers** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/towns-and-towers)) — adds villages, pillager outposts and ships.
- **End Villager Outpost** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/end-villager-outpost)) — adds a large villager outpost on an End moon.
- **Medieval Buildings [End Edition]** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/medieval-buildings-end-edition)) — adds medieval-style structures to the End.
- **Medieval Buildings [Nether Edition]** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/medieval-buildings-nether-edition)) — adds medieval-style structures to the Nether.
- **Underwater Village** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/underwater-village)) — adds new oceanic structures.
- **Unnamed Sea** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/unnamed-sea)) — adds structures to sea and beach biomes.

## Structure control

- **Structurify** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/structurify)) — lets players and pack makers change structure spacing, overlap and terrain rules from a menu instead of datapacks.
