These mods change what the world looks like and where things generate. Most of them only affect new worlds, or chunks that haven't been explored yet.

- **Alex's Caves** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/alexs-caves)) — adds rare cave biomes under the Overworld (such as Magnetic Caves and Toxic Caves), each with its own mobs, blocks and items. Its creatures and items appear in the pack's gear and enemy pages, and [[mods/dimensions|Dimensions of Alex's Caves]] can move the biomes into dimensions of their own.
- **Bigger Trees** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/bigger-trees)) — makes vanilla trees larger and more varied while keeping their look. It changes forests only.
- **Cinderscapes** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cinderscapes)) — adds new Nether biomes with their own blocks and plants, so the Nether has more variety to explore after the seal is broken.
- **Climate Rivers** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/climate-rivers)) — reshapes rivers so they wind and vary with the climate instead of cutting straight lines through the terrain.
- **Deeper and Darker** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/deeperdarker)) — expands the Deep Dark with new mobs, blocks, armor and weapons, and adds the Otherside dimension. In Soulrend it backs the optional Deeper and Darker quest chapter (Act III-b), and the Warden is part of the route to the Heart of the Deep.
- **Deeper Oceans** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/deeper-oceans)) — makes ocean floors much deeper, which leaves more room for ocean structures and monuments.
- **Endless Biomes** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/endless-biomes)) — adds new biomes to the End. Together with Nullscape and The Outer End it fills the End with terrain to explore beyond the main island.
- **Incendium** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/incendium)) — overhauls the Nether with new biomes, structures, unique weapons and tougher mobs, using vanilla blocks.
- **Nature's Spirit** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/natures-spirit)) — adds Overworld biomes, trees, plants and animals such as the redwood and aspen forests.
- **Nullscape** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nullscape)) — replaces the End's flat outer islands with strange, varied terrain and a few new biomes.
- **The Outer End** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-outer-end)) — adds biomes and structures to the End's outer islands.
- **William Wythers' Expanded Ecosphere** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/expanded-ecosphere)) — adds close to 200 Overworld biomes in a more realistic climate-based layout. It decides the Overworld's overall terrain and which biomes your travels cross.
- **William Wythers' Overhauled Overworld** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/william-wythers-overhauled-overworld)) — rebuilds each vanilla biome with sub-biomes and more natural terrain.
