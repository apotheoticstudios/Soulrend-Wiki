Apotheosis and the mods around it give the pack its affix gear, rarities, gems and reforging. See [[items/gear-progression|Gear Progression]] in the item pages for how gear tiers work.

- **Apotheosis** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apotheosis)) — adds affixed gear with rarities, sockets and gems, plus the Enchanting Library, Reforging Table, Salvaging Table and Augmenting Table. It is the base of Soulrend's gear progression.
- **Apothic Attributes** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apothic-attributes)) — a library that adds new attributes (such as crit chance and armor shred) and a tooltip layout for them. Many weapon and armor stats come from it.
- **Apothic Curios** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apothic-curios)) — links Apotheosis affixes to Curios slots, so jewelry and other curios can carry affixes.
- **Apothic Extensions** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apothic-extensions)) — adds extra features to Attributes and Apotheosis.
- **Apothic Category Compat** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apothic-category-compat)) — sorts every weapon from other mods into an Apotheosis loot category (sword or heavy weapon by attack speed, plus bows and crossbows), so modded weapons can roll affixes.
- **Apothic Staff Rarities** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apothic-staff-rarities)) — extends Fallen Gems & Affixes' staff affixes to Apotheotic Additions' rarities.
- **Apothic Tooltip Cleanup** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apothic-tooltip-cleanup)) — shortens Apotheosis tooltips: it truncates affix lists, compacts gem displays and merges empty sockets.
- **Apotheotic Additions** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apotheotic-additions)) — an Apotheosis add-on that adds more rarities, gems, boss drops and gear.
- **Fallen Gems & Affixes** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fallen-gems-affixes)) — an Apotheosis add-on with many more gems and affixes and compatibility with other mods' items and spells.
- **Ancient Reforging** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ancient-reforging)) — adds a third reforging table for the Ancient rarity.
- **Esoteric Reforging** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/esoteric-reforging-forge)) — adds a reforging table for rarities above Ancient.
- **Astral Calamity Extras** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/astral-calamity-extras)) — adds extra materials from the Astral Calamity modpack, such as the Astral Calamity ingot chain used by high-tier gear.
- **Loot Integrations** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/loot-integrations)) — puts items from one loot table into others, giving modded dungeons better loot.
- **Lootr** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/lootr)) — makes loot chests per-player, so every player gets their own contents and nobody misses out.
- **Enchantment Disabler Tag** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/enchantment-disabler-tag)) — adds a tag that removes enchantments from enchanting, trades, loot and the creative menu. Soulrend's tweaks datapack uses it to disable Mending, Unbreaking, Life Mending and many mod enchantments (most of Alex's Caves' and several of Soulslike Weaponry's).
- **Enchantment Descriptions** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/enchantment-descriptions)) — adds a description of each enchantment to its tooltip.
