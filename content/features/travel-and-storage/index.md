**Travel & Storage Features** covers moving around Soulrend's large world and keeping what you collect. Waystones make long trips cheap, the storage guides explain how to store items without losing them, and the backpack and corpse pages explain what you keep when a trip goes wrong. The items themselves are on [[items/travel|Travel Items]] and [[items/storage|Storage Items]].

## In this section

- [[features/travel-and-storage/waystones|Waystones Guide]]: finding, crafting and using waystones, warp stones and warp scrolls.
- [[features/travel-and-storage/storage-practices|Storage Practices]]: upgrading chests in place, picking containers, and waterlogging chests against explosions.
- [[features/travel-and-storage/toms-storage-network|Tom's Storage Network]]: a step-by-step guide to connecting storage and using terminals.
- [[features/travel-and-storage/backpacks|Backpacks (Feature)]]: making, wearing and upgrading a Traveler's Backpack.
- [[features/travel-and-storage/corpses|Corpses]]: where your items go when you die and how to get them back.
