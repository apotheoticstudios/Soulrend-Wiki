**Settings & Performance Features** covers how Soulrend tunes itself for the player's PC. Most of it comes from Feelix's Soulrend Optimizations, the pack's own performance mod: one-click graphics presets, a first start that picks sensible settings, and terrain generated in the background while the player is busy.

## In this section

- [[features/settings-and-performance/graphics-presets|Graphics Presets]]: Potato to Ultra on the Graphics page of Video Settings, and the two shaders.
- [[features/settings-and-performance/first-launch-setup|First-Launch Setup]]: the starting preset, faster first loading, and better Java arguments against stutters.
- [[features/settings-and-performance/pre-generation|Pre-generation]]: terrain generated around you while you pick your origin or stand idle.
