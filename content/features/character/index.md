**Character Features** are the screens Soulrend adds for managing a character. The inventory gains a row of tabs that lead to the Levelling screen, the skill tree, the quest book and Epic Fight's skill editor, and the first join opens Soulrend's own selector for the character's race and class.

Levels, stats, titles and ranks have their own section: see [[leveling|Leveling]].

## In this section

- [[features/character/inventory-tabs|Inventory Tabs]]: the Inventory, Levelling, Skill Tree, Quests and Combat Skills tabs, and what each screen holds.
- [[features/character/origin-selector|Origin Selector]]: the paged race and class picker shown on the first join.
