**Magic Features** explains how Soulrend's spellcasting works. Magic comes from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks and its add-ons, with Soulrend's own settings on top: mana regenerates at a tenth of the normal rate, and imbued weapons cost mana. The spells themselves, the spellbooks and the crafting stations are on [[items/magic|Magic Items]]; familiars are on [[items/magic/familiars|Familiars (Items)]].

## In this section

- [[features/magic/casting-spells|Casting Spells]]: casting from a spellbook, a weapon or a scroll; the spell wheel and quick casting.
- [[features/magic/mana|Mana]]: maximum mana, how fast it comes back, and how to raise both.
