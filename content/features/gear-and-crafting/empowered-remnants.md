**Empowered Remnants** ([[item:simplyswords:empowered_remnant]]) are a Simply Swords material that Soulrend lets players get back from used-up relic weapons. Thirteen Nightfall and Epic Fight Extra weapons smelt into one, or two for the dual-blade weapon.

## How it works

Put one of the weapons in a furnace. Each smelt takes 1000 ticks (50 s) and gives 35 experience points. The recipes are plain `minecraft:smelting` recipes.
