**Features** covers the mechanics Soulrend adds or changes, beyond single items, bosses and quests. Each part explains how a system works in this pack and links to the item, boss or quest pages that use it. Leveling has its own section: see [[leveling|Leveling]].

## In this section

- [[features/character|Character]]: the inventory tabs, the Levelling screen and the origin selector.
- [[features/combat|Combat]]: two-handed weapons, lifesteal, mounted bonuses, boss damage limits and companion protection. Epic Fight has its own section: see [[epic-fight|Epic Fight]].
- [[features/gear-and-crafting|Gear & Crafting]]: upgrades, reforging, gems, scrapping and removed items.
- [[features/magic|Magic]]: casting spells, the spell wheel and quick casting, and how mana works.
- [[features/travel-and-storage|Travel & Storage]]: waystones, storage habits, Tom's Simple Storage, backpacks and corpses.
- [[features/multiplayer|Multiplayer]]: teams, claims and the official servers.
- [[features/settings-and-performance|Settings & Performance]]: graphics presets, first-launch setup and pre-generation.
- [[features/commands|Commands]]: `/soulrend`, `/origin`, `/skilltree` and other useful commands.
