**Boss Summons** are the four items that call up a boss at its altar or arena. Each one belongs to a quest line, and each quest page has the full walkthrough.

## Usage

| Item | Summons | Where to use it | Quest line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[item:deeperdarker:sculk_transmitter]] | [[Corrupted Champion]] | the altar in a Lich Prison; it is used up | [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/sculk-transmitter\|Sculk Transmitter]] (Act III-b) |
| [[item:aquamirae:shell_horn]] | [[Captain Cornelia]] | near water in an Ice Maze | [[progression/side-chapters/aquamirae\|Aquamirae]] ("Call Captain back from the dead.") |
| [[item:irons_spellbooks:cinderous_soulcaller]] | [[Echo of Tyros]] | rung next to the Cinderous Soul Rune under the Citadel's glass dome | [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/defeat-echo-of-tyros\|Defeat Echo of Tyros]] (Act III) |
| [[item:dungeons_and_combat:blessed_beetle]] | [[Sunleia]], again | the Forgotten Sands | [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/dungeons-and-combat-bosses\|Dungeons and Combat bosses]] (Act II) |

- The **Sculk Transmitter** has no recipe: an Ancient Temple's secret chest always holds one. It also goes into several of Soulrend's weapon upgrades. Its "Unlinked" tooltip is Deeper and Darker's own container-linking feature.
- The **Blessed Beetle** is for a second fight: Sunleia's first fight happens in her Pantheon without it.
