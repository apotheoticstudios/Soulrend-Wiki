**Magic Items** covers spellbooks, spells and spellcasting materials. Staffs are on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] and magic rings, necklaces and charms on [[items/curios-and-accessories|Curios & Accessories]]. To learn how magic works, start with [[items/magic/spells|Spells]].

## In this section

- [[items/magic/spellbooks|Spellbooks]]: the books that hold spells, including the unique books that come with locked spells.
- [[items/magic/spells|Spells]] — 219 spells across 12 schools, one page per school, plus how scrolls, spellbooks and casting work.
- [[items/magic/runes-orbs-and-inks|Runes, Orbs & Inks]]: runes, upgrade orbs, inks, spell slot improvements and other materials.
- [[items/magic/familiars|Familiars (Items)]]: the shards that create familiars and the trinkets that strengthen them.
- [[items/magic/magic-stations|Magic Stations]]: the Inscription Table, Scroll Forge, Arcane Anvil and Alchemist Cauldron.
