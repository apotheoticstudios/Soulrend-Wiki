**Act III Materials** belong to the Nether and the Alex's Caves biomes: netherite and other late vanilla materials, Dungeons and Combat ebony and crimson ingots, Alex's Caves minerals, and the Astral Calamity ingots that lead to the Brimspec Ingot.

## Usage

- **Two different Crimson Ingots** exist. Dungeons and Combat's makes crimson tools, weapons and shields; Soulslike Weaponry's goes into the Duskfire, Nightmare and Bloodcrest Ingots and several blood weapons. Both go into the Bloodcrest Ingot.
- **Ebony Ingot** makes Dungeons and Combat's ebony tools, long sword and shield, the Eternal Night Scythe and the Twin Shadows.
- **Alex's Caves materials** are mostly used for that mod's own gear:
  - Azure and Scarlet Neodymium make magnets and the magnetic tools.
  - Pure Darkness makes Shadow Silk, the Dark Gem Of The Living Void and darkness weapons.
  - Bottles of Radon make the radon lamps and the Polymer Plate.
  - Tough Hide and Heavy Bone make the Primordial armor.
  - Uranium makes uranium rods and blocks.
  - Toxic Sulfur Dust also crafts Gunpowder.
  - The Sweet Tooth makes the Sack of Sating.
- **Astral Calamity ingots** (Soulrend recipes, shown below each):
  - **Gilded Ingot** (gold and Lava Power Cells) and **Corrupted Ingot** (Cursium, Black Steel, Phantom Membranes and Echo Shards) feed the Brimspec Ingot; the Corrupted Ingot also forges the spell Echoes.
  - **Molten Ingot** (netherite scrap, Ignitium, Dying Embers and Blaze Rods) upgrades Dragonsteel armor.
  - **Bloodcrest Ingot** (both Crimson Ingots and a Blood Orb) upgrades the crimson blood weapons.
  - **Brimspec Ingot** (a Nether Star, a Void Core, and a Molten, a Corrupted and a Gilded Ingot; makes 4) is one of the pack's main upgrade materials: it goes into over 20 Mechanical Fusion Anvil armor and weapon recipes and into the Universium Ingot.
