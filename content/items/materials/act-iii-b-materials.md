**Act III-b Materials** are two late materials: the Soul Infused Ingot from Soulslike Weaponry bosses and the Moon Crystals that Soulrend adds for the Universium Ingot.

## Usage

- **Soul Infused Ingot** forges the Translucent weapons (with Lost Souls), the Arkenplate, Nightfall and the Soul Ingot armor, and goes into the Universium Ingot. The [[Returning Knight]] drops two.
- **Moon Crystals** (Nether Quartz around an Abyssal Amethyst) have one use: the [[items/materials/act-v-materials|Universium Ingot]].
