**Wings** covers 84 items (Icarus wings: 5 styles in 16 colors, plus 3 uniques).

## Usage

Wings go in the Curios back slot.

**Icarus wings** let the wearer fly. The five styles (dragon, feathered, light, mechanical feathered and mechanical leather) and the three unique wings (Discord's, Flandre's and Zanza's) differ only in looks. Soulrend's Icarus settings:

- Armor slows flight, by up to 3 times.
- Flying costs hunger (0.05 exhaustion), and the wearer needs more than 6 hunger points to take off; an empty stomach stops flight.
- Sneaking does not slow the fall.
- Wings have no durability.
- Flight is capped 64 blocks above the build limit.
- Loop-de-loops are allowed.
- Some dimensions forbid flying, and the Flightless effect stops it.

**Fairy Wings** (Dungeons and Combat) give Levitation while the wearer is crouching.
