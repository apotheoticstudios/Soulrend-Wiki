## Usage

These curios use hand, head, mask, charm, bracelet, talent and familiar-trinket slots. Effects beyond the attributes in the tables, from their tooltips:

**Familiar trinkets** (Alshanex Familiars): each makes one class of the wearer's familiars cast spells at a higher level, and adds bonuses such as that school's resistance and spell power. The **Pandora Box** summons or dismisses several familiars at once.

**Hands:**

- **Blazing Grips:** a chance to apply Blazing Brand on hit. **Chitin Claw:** +0.5 entity reach and +0.75 block reach. **Sticky Gloves:** stop Koboletons from stealing items. (Cataclysm)
- **Blessed Bandages:** immunity to Bleeding. **Flaming Gloves:** Strength while enemies are burning. **Scorpion Claw:** more block reach. **Steel Shoulder:** more armor toughness. **Hand of Sunleia:** Haste II when attacking. (Dungeons and Combat)
- **Windwoven Gloves:** faster bow draw.

**Feet:** the **Hunter Boots** add movement speed.

**Head and mask:**

- **Engineer's Goggles** (Create): show stress and capacity of kinetic components, network statistics on stressometers, and the contents of some blocks.
- **Kitsune Mask:** Fire Resistance and immunity to Blindness and Slowness. **Oni Mask:** Night Vision and immunity to Slowness and Wither. (Samurai Dynasty, under Shift)
- The Aptrgangr and Draugr heads, the Kobolediator skull and the Flower Crown have no ability text.

**Echoes** (charms): each adds +15% power to one school. Right-clicking an unimbued echo spends experience to imbue it with a spell of that school.

**Bracelets and talents** change spells:

- **Bracelet of Inversion** (Weeping Cluster): crouch-casting makes the cluster absorb damage the caster takes instead of damage they deal, with a 70% lower absorb cap.
- **Azure Ignition Bracelet:** Ignis-themed spells burn with soul fire, raising their damage.
- **Bottled Raincloud** (Rainfall): the rain turns acid, stripping enemies' beneficial effects and applying Corroded; +2 s duration.
- **Cryostorm Bracelet:** ice magic damage on a Wet target summons a 5 s blizzard that damages and slows nearby entities (once every 8 s).
- **Hydrocharge Bracelet:** lightning magic damage on a Wet target deals bonus damage and chains to 2 more entities (once every 1.5 s).
- **Nightstalker's Band** (Reversal): Reversal can be cast without a weapon, and reflecting a projectile gives Assassin for 5 s (more speed and much more attack damage, ending on the next hit).
