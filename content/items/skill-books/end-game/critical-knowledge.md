**Critical knowledge** is a Passive skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a End Game book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Give you 20% chance to deal 60% more damage.
>
> Critical chance is increased with Fire protection.
>
> Critical damage is increased with Blast protection

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped.
