**All Eyes on Me** is an Identity skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a End Game book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> dealing damage with 8 different attack will trigger "look - at - me".
>
>  look - at - me :
>   Regenerate your health, stamina and innate power moderably over 4 sec however, performing action during that time will cancel that effect.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default).
