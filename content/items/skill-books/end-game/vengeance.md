**Vengeance** is a Passive skill from Epic Fight. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a End Game book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Gain a damage bonus against an enemy that recently hurt you by 30%. You can switch the target after 4 seconds since you don't hit or get hurt by the current target. Killing the target will grant you a 30% damage increase that gradually decreases for 10 seconds.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped.
