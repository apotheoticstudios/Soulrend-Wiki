**Time travel** is a Dodge skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a End Game book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Register your position and health on cast.
> After 5sec you'll be teleported back to your previus position and half of all damage taken within this time is healed.
>
> Cast again to teleport early or sneak to cancel.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Dodge** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It replaces the dodge, which is on **Left Alt** by default. It uses stamina.
