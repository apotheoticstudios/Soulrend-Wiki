**All Eyes on You** is an Identity skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a End Game book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Blocking or dodging 4 different attack will trigger "And watch over you".
>
>  And watch over you :
>   Regenerate your stamina and innate power slowly over 8 sec.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default).
