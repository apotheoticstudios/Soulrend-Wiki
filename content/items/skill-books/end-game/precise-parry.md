**『PRECISE PARRY』** is a Passive skill from EpicFight Nightfall. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a End Game book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Parry within Parry:
> -More precise parrying; More fierce counterattacks.
> -Triggering parry at the perfect moment grants significant buffs and enhances the next attack within 0.7s.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped.
