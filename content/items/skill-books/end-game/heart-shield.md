**Heart shield** is a Passive skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a End Game book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Give you 20 shield point that will recover if no action is performed for 0.0sec at the rate of 1 point every 0.0sec.
>
> Shield recovery rate can be improved with Protection enchantment.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped.
