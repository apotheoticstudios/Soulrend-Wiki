**Voodoo magic** is an Identity skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a End Game book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Loosing Health increase Stamina by half,
> Loosing Stamina increase Health by half.
>
> Sneak to transfer health into stamina at double rate.
>
> Disable stamina auto recovery.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default).
