**Lethal Focus** is a Passive skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a End Game book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Each instance of damage will increase your damage by 4% until it reaches 80%.
>  If no action is performed within 2sec, the damage will decrease rapidly.
>
> Damage addup is increased with Fire protection.
>
> Damage max is increased with blast protection lvl.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped.
