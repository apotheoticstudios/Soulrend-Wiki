**Spider techniques** is a Mover skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 1 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Allow you to run on walls.
>
>  Hold sprint and jump toward a wall to run on it.
>  Release sprint to fall or release movement input to slide slowly.
>  Sneak to reduce the slide fall even more.
>
>  You can jump from a wall to another by adjusting horizontal view angle.
>  The position you jump from will adjust the hight of the wall jump.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Mover** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It is used with the mover key, **Space** by default. It uses stamina.
