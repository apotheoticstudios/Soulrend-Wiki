**Demolition Leap** is a Mover skill from Epic Fight. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 1 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Gather energy to launch yourself into a targeted direction, reducing next fall damage by 50%.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Mover** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It is used with the mover key, **Space** by default. It uses stamina.
