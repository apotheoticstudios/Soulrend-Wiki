**Dodge master** is a Dodge skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 1 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Instinctly dodge a small distance if you would take damage after the cast within a window of 0.30s. Continuesly dodge all attack after the first one within a window of 0.50s and after each succesful dodge.
>
> Double stamina consumption on multiple cast.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Dodge** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It replaces the dodge, which is on **Left Alt** by default. It uses stamina.
