**Back and ... FORTH !** is an Identity skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 1 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Allow you to blackout your target after reaching a certain distance from it.
>
> Attack your target once then move away from it.
> Once the distance beetween you and your target is greater than 20,your next dash attack will be a strong stun that blackout your target for 5sec.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default).
