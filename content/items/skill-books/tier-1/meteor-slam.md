**Meteor Slam** is an Identity skill from Epic Fight. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 1 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Apocalypse descended from the sky.
>
> Crash into the ground with a powerful slam. Height increases damage done and stamina consumed. Take damage if fall damage exceeds stamina limit.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). The skill only applies to these weapon categories: Greatsword, Longsword, Spear, Tachi.
