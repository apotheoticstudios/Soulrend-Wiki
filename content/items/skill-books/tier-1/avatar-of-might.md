**Avatar of Might** is an Identity skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 1 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Holding sneak for 2 sec will release a chockwave that pushes enemy away and grant you with Stun immunity for a short period.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default).
