**Shooting Style** is an Identity skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 1 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> When empty handed, holding sneak while attacking will do kicks.
>
>  The combo contain 3 different kick.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default).
