**Natural Sprinter** is a Mover skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 1 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Improve running ability.
>
>  While [HOLDING] your sprint key for 1 sec,
>  burst into a faster sprinting motion.
>
>  Releasing sprint button will perform a [Burst step]:
>  -  Short Boost forward at the cost of stamina.
>
>  Jumping while spritting will lunch you further and slightly higher.
>  Sneaking while sprinting will allow you to slide into 1 block gap.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Mover** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It is used with the mover key, **Space** by default. It uses stamina.
