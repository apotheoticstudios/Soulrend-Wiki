**Emergency Escape** is a Passive skill from Epic Fight. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 2 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Allows a player dodging when being hit or out of stamina.
>
> Allows a player canceling attack motions and dodge. (Always activated)

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped. The skill only applies to these weapon categories: Amethyst Rapier, Dagger, Enderite Rapier, Fist, Oceanite Rapier, Rapier, Sword, Uchigatana, Witherite Rapier. It has a cooldown.
