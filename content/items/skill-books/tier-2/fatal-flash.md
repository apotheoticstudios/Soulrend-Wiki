**Fatal Flash** is an Identity skill from EpicFight-Resurrection. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 2 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Lock onto a low-health enemy and perform a kill, which cannot be interrupted during execution (Weapons allowed: uchigatana, katana, tachi, longsword)

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It has a cooldown.
