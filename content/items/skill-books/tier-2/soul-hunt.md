**『Soul Hunt』** is an Arts skill from EpicFight Nightfall. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 2 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Press [C] to launch an assault forward; 60.0s cooldown 
> -『Soul Hunt』 reduces cooldown by 6.0s per kill during cooldown period 
> -『Soul Hunt』 directly killing target reduces cooldown by 60.0s, gain 1 innate skill stacks 
> -『Soul Hunt』 hitting guard-broken target increases damage to 2.5x, impact to 2.5x, and reduces cooldown by 50.0s, gain 5 innate skill stacks 
> -『Soul Hunt』 directly killing target or hitting guard-broken target grants significant buffs

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Arts** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It takes EpicFight Nightfall's Arts slot.
