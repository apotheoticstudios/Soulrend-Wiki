**Revelation** is an Identity skill from Epic Fight. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 2 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Eye of Hawk catching the weakpoints of the enemies
>
> Blocking, Parrying, and Dodging gives stacks of Revelation. Reaching the indicated amount of stacks will allow a player to unleash a stunning counter attack.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default).
