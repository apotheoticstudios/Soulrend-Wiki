**Dread full buster** is a Guard skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 2 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Windup your weapon for 1 to 2s, then, release a powerfull attack that bypass guard and cause blackout if the target was blocking or attacking.
>
>  Available Weapon Types : greatsword, uchigatana, longsword, sword, tachi, spear, agony, ruine

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Guard** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It replaces the guard, used by holding **right mouse** with a weapon that can guard. The skill only applies to these weapon categories: Axe, Greatsword, Longsword, Spear, Sword, Tachi, Uchigatana. It uses stamina.
