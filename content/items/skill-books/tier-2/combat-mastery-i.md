**Combat Mastery I** is a Passive skill from Epic Fight Extra. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 2 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> You have grown more adept at seizing opportunities between offense and evasion. You can dodge while attacking, or follow up with an attack after dodging.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped.
