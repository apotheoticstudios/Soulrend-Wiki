**『Gravity Stomp』** is an Arts skill from EpicFight Nightfall. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 2 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Press [C] to consume 6.0 stamina for a wide-area stomp attack; 20.0s cooldown 
> -Deals extra damage to 'Vex' entities

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Arts** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It takes EpicFight Nightfall's Arts slot.
