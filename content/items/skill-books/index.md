**Skill Books** teach Epic Fight skills. Every book is the same [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] item carrying one skill; right-clicking it and pressing **Learn** teaches the skill, which is then equipped in the Skill Editor (**K** by default). Each skill fills one slot: Dodge, Guard, Mover, Identity, Passive, or EpicFight Nightfall's Arts. Soulrend adds a crafting recipe for every book, and the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter asks for each one.
