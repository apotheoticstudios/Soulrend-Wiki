**Perfect bulwark** is a Guard skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 3 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Holding Guard stance will block attacks from all directions at low progressive stamina cost.
> Releasing guard after being hit 5 times will send a shockwave that pushes enemys away. 
>
> Available Weapon Types : uchigatana, longsword, sword, tachi, spear, agony, ruine, staff

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Guard** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It replaces the guard, used by holding **right mouse** with a weapon that can guard. The skill only applies to these weapon categories: Axe, Blackstar, Claws, Evil Tachi, Greatsword, Hollow Longsword, Horus Fang, Iron Fist, Longsword, Nova, Poleblade, Ritual Daggers, Shield, Sickles, Spear, Sword, Tachi, Uchigatana, Greataxes. It uses stamina.
