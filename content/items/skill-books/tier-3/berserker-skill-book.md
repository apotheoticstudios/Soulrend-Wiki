**Berserker (Skill Book)** is a Passive skill from Epic Fight. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 3 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Gain 0.5% additional attack damage and 0.3% additional attack speed for each 1% of missing health.
>
> Additional attack speed can't exceed over 5.0

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped.
