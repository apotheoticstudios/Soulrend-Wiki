**Lunatic Vivacity** is an Identity skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 3 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Cycle between 2 phase while facing a target.
>
>  [Lunatic Rush] Decrease your stamina consumption by 85%.
>  Not facing target will stop the timer but not grant any stamina reduction.
>
>  [Lunatic Linger] Increase your stamina consumption by 85%.
>  Facing a target will double the speed of the timer.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Identity** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default).
