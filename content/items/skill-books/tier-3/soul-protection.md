**Soul protection** is a Guard skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 3 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Loose the ability to guard.
>
>  Each second you summon a soul that give 1% damage negation.
> Your own soul give you 20% damage negation.
>
>  Getting damaged will remove 3 souls.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Guard** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It replaces the guard, used by holding **right mouse** with a weapon that can guard.
