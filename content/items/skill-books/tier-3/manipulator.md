**Manipulator** is a Passive skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 3 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Blocking an attack will grant you stamina based on the timming of the block and your missing stamina.
>
>  Perfect block = 60% missing stamina 
>
>  Regular block = 0~30% missing stamina

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped.
