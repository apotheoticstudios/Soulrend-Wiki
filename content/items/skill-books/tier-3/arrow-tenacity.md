**Arrow tenacity** is a Passive skill from Weapons of Miracles. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 3 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Prevent the stun from arrows damage. This skill is permanent, thus you can use another passive on top of it but, you'll only keep the current passive if you die.

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped.
