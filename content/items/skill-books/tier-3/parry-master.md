**『PARRY_MASTER』** is a Passive skill from EpicFight Nightfall. A [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] carrying it teaches it; in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] quest chapter it is a Tier 3 book.

## Usage

> [!LORE] Skill tooltip
> Gains skill charge after each parry
> -0.1% chance to ignore enemy defense (increases per successful parry)
> -Enables ICHIMONJI counters when wielding tachi/uchigatana

Right-click the book and press **Learn**, then equip the skill in the **Passive** slot of the Skill Editor (**K** by default). It works on its own while equipped. It has a cooldown.
