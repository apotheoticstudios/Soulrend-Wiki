**Items** — Every gameplay-relevant item group in Soulrend.

## In this section

- [[items/weapons|Weapons]] — Weapons grouped by their Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter, each in quest order. Epic Knights weapons are on one page; weapons no quest mentions are on Misc Weapons.
- [[items/armor|Armor]] — The three armor paths from Rise of the Champion, each with its dragon line merged in, then Mage Armor, Epic Knights Armor and Misc Armor. One page per set for every set a quest mentions.
- [[items/magic|Magic Items]] — Spellbooks, spells and spellcasting gear. Staffs are on Weapons → Magic Weapons; magic curios are on Curios & Accessories.
- [[items/dragons|Dragon Items]] — Ice and Fire dragon materials, eggs, crystals and forges.
- [[items/boss-drops|Boss Drops]] — Materials and trophies that come from bosses.
- [[items/progression-keys|Progression Keys]] — Eyes, keys, seals and summoning items that gate progression.
- [[items/gear-progression|Gear Progression]] — Apotheosis reforging, gems, sigils, augments and rarity materials.
- [[items/skill-books|Combat Skill Books]] — Books that teach Epic Fight skills, one page per book, sorted by their tier in the Epic Fight quest chapter (the chapter reads right to left: Tier 1, 2, 3, End Game).
- [[items/tools|Tools]] — Tool tiers from vanilla and mods, one page per tool type. Each page lists every material tier.
- [[items/consumables|Consumables]] — Healing items, totems and food, one page per category.
- [[items/materials|Materials]] — Ingots, gems and mob drops used in crafting, many per page, one page per act in progression order.
- [[items/curios-and-accessories|Curios & Accessories]] — One page per curio slot. The index adds a second sort by class or purpose (melee, mage, ranged, summoner, utility). Magic curios live here now.
- [[items/travel|Travel Items]] — Teleporting, gliding and finding your way.
- [[items/storage|Storage Items]] — Chests, drawers and storage networks.
- [[items/decoration-and-building|Decoration & Building Items]] — Collapsed groups for the thousands of building and decoration blocks, one page per group. Minimal info, mostly an index.
