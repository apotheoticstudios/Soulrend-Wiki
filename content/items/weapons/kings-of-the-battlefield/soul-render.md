**Soul Render** is a spear from cataclysm. It is weapon 45 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

P1nero's Epic Cataclysm turns it into an Epic Fight weapon with a skill-point system, explained in its tooltip:

- A perfect dodge or a perfect parry earns Skill Points.
- **1:** dash forward, leaving phantom halberds along the path. 2 Skill Points.
- **2:** slam the ground, dealing heavy damage and stunning entities around the wielder. 2 Skill Points.
- **3:** summon many phantom halberds around the wielder. 3 Skill Points.

Cataclysm's own text (Rush of Render, and phantom halberds in a spiral when used while sneaking) is not shown in its tooltip in Soulrend.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a sword. Its default Two Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and an innate skill of its own.
