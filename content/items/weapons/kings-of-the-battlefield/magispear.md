**Magispear** is a spear from Simply Swords. It is weapon 38 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Magislam** (unique effect): each hit has a 35% chance to deal extra magic damage.
- **On right-click:** the wielder leaps forward and slams down, damaging enemies within 4 blocks. They are immune to damage during the leap. Cooldown 7 s.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Orbital beam.
