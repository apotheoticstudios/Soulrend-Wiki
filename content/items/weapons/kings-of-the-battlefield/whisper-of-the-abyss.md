**Whisper of the Abyss** is a spear from Aquamirae. It is weapon 49 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 18.5 |
| Attack Speed | 0.80 |
| Impact | 2.5 |
| Targets per swing | 4 |
| Armor Negation | 30% |
| Entity Reach | 3.75 |
| Enchantability | 14 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It is a Sea Wolf weapon from Aquamirae. Its ability, **Perforation** (tooltip under Shift), lowers the target's armor by 10% for a while, stacking up to 5 times.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Grasping Spire. It has one other style, shown below.
