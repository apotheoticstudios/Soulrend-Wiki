## Usage

Its tooltip describes a heavy, all-netherite lance that "defies conventional spear techniques". Its innate skill, **Embers**, builds up flames that peak in a whirlwind attack. See [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Active Skill-Embers. It also has a passive skill.
