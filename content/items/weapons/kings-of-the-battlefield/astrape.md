**Astrape** is a spear from cataclysm. It is weapon 42 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

P1nero's Epic Cataclysm turns it into an Epic Fight weapon with a skill-point system, explained in its tooltip:

- A perfect dodge or a perfect parry earns Skill Points.
- **1:** release a torrent forward that calls lightning along its path and summons Thunderfall Canglings. 2 Skill Points.
- **2:** call very wide lightning strikes and many Thunderfall Canglings at the target location. 3 Skill Points.

Cataclysm's own text (right-click to shoot a Lightning Spear) is not shown in its tooltip in Soulrend.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a sword. Its default Two Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and an innate skill of its own.
