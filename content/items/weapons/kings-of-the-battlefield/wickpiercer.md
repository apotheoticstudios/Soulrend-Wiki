**Wickpiercer** is a spear from Simply Swords. It is weapon 48 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Flicker Fury** (unique effect): when the wielder would die, regenerative wax heals them to full health and gives them Resistance for a short time instead. That can happen once every 60 s, and only while it is in the main hand.
- **On right-click:** the wielder gets a large attack speed bonus, and each strike hits twice for 2 s. Cooldown 11 s.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a seven-hit basic combo and the innate skill Sky dive.
