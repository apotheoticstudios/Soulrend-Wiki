**Flamewind** is a glaive from Simply Swords. It is weapon 50 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

- **Emberstorm** (unique effect): when a flame seed detonates, the wielder gets Haste.
- **On right-click:** the wielder plants a seed of flame in the nearest enemy, dealing damage over time (5 in Soulrend). When it expires, the seed detonates for area damage (15) and spreads to nearby enemies, up to 6 times. Cooldown 49 s.

The ability scales with Fire spell power.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Grasping Spire.
