**CrescentMoon** is a spear from EpicFight Nightfall. It is weapon 40 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

Its tooltip reads "This Weapon Smelts Enemy's Soul". Its innate skill, **Soul Fusion**, deploys a domain that gives super armor and heals the wielder when hit, and its passive adds stacking true damage. See [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed：CrescentMoon style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Soul Fusion.
