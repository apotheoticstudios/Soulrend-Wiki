**Arcanethyst** is a halberd from Simply Swords. It is weapon 43 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

## Usage

- **Arcane Assault** (unique effect): each hit has a 25% chance to make the target levitate.
- **Hold right-click:** the wielder channels arcane energy at enemies within 6 blocks, lifting them into the air and damaging them over time, then slams them into the ground. Cooldown 11 s.

The ability scales with Arcane spell power.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Sky dive.
