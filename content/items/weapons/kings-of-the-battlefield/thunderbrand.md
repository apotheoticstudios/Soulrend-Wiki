**Thunderbrand** is a halberd from Simply Swords. It is weapon 51 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

- **Thunder Blitz** (unique effect): each hit has a 15% chance to reset the ability's cooldown.
- **Hold right-click:** the wielder slows down and charges the weapon, damaging nearby enemies. Once charged, they dash forward with Haste, dealing heavy damage to enemies in the way. Cooldown 12.5 s.

The ability scales with Lightning spell power.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Piercing Rush.
