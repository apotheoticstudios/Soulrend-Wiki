**Iudex Gundyr's Halberd** is a halberd from EpicFight - Dawnday. It is weapon 31 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Enchantability | 15 |

## Usage

Its lore says the halberd of Gundyr the Champion can break poise and never crumbles. Its abilities come from its Epic Fight skills, which are described under [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Champions Might.
