**Icewhisper** is a halberd from Simply Swords. It is weapon 44 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

- **Permafrost** (unique effect): while it is wielded, a frost aura damages and slows enemies within 4 blocks.
- **Hold right-click:** the wielder spends hunger to summon a blizzard that slows and damages enemies within 8 blocks, for up to 10 s. Cooldown 30 s.

The ability scales with Frost spell power.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Geschoss.
