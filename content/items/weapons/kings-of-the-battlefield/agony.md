**Agony** is a spear from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 37 of 52 in [[items/weapons/kings-of-the-battlefield|Kings of the Battlefield]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Enchantability | 22 |

## Usage

Its tooltip describes a spear that moves its wielder around "regardless of gravity's rules". Its innate skill, **Sky Dive**, vanishes into the sky and dives onto enemies; attacks charge it up to three times, and the final damage is multiplied by the number of charges. See [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Sky dive.
