## Usage

Its tooltip tracks the kills and total damage dealt with it. At 100 kills, Soulrend's config grants the **Judgement Cut End** skill book. Its innate skill describes a full style system (Basic, Angel and Devil styles, summoned swords and a doppelganger); see [[#Moveset]].
