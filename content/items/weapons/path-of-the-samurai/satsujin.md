**Satsujin** is a katana from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 35 of 42 in [[items/weapons/path-of-the-samurai|Path of the Samurai]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

## Usage

Its tooltip says a demon is sealed inside the blade, strengthening it. **Passive:** the blade is sheathed after 4 s without attacking, and sooner at the end of a sheathed attack. Its skills are described under [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an uchigatana. Its default Two Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Sakura state. It has one other style, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
