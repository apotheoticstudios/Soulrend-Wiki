**Evil Ôdachi** is a katana from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 30 of 42 in [[items/weapons/path-of-the-samurai|Path of the Samurai]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

## Usage

WoM Plus gives it its own weapon type, with the innate skill Evil Punishment and a passive skill. Its abilities come from its Epic Fight skills, which are described under [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an evil tachi. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Evil Punishment. It also has a passive skill.
