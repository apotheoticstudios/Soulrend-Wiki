**Final Fractal** is a katana from cataclysm. It is weapon 42 of 42 in [[items/weapons/path-of-the-samurai|Path of the Samurai]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Durability | 6,666 |
| Enchantability | 12 |

## Usage

Each hit deals extra damage equal to 3% of the target's maximum health. Its tooltip marks it as a work in progress (W.I.P.) in Cataclysm.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an uchigatana. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Sky dive. It has four other styles, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
