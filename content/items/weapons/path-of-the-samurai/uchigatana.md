**Uchigatana** is a katana from Epic Fight. It is weapon 15 of 42 in [[items/weapons/path-of-the-samurai|Path of the Samurai]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

After 5 s without attacking, the Uchigatana is sheathed, and the next attack is empowered. Its skills are described under [[#Moveset]].
