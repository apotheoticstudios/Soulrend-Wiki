**Whisperwind** is a katana from Simply Swords. It is weapon 34 of 37 in [[items/weapons/path-of-the-samurai|Path of the Samurai]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

Soulrend's Simply Swords Overhaul mod reworks this weapon.

- **Fatal Flicker** (unique effect): killing an enemy resets the dash's cooldown, including kills made by the dash.
- **On right-click:** the wielder dashes up to 22 blocks forward, dealing critical damage to enemies in the way. Using it again during the dash cancels it. Cooldown 20 s.

## Moveset
