**Jorogumo Katana** is a katana from Samurai Dynasty. It is weapon 28 of 42 in [[items/weapons/path-of-the-samurai|Path of the Samurai]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

Its Shift summary lists:

- **On hit:** it gives the target Wither and Slowness.
- **Ability (right-click):** it gives the wielder Speed.
