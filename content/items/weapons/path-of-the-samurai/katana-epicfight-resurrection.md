**Katana (EpicFight-Resurrection)** is a katana from EpicFight-Resurrection. It is weapon 22 of 42 in [[items/weapons/path-of-the-samurai|Path of the Samurai]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Enchantability | 22 |

## Usage

After 5 s without attacking, the katana is sheathed, and the next attack is enhanced; its skills are enhanced too. Its innate skill, **Stance Flow**, builds stance stacks with each attack and spends them in a sheathed or unsheathed finisher. See [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a katana. Its default Two Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Critical Strike. It has one other style, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
