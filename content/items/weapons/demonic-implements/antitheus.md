**Antitheus** is a scythe from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 26 of 27 in [[items/weapons/demonic-implements|Demonic Implements]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Attack Speed | 1.90 |
| Impact | 2.6 |
| Targets per swing | 7 |
| Armor Negation | 20% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Durability | 6,666 |
| Enchantability | 22 |

## Usage

Its attacks have a 20% chance to give the target Wither for 6 s. If the target already has Wither, it gets a stronger Wither with the duration refreshed. Each level of Sweeping Edge adds 10% to the chance and 2 s to the duration. Its other abilities are its Epic Fight skills (see [[#Moveset]]).

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an antitheu. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Demonic Ascension. It has one other style, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
