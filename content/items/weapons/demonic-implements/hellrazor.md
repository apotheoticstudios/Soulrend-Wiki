**Hellrazor** is a scythe from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is weapon 18 of 27 in [[items/weapons/demonic-implements|Demonic Implements]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Attack Speed | 1.40 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Durability | 2,031 |
| Enchantability | 16 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It comes imbued with the spell **Raise Hell** (level 3, 0.8 s cast, 12.5 s cooldown): the caster slams the weapon down, sending a fiery shockwave through a large radius. The damage scales with the held weapon. Its tooltip shows one imbue slot, so the spell can be replaced.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a tachi. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Decapitate / Hymn of the Night. It has one other style, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
