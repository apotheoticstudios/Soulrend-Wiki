**CRIMSON_MOON** is a scythe from EpicFight Nightfall. It is weapon 27 of 27 in [[items/weapons/demonic-implements|Demonic Implements]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

Its innate skill, **Crimson Harvest**, builds the whole weapon around the Curse of Blood: basic attacks apply it, and dash, jump and skill attacks consume it for buffs and energy. The full skill text is under [[#Moveset]]. The enchantment **Crimson Moon - Demonized** makes every strike echo with a blood cut while the skill's energy is full.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Decapitate / Hymn of the Night. It has one other style, shown below.
