**Scythe of the Forlorn** is a scythe from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 20 of 27 in [[items/weapons/demonic-implements|Demonic Implements]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

One loot table drops it:

| Source | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[bosses/act-ii/night-prowler\|Night Prowler]] (drop) | 100% | 1 |

It can also be crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Corrupted Release** (tooltip under Left Alt): using it fires a Wither Skull powered by the souls the scythe has harvested. Every third shot is charged, and using it again detonates the previous skull early.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
