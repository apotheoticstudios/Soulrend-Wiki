## Usage

**Inner Conflict** (tooltip under Left Alt): killing mobs harvests their souls, which are sorted into blue and red. A mob counts as blue or red depending on whether it was passive, held a ranged weapon, or was a hostile monster. Each soul raises the weapon's damage. At the maximum number of souls, the scythe transforms based on the dominant color, keeping its enchantments. The tooltip shows the souls harvested so far.
