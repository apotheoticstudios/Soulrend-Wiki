**Epic Knights Weapons** covers Every Epic Knights weapon and shield on one page (45 items in `soulrend:all_weapons`), plus the Epic Knights: Ice and Fire add-on 15 weapon types in 7 dragon materials.

[[item:magistuarmory:diamond_shortsword]] and [[item:magistuarmory:netherite_shortsword]] have their own entry on another page.

## Usage

> [!NOTE]
> Soulrend removes every estoc, stylet, bastard sword, ranseur and ahlspiess with Item Obliterator, so those weapons in the tables below don't exist in game.

Most Epic Knights weapons have no abilities beyond their stats. The exceptions, from their tooltips:

- **Two-Handed I and II:** a two-handed weapon used single-handed (with something in the off hand) loses attack speed, damage or both. The Noble Sword, guisarmes, lochaber axes and lucern hammers are Two-Handed I; claymores, concave edged halberds, flame-bladed swords, pikes and zweihanders are Two-Handed II.
- **Concave edged halberds** have a chance to push the enemy off its horse.
- **Flame-bladed swords** reduce the enemy's maximum health.
- **Lances** work only while riding. Their damage depends on mass and speed, and left-clicking just before a hit dismounts the target.

The Epic Knights: Ice and Fire add-on makes each weapon kind in dragon and myrmex materials, with effects by material:

| Material | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Dragon Bone Fire, Ice and Lightning | Legendary Weapon, no extra effect listed |
| Dragon Steel Fire | Sets targets on fire and knocks them back |
| Dragon Steel Ice | Freezes targets |
| Dragon Steel Lightning | Strikes targets with lightning |
| Myrmex Jungle and Myrmex Jungle Venom | Poisons for 10 s |
| Dragon Bone, Myrmex Desert and Myrmex Desert Venom | None listed |

## Movesets

Most weapon kinds share one Epic Fight weapon type. Claymores and concave edged halberds are split by material: Soulrend's capability datapack gives the diamond, iron, steel and dragon claymores the Dual Tachi type and the other claymores the greatsword type, while the myrmex claymores keep the add-on's own longsword type. Each moveset is shown once, for one weapon that uses it.

### Axe (`epicfight:axe`)

Used by all chained morning stars; all heavy maces; all heavy warhammers; all lucern hammers; all morning stars.

{{moveset epic_knights_ice_and_fire:dragon_chainmorgenstern}}

### Spear (`epicfight:spear`)

Used by all lances; concave edged halberds in Dragon Bone, Dragon Bone Fire, Dragon Bone Ice, Dragon Bone Lightning, Dragon Steel Fire, Dragon Steel Ice, Dragon Steel Lightning, Myrmex Desert, Myrmex Desert Venom, Myrmex Jungle, Myrmex Jungle Venom; all guisarmes; all lochaber axes; all pikes.
