**Dark Moon Greatsword (Souls Like Bosses)** is a greatsword from Souls Like Bosses. It is weapon 9 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 12 |
| Attack Speed | 1.20 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Durability | 2,031 |
| Enchantability | 15 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Right-click:** it summons an Elemental Light Wave. It holds 3 charges; once all are spent, they recharge after 120 s. Its tooltip also carries four lines of lore.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
