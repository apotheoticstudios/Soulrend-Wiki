**Genesis Fracture** is a greatsword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 53 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Attack Speed | 1.60 |
| Impact | 1.7 |
| Targets per swing | 4 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 10 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It has three abilities, listed in the tooltip under Left Alt:

- **Blazing Blade:** it sets mobs on fire for longer, more so with higher Fire Aspect.
- **Chaos Storm:** charge the sword to raise Flame Pillars at random around the wielder (15 damage in Soulrend, 9 s cooldown).
- **Veil of Fire:** the ability key unleashes Meridia's Retribution and gives the wielder the Veil of Fire effect, which keeps dealing fire damage to nearby mobs.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Ender Ritual.
