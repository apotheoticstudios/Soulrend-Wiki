**Solar** is a greatsword from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 49 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

Its tooltip reads "The power of the sun, in the palm of my hand". Its innate skill, Solar Arcano, drives the whole weapon: timed skill presses after basic attacks ignite and explode targets, and a heat meter powers its incendiary spells. The full skill text is under [[#Moveset]].
