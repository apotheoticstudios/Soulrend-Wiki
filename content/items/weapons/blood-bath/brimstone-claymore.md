**Brimstone Claymore** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 43 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Brimstone** (unique effect): each hit has a 15% chance to spew explosive brimstone, which damages nearby enemies and sets them on fire.
