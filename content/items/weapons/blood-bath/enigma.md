**Enigma** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 36 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Galeforce** (unique effect): while the wielder is near their twister, they get Haste.
- **On right-click:** it summons a twister that chases nearby enemies (within 16 blocks) and deals more damage the longer an enemy stays trapped inside. Cooldown 40 s.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Geschoss.
