**Lightning Dragonsteel Greatsword** is a greatsword from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 31 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Enchantability | 12 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Hit effect (Lightning Strike):** a hit gives the target the Conductive effect for 2.25 s, unless it already has it. When Conductive ends, a lightning bolt strikes where the target stands.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
