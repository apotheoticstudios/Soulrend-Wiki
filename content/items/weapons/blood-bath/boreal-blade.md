**Boreal Blade** is a greatsword from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is weapon 37 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It comes imbued with the spell **Frostbite** (level 3, 17.3 s effect). While Frostbite lasts, any fully frozen creature the caster kills turns into an Ice Shadow, which shatters into icicles after 5 s or when it takes damage. Its tooltip shows one imbue slot, so the spell can be replaced.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Ender Ritual.
