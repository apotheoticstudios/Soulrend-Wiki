**The Watcher (Weapon)** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 57 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Attack Speed | 1.14 |
| Impact | 3.1 |
| Targets per swing | 6 |
| Armor Negation | 12% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

Soulrend's Simply Swords Overhaul mod reworks this weapon.

- **Omen** (unique effect): killing an enemy heals the wielder for 10% of the victim's maximum health.
- **Watcher** (unique effect): each hit heals the wielder for 0.5 health.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a tachi. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Mortal Slash.
