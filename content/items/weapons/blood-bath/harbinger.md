**Harbinger** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 54 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Abyssal Standard** (unique effect): each hit has a 15% chance to give the target Weakness.
- **On right-click:** it summons an Abyssal Battle Standard that pulls in, damages and slows enemies, and gives friendly players near it Haste now and then. Cooldown 35 s.

The ability scales with Soul spell power. It is crafted from the [[items/weapons/blood-bath/tainted-relic|Tainted Relic]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a tachi. Its default Two Handed style has a seven-hit basic combo and the innate skill Ender Ritual. It has one other style, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
