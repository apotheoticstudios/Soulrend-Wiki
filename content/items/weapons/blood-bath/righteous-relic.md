**Righteous Relic** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 47 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Righteous Standard** (unique effect): each hit has a 15% chance to regenerate the wielder's health. Its tooltip says a growing power emanates from within: crafted with a Runic Tablet, it becomes [[items/weapons/blood-bath/sunfire|Sunfire]]. It is made from the [[items/weapons/blood-bath/dormant-relic|Dormant Relic]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Liechtenauer. It has one other style, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
