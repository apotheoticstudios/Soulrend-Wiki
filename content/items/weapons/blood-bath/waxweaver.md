**Waxweaver** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 60 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Waxweave** (unique effect): attacking burning enemies gives the wielder Strength and Haste. When the wielder would die, regenerative wax heals them to full health and gives them Resistance for a short time instead. That can happen once every 60 s, and only while it is in the main hand.
