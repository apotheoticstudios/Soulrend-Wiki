**Warriors-BroadBlade** is a greatsword from EpicFight Nightfall. It is weapon 61 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 15 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

Its tooltip counts the kills made with it. Several parts of its skill depend on that count, such as Heroic Soul, which unlocks after 100 kills. Its abilities come from its Epic Fight skills, which are described under [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed：Warriors-BroadBlade style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Breaking Edge.
