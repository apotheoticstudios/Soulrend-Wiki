**Dormant Relic** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 40 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 16 |
| Attack Speed | 1.71 |
| Impact | 1.8 |
| Targets per swing | 3 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

Its tooltip only says "A faint power seems to emanate from within". It is the start of Simply Swords' relic line: with a Runic Tablet it is crafted into the [[items/weapons/blood-bath/tainted-relic|Tainted Relic]] or the [[items/weapons/blood-bath/righteous-relic|Righteous Relic]], which lead on to [[items/weapons/blood-bath/harbinger|Harbinger]] and [[items/weapons/blood-bath/sunfire|Sunfire]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Liechtenauer. It has one other style, shown below.
