**Night's Edge** is a greatsword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 44 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 16 |
| Attack Speed | 1.70 |
| Impact | 1.7 |
| Targets per swing | 4 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 10 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

Its abilities, listed in the tooltip under Left Alt:

- **Edge of Night:** using it summons a line of Edges of Night; it can also summon circles of them around the wielder.
- **Blight:** its hits apply Blight, which makes the target take more damage, a percentage based on its armor.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Ender Ritual.
