**Slumbering Lichblade** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 51 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

It is the first stage of the Lichblade (Slumbering, Waking, Awakened).

**Soul Anguish I** (unique effect): tortured souls surround the wielder and damage nearby enemies. It has no right-click ability yet; the [[items/weapons/blood-bath/waking-lichblade|Waking Lichblade]] adds one.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Accusatio.
