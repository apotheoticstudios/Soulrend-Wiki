**Iced Dragon Bone Greatsword** is a greatsword from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 20 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Enchantability | 12 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Hit effect:** each hit gives the target Frostbite for 9 s and Mining Fatigue for 6 s. The tooltip describes Frostbite as continuous freezing damage.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
