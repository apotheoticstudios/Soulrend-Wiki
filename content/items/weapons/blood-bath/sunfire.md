**Sunfire** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 55 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

- **Righteous Standard** (unique effect): each hit has a 15% chance to regenerate the wielder's health.
- **On right-click:** it summons a Righteous Battle Standard that ignites, damages and slows enemies, and gives friendly players near it Strength and health now and then. Cooldown 35 s.

The ability scales with Fire and Healing spell power. It is crafted from the [[items/weapons/blood-bath/righteous-relic|Righteous Relic]].
