**Awakened Lichblade** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 59 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

It is the last stage of the Lichblade (Slumbering, Waking, Awakened).

- **Soul Anguish III** (unique effect): tortured souls surround the wielder and damage nearby enemies.
- **Hold right-click:** the souls attack a distant target (up to 22 blocks away) with rapid hits that have a chance to heal the wielder. When the attack ends, the souls return and give the wielder absorption. In Soulrend the attack lasts 10 s, and its cooldown is 35 s.

The ability scales with Soul spell power.
