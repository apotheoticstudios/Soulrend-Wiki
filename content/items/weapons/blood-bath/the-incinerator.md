**The Incinerator** is a greatsword from cataclysm. It is weapon 58 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 19 |
| Attack Speed | 1.00 |
| Impact | 3 |
| Targets per swing | 5 |
| Armor Negation | 20% |
| Entity Reach | 5 |
| Enchantability | 16 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

P1nero's Epic Cataclysm turns it into an Epic Fight weapon with a skill-point system, explained in its tooltip:

- A perfect dodge or a perfect parry earns Skill Points.
- **2:** charge up and fire five fireballs forward, for 2 Skill Points.
- **3:** release the Flame Soul Slash forward, for 3 Skill Points.

Cataclysm's own text for the weapon (hold right-click for 3 s to summon a flame strike) is not shown in its tooltip in Soulrend.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and an innate skill of its own.
