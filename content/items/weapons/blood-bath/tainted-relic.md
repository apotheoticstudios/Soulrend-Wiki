**Tainted Relic** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 46 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Impact | 2.5 |

| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Abyssal Standard** (unique effect): each hit has a 15% chance to give the target Weakness. Its tooltip says a growing power emanates from within: crafted with a Runic Tablet, it becomes [[items/weapons/blood-bath/harbinger|Harbinger]]. It is made from the [[items/weapons/blood-bath/dormant-relic|Dormant Relic]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a tachi. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Rushing Tempo.
