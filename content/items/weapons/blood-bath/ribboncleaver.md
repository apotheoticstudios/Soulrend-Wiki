**Ribboncleaver** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 38 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Ribbonwrath** (unique effect): while it is wielded, the wielder moves 5% slower but takes 15% less damage.
- **On right-click:** the wielder dashes forward, shrugs off the next few hits, gains knockback resistance, and their next attack deals +95% damage to a single target. Cooldown 2 s.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Accusatio.
