**Molten Edge** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 50 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |

| Impact | 3.3 |
| Targets per swing | 6 |
| Armor Negation | 5.5% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

Soulrend's Simply Swords Overhaul mod reworks this weapon.

- **Molten Roar** (unique effect): hits set the enemy on fire. When the wielder's health is low, they get Strength, Haste and Speed, growing stronger for every 20% of missing health. Wielding two Molten Edges raises each effect by one level.
- **On right-click:** the wielder loses 4 health and gets Regeneration for 10 s.
