**Dawnbreaker** is a greatsword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 45 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

One loot table drops it:

| Source | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[bosses/act-ii/day-stalker\|Day Stalker]] (drop) | 100% | 1 |

It can also be crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

It has two abilities, listed in the tooltip under Left Alt:

- **Blazing Blade:** it sets mobs on fire for longer, more so with higher Fire Aspect.
- **Meridia's Retribution:** killing an undead mob may set off an explosion (10 damage in Soulrend) that damages, frightens and ignites nearby undead.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Ender Ritual.
