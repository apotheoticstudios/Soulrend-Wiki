**Twisted Blade** is a greatsword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 42 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

- **Ferocity** (unique effect): each hit has a 75% chance to raise the wielder's attack speed, stacking up to 15 times.
- **On right-click:** all stacks are used up for Strength II, lasting longer the more stacks were used.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a tachi. Its default Two Handed style has a nine-hit basic combo and the innate skill Rushing Tempo.
