**Heap of Raw Iron** is a greatsword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 24 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 10 |
| Attack Speed | 1.10 |
| Impact | 3 |
| Targets per swing | 10 |
| Armor Negation | 20% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 16 |

## Usage

It has two abilities, listed in the tooltip under Left Alt:

- **Rage:** the wielder gets the Bloodthirsty and Strength effects.
- **Dragon's Scourge:** it deals more damage to dragons.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
