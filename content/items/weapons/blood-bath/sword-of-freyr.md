**Sword of Freyr** is a greatsword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 26 of 61 in [[items/weapons/blood-bath|Blood Bath]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Bound Armament** (tooltip under Left Alt): using it summons the sword to fight by the wielder's side. A key ("Return Freyr Sword to owner") or right-clicking the sword picks it back up, and another key switches it between staying in place and following. Only one summoned sword can exist at a time, and quitting the game leaves it where it stood until it is picked up.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a tachi. Its default Two Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Rushing Tempo.
