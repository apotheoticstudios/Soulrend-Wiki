**Tidal Claws** is a fist weapon from cataclysm. It is weapon 26 of 27 in [[items/weapons/bulwark-and-brawl|Bulwark and Brawl]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

P1nero's Epic Cataclysm turns it into an Epic Fight weapon with a skill-point system, explained in its tooltip:

- A perfect dodge or a perfect parry earns Skill Points.
- **2:** charge forward and launch an Abyss Blast, for 2 Skill Points.
- **3:** open an Abyss Blast Portal at the feet of every entity within 10 blocks, for 3 Skill Points.

Cataclysm's own text for the claws (right-click fires a grappling hook, left-click shoots a damaging tentacle) is not shown in its tooltip in Soulrend.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a fist. Its default Two Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and an innate skill of its own.
