**Dragonsteel Fire Shield** is a shield from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 7 of 27 in [[items/weapons/bulwark-and-brawl|Bulwark and Brawl]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Durability | 10,000 |
| Enchantability | 1 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It is a shield: it blocks attacks and has no other abilities of its own. Like every shield in Soulrend, it can also parry (tooltip under Left Alt): with it in the off hand, Soulslike Weaponry's "Parry with shield" key attempts a parry that Posture Breaks an attacker. The parry has a 2 s cooldown.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a shield with no attack combo of its own.
