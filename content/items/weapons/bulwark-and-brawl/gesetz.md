**Gesetz** is a shield from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 5 of 27 in [[items/weapons/bulwark-and-brawl|Bulwark and Brawl]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

Its tooltip calls it a strong shield that blocks even the strongest attacks but "seems to need something in order to release its full potential". That something is its partner sword, [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion/herrscher|Herrscher]]: with Herrscher in the main hand, Gesetz uses Herrscher's guard poses, and Herrscher's innate skill **Regierung** adds shield attacks. On its own it has no attacks.
