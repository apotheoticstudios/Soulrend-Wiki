**Frozen Fist** is a fist weapon from Bosses'Rises. It is weapon 2 of 27 in [[items/weapons/bulwark-and-brawl|Bulwark and Brawl]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

| --- | --- | --- |
| `block_factorys_bosses:bosses/yeti` (mob drop) | 100% | 1 |

It can also be crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Knockback | +2.5 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Enchantability | 1 |

## Usage

- **On hit:** it freezes the target.
- **Sneak and right-click:** it freezes nearby entities.
- It can also be used as a shield.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a fist. Its default One Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Relentless Combo.
