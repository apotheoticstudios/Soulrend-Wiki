**Blessed Gold Fist** is a fist weapon from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 22 of 27 in [[items/weapons/bulwark-and-brawl|Bulwark and Brawl]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 7 |
| Attack Speed | 3.00 |
| Impact | 1.2 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Durability | 1,821 |
| Enchantability | 30 |

## Usage

**Ability** (sneak and right-click, 60 s cooldown): the wielder gets Haste V for 3 s. The tooltip says this works only in daytime.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a fist. Its default One Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Relentless Combo.
