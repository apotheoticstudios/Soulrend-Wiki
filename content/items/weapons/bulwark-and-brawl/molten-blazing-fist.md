**Molten Blazing Fist** is a fist weapon from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 24 of 27 in [[items/weapons/bulwark-and-brawl|Bulwark and Brawl]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 7 |
| Attack Speed | 3.00 |
| Impact | 1.2 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Durability | 2,421 |
| Enchantability | 12 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

- **Hit effect:** each hit sets the target on fire for 8 s.
- **Flame Grant Me Strength** (sneak and right-click, 31 s cooldown): the wielder gets the Flame Grant Me Strength effect for 40 s, which the tooltip describes as Resistance I and Strength II.
