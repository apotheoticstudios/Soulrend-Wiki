**Crimson Blades** is a sickle from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 47 of 50 in [[items/weapons/assassins-armoury|Assassin's Armoury]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Targets per swing | 3 |
| Armor Negation | 30% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Durability | 3,021 |
| Enchantability | 8 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

- **Hit effect:** each hit makes the target bleed for 16 s. Bleeding deals 1 damage every 1.5 s and removes Regeneration and Health Boost.
- **Blood Oath** (sneak and right-click, 11 s cooldown): the wielder starts Bleeding for 40 s.
