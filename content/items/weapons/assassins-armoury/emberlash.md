## Usage

Soulrend's Simply Swords Overhaul mod reworks this weapon.

- **Smoulder** (unique effect): attacks apply stacks of Smouldering, and attacking a Smouldering enemy deals extra damage per stack.
- **On right-click:** the wielder dashes 12 blocks in the direction they are looking and heals for 10% of their maximum health. Cooldown 4 s. Holding Shift dashes in the opposite direction. Wielding two Emberlash gives the dash a second charge, but only the first charge heals.

The ability scales with Fire spell power.
