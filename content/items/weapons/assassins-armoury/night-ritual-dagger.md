**Night Ritual Dagger** is a dagger from EpicFight - Dawnday. It is weapon 32 of 50 in [[items/weapons/assassins-armoury|Assassin's Armoury]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Weapon passive:** executing a target restores stamina, and the effect is stronger in moonlight. Its other abilities are its Epic Fight skills (see [[#Moveset]]).
