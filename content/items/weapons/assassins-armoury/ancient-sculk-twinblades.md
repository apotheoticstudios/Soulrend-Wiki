**Ancient Sculk Twinblades** is a shortsword from EpicFight Nightfall. It is weapon 37 of 50 in [[items/weapons/assassins-armoury|Assassin's Armoury]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

Its abilities come from its Epic Fight skills, which are described under [[#Moveset]].

The command `/efn secludedAttack` turns its effects on other entities on or off, as its tooltip notes.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a sword. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Active Skill-Execution. It also has a passive skill.
