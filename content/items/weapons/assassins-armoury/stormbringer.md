## Usage

**Shock Deflect** (hold right-click): the wielder focuses energy into the blade and blocks incoming attacks for a moment. Timing it against an enemy's melee swing performs a parry, which damages and launches the attacker and shortens the cooldown. Parrying several times in a row raises the ability's damage and its cooldown.

The ability scales with Lightning spell power.
