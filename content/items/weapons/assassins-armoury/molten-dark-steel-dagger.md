**Molten Dark Steel Dagger** is a dagger from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 29 of 50 in [[items/weapons/assassins-armoury|Assassin's Armoury]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 14 |
| Attack Speed | 2.40 |
| Impact | 1.3 |
| Targets per swing | 2 |
| Armor Negation | 25% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 6 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Main-hand effect, Flame Absorption:** killing a target while it is on fire heals the wielder a little. The item itself has no hit or use procedure; the effect is handled elsewhere in the mod.
