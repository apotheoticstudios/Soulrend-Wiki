**Moonlight Shortsword** is a shortsword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 39 of 50 in [[items/weapons/assassins-armoury|Assassin's Armoury]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Moonlight** (tooltip under Left Alt): attacking also fires a small Moonlight Beam. In Soulrend the beam deals 3 damage with a 0.65 s cooldown. The **Lunar Herald** effect raises the beam's damage and shortens its cooldown.
