## Usage

- **Soul Steal** (unique effect): hits have a chance to take the target's soul. The wielder gets Haste, and the target is slowed and marked for death.
- **On right-click:** within 5 blocks of a soulless target, the wielder returns its soul, leaps forward and turns invisible. Farther than 5 blocks away, the wielder blinks to the target and returns its soul with force, which blinds and damages it.

The ability scales with Soul spell power.
