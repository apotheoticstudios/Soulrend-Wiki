**EXSILIUMGLADIUS-L** is a shortsword from EpicFight Nightfall. It is weapon 49 of 50 in [[items/weapons/assassins-armoury|Assassin's Armoury]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

It is the left blade of a pair with [[items/weapons/assassins-armoury/exsiliumgladius-r|EXSILIUMGLADIUS-R]]; holding both unlocks their true form, as the tooltip says. Its abilities come from its Epic Fight skills, which are described under [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a sword. Its default One Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Sweeping Edge. It has one other style, shown below.
