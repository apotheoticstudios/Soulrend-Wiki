**Flamed Dragon Bone Dagger** is a dagger from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 40 of 50 in [[items/weapons/assassins-armoury|Assassin's Armoury]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Hit effect:** each hit sets the target on fire for 8 s.
