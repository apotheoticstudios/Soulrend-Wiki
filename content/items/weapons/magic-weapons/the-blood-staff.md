**The Blood Staff** is a magic weapon from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is listed on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] under Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.

## Obtaining

One loot table drops it:

| Source | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `irons_spellbooks:entities/dead_king` (mob drop) | 25% (player kill, +5% per Looting level) | 1 |

It can also be crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

It is a spellcasting staff: holding it in the main hand adds the spell attributes listed in its tooltip, such as spell power and cooldown reduction. It has no ability of its own.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
