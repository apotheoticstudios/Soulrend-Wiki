## Usage

**Primeval Devour:** each hit has a 45% chance to summon a Tremorsaurus spirit that follows up with a crushing bite. It comes imbued with the spell **Extinction** (level 1).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** 60% chance.
- **Evo 2:** the bite can trigger even when the gauntlet isn't equipped, once every 2.5 s.
- **Evo 3:** the spirit's damage scales with almost 50% of the wielder's attack damage.
