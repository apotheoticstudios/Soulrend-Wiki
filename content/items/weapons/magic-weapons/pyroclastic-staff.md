**Pyroclastic Staff** is a magic weapon from dacxirons. It is listed on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] under Dacxirons.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 4 |
| Attack Speed | 1.6 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Fire Spell Power | +15% |
| Enchantability | 20 |

## Usage

**Ability:** the wielder's spells set enemies on fire, and Fire school spells give the wielder Strength. It comes imbued with the spell **Triple Fireball** (level 1, 25 s cooldown).

## Moveset

Epic Fight gives it no weapon capability, so it has no moveset of its own.
