## Usage

**Sands of Annihilation** (hold right-click, then release): it drops an Ancient Desert Stele onto every enemy within 12 blocks, up to 6 targets. It comes imbued with the spell **Stele Cascade** (level 6).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** +6 blocks radius and +4 targets.
- **Evo 2:** it also summons a Sandstorm (two manifestations).
- **Evo 3:** both manifestations scale with Holy spell power.
