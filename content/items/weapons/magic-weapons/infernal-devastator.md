## Usage

**Blazing Salvo:** holding right-click for 2.85 s fires 3 flare bombs in a 50-degree arc. They explode on impact and leave flame jets that burn entities inside. Using the weapon's transformation overloads it and cancels other transformations. It comes imbued with the spell **Gyro Slash** (level 1).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** 5 flare bombs in a 60-degree arc.
- **Evo 2:** Gyro Slash fires a piercing slash that applies Flare Vacuum, and flame jets deal damage.
- **Evo 3:** flame jets scale with Fire spell power.
