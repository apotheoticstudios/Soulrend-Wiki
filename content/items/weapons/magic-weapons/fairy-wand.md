**Fairy Wand** is a magic weapon from dacxirons. It is listed on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] under Dacxirons.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Ability:** the wielder's spells make enemies levitate. It comes imbued with the spell **Fairy's Wish** (level 3, 30 s cooldown).

Epic Fight gives it no weapon capability, so it has no moveset of its own.
