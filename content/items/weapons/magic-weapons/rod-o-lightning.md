**Rod o' Lightning** is a magic weapon from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is listed on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] under Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |

| Attack Speed | 1 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Cooldown Reduction | +15% |
| Lightning Spell Power | +15% |
| Spell Power | +5% |
| Enchantability | 20 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It is a spellcasting staff: holding it in the main hand adds the spell attributes listed in its tooltip, such as spell power and cooldown reduction. It has no ability of its own.

## Moveset

Epic Fight gives it no weapon capability, so it has no moveset of its own.
