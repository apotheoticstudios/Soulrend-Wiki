## Usage

**Oblivion Ray:** normal attacks have a 45% chance to give the target Abyssal Curse II (damage over time for 5 s), and the weapon deals more critical damage to cursed targets. Holding right-click for 2 s while wielding it in both hands opens an Abyss Blast Portal ahead, which fires a searing laser. It comes imbued with the spell **Shadowed Miasma** (level 3).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** Oblivion Ray works with one weapon equipped.
- **Evo 2:** it opens 4 portals around the wielder instead of one ahead.
- **Evo 3:** portal damage scales with Ender spell power.
