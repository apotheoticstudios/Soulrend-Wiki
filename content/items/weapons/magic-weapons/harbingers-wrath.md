## Usage

**Mini EM-Pulse** (passive): hits have a 15% chance to stun the target with a small EM-Pulse for 2 s; further procs refresh the duration. It comes imbued with the spell **EM-Pulse** (level 5).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** +1.5 s duration.
- **Evo 2:** +10% proc chance.
- **Evo 3:** the pulse hits an area, but its chance drops by 5% and its duration by 0.5 s.
