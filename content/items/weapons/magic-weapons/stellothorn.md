## Usage

**Spectral Guillotine** (hold right-click): the wielder throws the scythe forward. It damages every enemy it passes and returns after 0.75 s. When it strikes its first enemy, a clone appears 8 blocks above that enemy and drops down, dealing extra damage in a 3.5-block radius and applying Abyssal Fear. It comes imbued with the spell **Shear of the Stars** (level 1).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** just before returning, the scythe explodes in a 6-block radius for 50% of its base damage.
- **Evo 2:** if it hits anything, the wielder heals 15% of their maximum health.
- **Evo 3:** the clone's damage scales with Eldritch spell power.
