## Usage

**Seabreaker's Curse:** thrown at an enemy, it deals extra direct damage. Where it lands it creates a Maelstrom that pulls in nearby enemies, strikes them with Boltstrike and gives them Tidal Torment (20% Slowness). Attacking an enemy with Tidal Torment makes all nearby afflicted enemies take 50% of the damage. It comes imbued with the spell **Skypiercer** (level 1).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** +25% Tidal Torment area damage.
- **Evo 2:** each entity hit by Skypiercer raises the wielder's mana regeneration by 10% for 2.5 s, stacking up to 8 times.
- **Evo 3:** Boltstrike scales with Aqua spell power.
