## Usage

**Rune Surge:** right-clicking summons Void Runes in rings around the wielder, 8 waves with 15 ticks (0.75 s) between them, damaging everything they touch. It comes imbued with the spell **Vortex Uppercut** (level 3).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** 10 ticks less between waves.
- **Evo 2:** bonus damage scaling with Ender spell power.
- **Evo 3:** twice the bonus scaling and 2 more runes per wave.
