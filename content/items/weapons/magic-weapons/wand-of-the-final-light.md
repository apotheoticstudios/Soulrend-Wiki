## Usage

It comes imbued with the special spell **Supernova** (level 1, 5 s cast, 1.5-minute cooldown): the caster summons a collapsing star that grows in intensity, then erupts in a huge explosion that leaves a black hole. The black hole pulls in nearby entities and keeps damaging them.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an enhanced spear. Its default Two Handed style has a two-hit basic combo and the innate skill Spear Dance.
