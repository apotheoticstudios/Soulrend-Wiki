**Hither-Thither Wand** is a magic weapon from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is listed on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] under Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.

## Obtaining

One loot table drops it:

| Source | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `alshanex_familiars:chests/end_laboratory/treasure` (chest) | 30% | 1 |

It can also be crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Ender Spell Power | +15% |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Enchantability | 1 |

## Usage

It is a unique wand imbued with the spell **Portal** (level 1): portals last 1.75 minutes, with a 1.5-minute cooldown.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a staff. Its default One Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Charybdis into Scylla. It has one other style, shown below.
