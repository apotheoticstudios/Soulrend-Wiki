**The Stonecaller** is a magic weapon from GTBC's Geomancy Plus. It is listed on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] under GTBC's Geomancy Plus.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 6 |
| Attack Speed | 1.00 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Cooldown Reduction | +10% |
| Geo Spell Power | +30% |
| Mana Regeneration | +15% |
| Enchantability | 20 |

## Usage

It is a spellcasting staff: holding it in the main hand adds the spell attributes listed in its tooltip, such as spell power and cooldown reduction. It has no ability of its own.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a staff. Its default One Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Charybdis into Scylla. It has one other style, shown below.
