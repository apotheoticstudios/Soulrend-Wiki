## Usage

**Pulse of Divergence:** each hit has a chance to give the target a Scarlet Charge or an Azure Charge, or add a stack to its charge. Hitting charged enemies builds Galena Power of the matching color in the weapon. Right-clicking a block releases that power as charges on nearby enemies, up to 10 stacks per use. The imbued spell **Galena Shatter** bursts the charges.

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** up to 15 stacks per right-click.
- **Evo 2:** hits have a 50% chance to give an extra Galena Power.
- **Evo 3:** casting Galena Shatter while crouching spends 10 Scarlet Power for a 50% larger radius and 10 Azure Power for an attack speed bonus for 15 s.
