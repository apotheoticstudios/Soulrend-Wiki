**Sanguine Scepter** is a magic weapon from dacxirons. It is listed on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] under Dacxirons.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 4 |
| Attack Speed | 1.6 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Blood Spell Power | +45% |

## Usage

**Ability:** the wielder's spells steal health from enemies. It comes imbued with the spell **Bloody Arrow** (level 3, 1.5 s cooldown).

Epic Fight gives it no weapon capability, so it has no moveset of its own.
