## Usage

**Thorn Cascade** (passive): every 7th hit launches 12 Amethyst Cluster projectiles in every direction. It comes imbued with the spell **Blight** (level 8).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** the ability needs 2 fewer hits.
- **Evo 2:** it gains a second, conical pattern next to the circular one.
- **Evo 3:** +50% projectiles in both patterns.
