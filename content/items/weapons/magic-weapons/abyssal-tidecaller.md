## Usage

**Contagious Touch:** normal attacks have a 40% chance to give the target Abyssal Curse, which deals damage over time for 5 s. Further procs speed up the damage ticks, stacking up to 3 times. It comes imbued with the spell **Shadowed Miasma** (level 3).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** +20% proc chance.
- **Evo 2:** +2 stacks.
- **Evo 3:** it also applies Abyssal Fear (two manifestations).
