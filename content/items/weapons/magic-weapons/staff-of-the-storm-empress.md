**Staff Of The Storm Empress** is a magic weapon from T.O Magic 'n Extras. It is listed on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] under T.O Magic 'n Extras.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 10 |
| Attack Speed | 1 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Aqua Spell Power | +30% |
| Mana Regeneration | +20% |
| Enchantability | 20 |

## Usage

**Passive:** while it is held in either hand, the Cryostorm spell gains +2 radius and 15% damage, and Hydrocharge chains to up to 5 targets. Its tooltip shows one empty imbue slot.

## Moveset

Epic Fight gives it no weapon capability, so it has no moveset of its own.
