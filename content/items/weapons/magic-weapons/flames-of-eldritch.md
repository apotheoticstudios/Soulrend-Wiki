## Usage

**Rend and Tear:** normal attacks have a 40% chance to give the target Blazing Brand for 3 s, lowering its armor and armor toughness. Further procs refresh the duration. It comes imbued with the spell **Burning Judgment** (level 6).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** +2 s duration.
- **Evo 2:** +20% proc chance.
- **Evo 3:** Blazing Brand stacks up to 3 times.
