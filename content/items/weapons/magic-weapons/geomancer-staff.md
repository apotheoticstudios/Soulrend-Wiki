**Geomancer Staff** is a magic weapon from Mowzie's Mobs. It is listed on [[items/weapons/magic-weapons|Magic Weapons]] under Mowzie's Mobs.

## Obtaining

3 loot tables drop it:

| Source | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tongbi, the Sculptor (drop) | 20% (player kill) | 1 |
| `gtbcs_geomancy_plus:chests/geomancer_tower/geomancer_treasure_storage` (chest) | 9.38% | 1 |
| `mowziesmobs:chests/monastery_chest` (chest) | 1% per roll, 3–8 rolls | 1 |

It can also be crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Right-click:** it raises three small rocks that fire, after a short delay, in the direction the wielder is looking.

Epic Fight gives it no weapon capability, so it has no moveset of its own.
