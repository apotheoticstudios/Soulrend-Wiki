## Usage

**Arc-Storm:** normal attacks also fire a piercing Lightning Sandstorm in the direction the wielder looks, dealing extra Lightning magic damage. It comes imbued with the spell **Lightning Lance** (level 10).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** attacks fire 3 sandstorms in an arc that home in on the target.
- **Evo 2:** instead of piercing, the sandstorms zap entities within 8 blocks on hit.
- **Evo 3:** sandstorm damage scales with generic and Lightning spell power.
