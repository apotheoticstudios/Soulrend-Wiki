## Usage

- **Soul Collector:** hits reap Soul Fragments in a three-hit cycle (up to 200 stored).
- **Cursed Blast** (hold right-click): it charges a rail-shot whose damage depends on the Soul Fragments stored, and uses them up. Entities within 3 blocks of the path take 50% damage. Every 50 fragments spent creates a Soul Apex (up to 5).
- With Soul Apex stored, Cursed Blast instead spends one Apex for a fixed-damage blast with full damage in a 3-block radius, scaling with generic and Ice spell power.

It comes imbued with the spell **Apex Harvest**, which collects Soul Apex within 20 blocks. Its tooltip shows the stored fragments and Apex.

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** hits reap 50% more Soul Fragments.
- **Evo 2:** a second manifestation, Quick Shot, spends only 20 fragments for a fixed-damage shot that makes no Soul Apex. The manifestations can be switched.
- **Evo 3:** fragment-powered Cursed Blasts scale with generic and Ice spell power.
