## Usage

**Plasma Overdrive** (hold right-click): it fires a continuous laser powered by stored Plasma Core. Each hit generates Plasma Core, which is both the laser's ammunition and a boost to its damage. When the core drops to 5, a Plasma Power Cell from the inventory is used up to restore 50%. It comes imbued with the spell **Rapid Laser** (level 5).

Its tooltip shows three evolution stars. Each evolution is a separate item, made at a Smithing Table from the level before it (see [[#Used in]]), and each one improves the ability:

- **Evo 1:** hits generate 50% more Plasma Core.
- **Evo 2:** less spread and faster shots.
- **Evo 3:** laser damage scales further with Lightning spell power.
