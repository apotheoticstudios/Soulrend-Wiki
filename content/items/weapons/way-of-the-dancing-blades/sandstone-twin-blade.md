**Sandstone Twin Blade** is a sickle from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 18 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 11.5 |
| Attack Speed | 2.75 |
| Impact | 1.8 |
| Targets per swing | 4 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 30 |

## Usage

**Ability** (sneak and right-click, 60 s cooldown): the wielder gets Haste V for 3 s. The tooltip says this works only in daytime.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a staff. Its default One Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Charybdis into Scylla. It has one other style, shown below.
