**Withered Wabbajack** is a battlestaff from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 23 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

One loot table drops it:

| Source | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[bosses/act-ii/monarch-of-chaos\|Monarch of Chaos]] (drop) | 100% | 1 |

It can also be crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |

| Attack Speed | 2.75 |
| Impact | 1.8 |
| Targets per swing | 4 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 10 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It has two abilities, listed in the tooltip under Left Alt:

- **Hell's Kitchen:** using it summons random projectiles with random effects.
- **Lucky Rolls:** the Luck attribute improves the chances of better rolls.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a staff. Its default One Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Charybdis into Scylla. It has one other style, shown below.
