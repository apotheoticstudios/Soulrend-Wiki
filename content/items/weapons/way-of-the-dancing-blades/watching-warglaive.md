**Watching Warglaive** is a warglaive from Simply Swords. It is weapon 30 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

Soulrend's Simply Swords Overhaul mod reworks the Watcher effects it shares with [[items/weapons/blood-bath/the-watcher|The Watcher]].

- **Omen** (unique effect): killing an enemy heals the wielder for 10% of the victim's maximum health.
- **Watcher** (unique effect): each hit heals the wielder for 0.5 health.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Rechargement.
