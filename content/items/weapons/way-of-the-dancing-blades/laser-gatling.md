**Laser Gatling** is a greatsword from cataclysm. It is weapon 31 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Impact | 2.5 |
| Targets per swing | 6 |
| Armor Negation | 5% |
| Durability | 50 |
| Enchantability | 1 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It is a Cataclysm gun that fires lasers. It reloads with Redstone from the inventory.
