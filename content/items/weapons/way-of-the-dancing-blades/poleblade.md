**Poleblade** is a poleblade from EpicFight - Dawnday. It is weapon 17 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Weapon passive:** killing a mob with it restores some of the wielder's health. Its lore says the Blood Cult's elite soldiers carried it in the Unholy War. Its other abilities are its Epic Fight skills (see [[#Moveset]]).

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a poleblade. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Blood Dance.
