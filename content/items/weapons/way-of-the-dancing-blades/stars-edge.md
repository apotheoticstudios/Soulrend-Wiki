**Star's Edge** is a twinblade from Simply Swords. It is weapon 33 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

## Usage

Soulrend's Simply Swords Overhaul mod reworks this weapon.

- **Star's Edge** (unique effect): attacks deal 3 extra magic damage.
- **On right-click:** the wielder saves their position and dashes forward, gaining Speed II and Haste II for 15 s. After 15 s, or when they use it again, they teleport back to the saved position. Holding Shift while using it skips the dash. Cooldown 15 s.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Concitus. It has one other style, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
