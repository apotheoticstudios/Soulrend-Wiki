**Napoleon** is a napoleon from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 19 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

Its tooltip carries only a motto: "L'intarissable moteur du conquérant" ("the conqueror's inexhaustible engine"). Its abilities come from its Epic Fight skills, which are described under [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a napoleon. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Rechargement. It also has a passive skill.
