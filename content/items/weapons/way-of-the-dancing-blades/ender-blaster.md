**Ender blaster** is an enderblaster from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 37 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

| Durability | 3,250 |
| Enchantability | 22 |

## Usage

Its tooltip calls it a weapon that "has transcended time and space". It fights at range and in melee through its innate skill, **Ender Blast**, which loads kinetic energy into twelve chambers and fires it as ender power. See [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an enderblaster. Its default One Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Ender blast. It has one other style, shown below.
