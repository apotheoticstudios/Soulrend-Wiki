**Tempest** is a chakram from Simply Swords. It is weapon 32 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

- **Vortex** (unique effect): attacks attach elemental energy to the target, causing damage over time and element-specific debuffs. The effect stacks (up to 10).
- **On right-click:** the wielder pulls all nearby elements back to themselves, forming a vortex of elemental energy that damages nearby enemies and grows in size.

The ability scales with Frost and Fire spell power.
