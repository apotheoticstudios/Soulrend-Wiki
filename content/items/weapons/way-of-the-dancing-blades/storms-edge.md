**Storm's Edge** is a twinblade from Simply Swords. It is weapon 35 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

Soulrend's Simply Swords Overhaul mod reworks this weapon.

- **Storm Jolt** (unique effect): each hit shortens the dash's cooldown by 1 s, including hits from the dash itself.
- **On right-click:** the wielder dashes 12 blocks in the direction they look, dealing normal attack damage to enemies in the way, and gains Haste and Speed. If they already have Haste, its level rises by one, up to a cap. Cooldown 10 s.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a spear. Its default Two Handed style has a seven-hit basic combo and the innate skill Wrathful Lightning.
