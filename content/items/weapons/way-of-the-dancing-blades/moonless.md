**Moonless** is a tachi from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 36 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

Moonless has two sets of attacks, **Recto** and **Verso**; a dash attack switches between them, as its tooltip explains. Its innate skill, **Lunar Eclipse**, marks enemies and plays the two sets against each other. See [[#Moveset]].
