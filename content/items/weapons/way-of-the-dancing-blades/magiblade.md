**Magiblade** is a twinblade from Simply Swords. It is weapon 34 of 37 in [[items/weapons/way-of-the-dancing-blades|Way of the Dancing Blades]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Magisonic** (unique effect): the Magiblade sometimes senses approaching enemies and repels them (a 55% chance, within 4 blocks).
- **Hold right-click:** after a short delay, it releases a sonic boom ahead that damages enemies in its path, up to 16 blocks away.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Tripudium.
