**Frostfall** is a greathammer from Simply Swords. It is weapon 53 of 56 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

- **Frost Fury** (unique effect): each hit has a 15% chance to encase nearby enemies (within 3 blocks) in ice. Encased enemies take no damage; after 4 s the ice shatters for 18 damage.
- **On right-click:** the wielder encases themselves in ice for 4 s, becoming immune to damage and regenerating. Cooldown 19 s.

The ability scales with Frost spell power.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
