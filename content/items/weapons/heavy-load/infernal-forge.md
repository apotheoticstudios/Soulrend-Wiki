## Usage

Cataclysm makes it a pickaxe as well as a weapon. P1nero's Epic Cataclysm adds a skill-point system, explained in its tooltip:

- A perfect dodge or a perfect parry earns Skill Points.
- **1:** swing the hammer forward repeatedly; the last strike detonates lava energy. 1 Skill Point.
- **2:** launch a bombardment ahead that sets creatures on fire and deals heavy damage. 2 Skill Points.
- **3:** leap forward and smash the ground, erupting lava energy over a wide area. 3 Skill Points.

Cataclysm's own text (right-click a block with it in the main hand for area damage) is not shown in its tooltip in Soulrend.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a sword. Its default Two Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and an innate skill of its own.
