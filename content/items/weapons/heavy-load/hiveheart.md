**Hiveheart** is a greathammer from Simply Swords. It is weapon 51 of 56 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Attack Speed | 1.05 |
| Impact | 3.3 |

| Armor Negation | 20% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Durability | 3,270 |
| Enchantability | 30 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

- **Hivemind** (unique effect): a hit releases an angry bee that stings the target and disappears. This can happen once every 3 s.
- **On right-click:** the bees make regenerative honey instead, healing the wielder over time but putting Hivemind on cooldown.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Rushing Slam.
