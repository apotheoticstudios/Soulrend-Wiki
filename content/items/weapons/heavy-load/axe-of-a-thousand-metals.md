**Axe of a Thousand Metals** is a greataxe from Mowzie's Mobs; its item id is `mowziesmobs:wrought_axe`. It is weapon 12 of 58 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter. The [[bosses/act-ii/ferrous-wroughtnaut|Ferrous Wroughtnaut]] drops it, and the tooltip promises that it never breaks.

## Obtaining

The Ferrous Wroughtnaut always drops one when a player kills it, together with its [[item:mowziesmobs:wrought_helmet]] (Wrought Helm). It can also be made at a [[item:minecraft:smithing_table]] with Soulrend's upgrade recipe: the base is a [[item:wom:iron_greataxe]], the template an [[item:minecraft:iron_block]] and the addition a Wrought Helm. The upgrade keeps the base's enchantments, gems and affixes (see [[progression/prologue/jei-menu#Upgrading Gear|Upgrading Gear]]).

## Usage

Its tooltip lists three abilities: "Never breaks", "Right-click to attack in an large arc" and "Shift-right-click to slam the ground and create a shockwave". Soulrend gives it the same Epic Fight moveset as the Runic Greataxe, so in Epic Fight it also has the innate skill shown under [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Accusatio.
