**Soul Pyre** is a greataxe from Simply Swords. It is weapon 47 of 56 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

Soulrend's Simply Swords Overhaul mod reworks this weapon.

- **Soul Tether** (unique effect): each hit gives the target Wither II for 10 s.
- **On right-click:** the wielder pulls a selected target to themselves from up to 32 blocks away. Cooldown 20 s.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a ten-hit basic combo and the innate skill Ender Ritual. It also has a passive skill.
