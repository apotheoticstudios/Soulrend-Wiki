**Mjolnir** is a greathammer from Simply Swords. It is weapon 45 of 56 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Storm** (unique effect): while it is raining or the wielder is wet, each hit has a 15% chance to bring lightning down on the target.
- **Hold right-click:** while channeling, the wielder gets Resistance and creates a storm that rains lightning on enemies within 10 blocks, for up to 10 s. Cooldown 35 s.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has an eight-hit basic combo and the innate skill Wrathful Lightning.
