**Acid Molten Blazing Hammer** is a hammer from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 40 of 58 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 11.5 |
| Attack Speed | 1.30 |
| Impact | 2.5 |
| Targets per swing | 5 |
| Armor Negation | 10% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 12 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

- **Hit effect:** each hit sets the target on fire for 8 s.
- **Flame Grant Me Strength** (sneak and right-click, 31 s cooldown): the wielder gets the Flame Grant Me Strength effect for 40 s, which the tooltip describes as Resistance I and Strength II.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default One Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
