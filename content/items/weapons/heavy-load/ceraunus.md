**Ceraunus** is a greataxe from Cataclysm. It is weapon 48 of 58 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter, and one of the two item quests on the Storm Eye line of Act III. It is a lightning weapon, and the more you dodge and parry, the more of its attacks you can use.

## Obtaining

It is made at a [[item:cataclysm:mechanical_fusion_anvil]] by fusing a [[item:simplyswords:runic_greataxe]] with an [[item:cataclysm:essence_of_the_storm]]. Essence of the Storm is a guaranteed drop of [[bosses/act-iii/scylla|Scylla]], the Sin of the Storm.

## Usage

Its tooltip works on skill points. A Perfect Dodge or Perfect Parry gives you a point, and three attacks spend them:

| Key | Cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 skill point | Throws Ceraunus forward |
| 2 | 2 skill points | Summons a sector-shaped wave forward |
| 3 | 3 skill points | Leaps forward, releases lightning in all directions and summons waves |

The tooltip's Left Shift line points to an innate skill, but the game gives that skill no text of its own.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a sword. Its default Two Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and an innate skill of its own.
