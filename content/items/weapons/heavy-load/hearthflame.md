**Hearthflame** is a greathammer from Simply Swords. It is weapon 48 of 56 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

- **Volcanic Fury** (unique effect): each hit has a 25% chance to set the target on fire and send it flying.
- **Hold right-click:** the wielder channels their fury, trading health for Resistance while tremors pull enemies toward them. When the channel stops, the fury is released: enemies nearby are launched, set on fire and damaged, more the longer the channel lasted. Cooldown 15 s.

The ability scales with Fire spell power.
