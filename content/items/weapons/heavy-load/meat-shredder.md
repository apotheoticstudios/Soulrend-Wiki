**Meat Shredder** is an axe from cataclysm. It is weapon 49 of 56 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 16 |
| Attack Speed | 1.40 |
| Impact | 3.1 |
| Targets per swing | 14 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 16 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Right-click:** it damages entities in front of the wielder, ignoring their invulnerability frames (the short immunity after taking damage).

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a tachi. Its default One Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind. It has one other style, shown below.
