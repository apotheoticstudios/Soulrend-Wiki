**The Gravedigger** is a greataxe from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 19 of 58 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Rise Palehand** (ability): it summons a Palehand minion, using Cursed Souls from the inventory. The item itself has no use procedure; the ability is handled elsewhere in the mod.
