**Soulkeeper** is a greathammer from Simply Swords. It is weapon 52 of 56 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Soul Meld** (unique effect): each hit has a 75% chance to absorb nearby souls, making the wielder tougher but slowing their attack speed.
- **On right-click:** the wielder releases the souls, passing the attack speed penalty to enemies within 5 blocks and regenerating.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Terra Destruction.
