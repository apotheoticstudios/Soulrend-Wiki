**Rotten Hammer** is a greathammer from dungeons and combat. It is weapon 1 of 58 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 1 |
| Attack Speed | 2.12 |
| Impact | 1.9 |
| Targets per swing | 6 |
| Armor Negation | 36.6% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 2 |

## Usage

Its tooltip lists **Slowness** as a hit effect, but the item itself has no hit procedure; the effect is handled elsewhere in the mod.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default Two Handed style has a six-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
