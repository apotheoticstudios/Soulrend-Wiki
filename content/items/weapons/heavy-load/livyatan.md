**Livyatan** is a greataxe from Simply Swords. It is weapon 46 of 56 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Frost Shatter** (unique effect): each hit has a 15% chance to encase nearby enemies (within 3 blocks) in ice. Encased enemies take no damage; after 4 s the ice shatters for 18 damage.
- **On right-click:** the wielder shatters the ice early, dealing more damage the earlier they do it.

The ability scales with Frost spell power.
