**Whirligig Sawblade** is a greataxe from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 23 of 58 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 8 |
| Attack Speed | 1.60 |
| Impact | 1.7 |
| Targets per swing | 4 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Durability | 1,537 |
| Enchantability | 16 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Rip and Tear** (tooltip under Left Alt): using it shreds targets ahead (5 damage per hit in Soulrend, for up to 5 s) and makes them Bleed for 8 s. Cooldown 5 s, which enchantments can lower.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Ender Ritual.
