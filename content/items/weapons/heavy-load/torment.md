**Torment** is a greataxe from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 34 of 58 in [[items/weapons/heavy-load|Heavy Load]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

Its tooltip describes an axe that absorbed the wrath of its former bearer. Its innate skill builds that rage into its combat; see [[#Moveset]].

In Epic Fight it is a torment. Its default Two Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill True Wrath. It has one other style, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
