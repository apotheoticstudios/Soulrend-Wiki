**Featherlight** is a sword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 17 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Durability | 1,756 |
| Enchantability | 10 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Delicate Dragonglass** (tooltip under Left Alt): runes inscribed on the blade give it a high attack speed. It has no active ability.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default One Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
