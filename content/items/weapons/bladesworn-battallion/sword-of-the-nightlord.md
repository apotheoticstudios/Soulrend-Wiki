**Sword of the Nightlord** is a sword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 32 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 10 |
| Attack Speed | 1.20 |
| Impact | 3.7 |
| Targets per swing | 4 |
| Armor Negation | 15% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 10 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Weapon Affinity** (tooltip under Left Alt): while the wielder has the Potency effect, it deals more damage.
