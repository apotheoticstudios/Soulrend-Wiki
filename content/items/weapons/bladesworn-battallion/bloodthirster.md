**Bloodthirster** is a sword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 31 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Durability | 1,984 |
| Enchantability | 8 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It has two abilities, listed in the tooltip under Left Alt:

- **Life Steal:** every hit heals the wielder while the item is not on cooldown.
- **Overheal:** at full health, the wielder gets a shield (absorption) instead of healing.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default One Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Heartpiercer.
