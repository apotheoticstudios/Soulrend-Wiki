**Blade of the Dreaded Queen** is a sword from Ice and Fire. It is weapon 47 of 49 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Stats

| --- | --- |

| Impact | 3.3 |
| Targets per swing | 6 |

| Enchantability | 10 |

## Usage

It has no abilities of its own: its tooltip lists only stats (see [[#Stats]]), and it fights with the Epic Fight moveset below.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default One Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Heartpiercer.
