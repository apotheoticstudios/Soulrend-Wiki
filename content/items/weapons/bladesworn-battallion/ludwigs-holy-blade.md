**Ludwig's Holy Blade** is a sword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 13 of 49 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Durability | 1,537 |
| Enchantability | 16 |

## Usage

**Righteous** (tooltip under Left Alt): it deals extra damage to undead, more at higher upgrade levels. The tooltip shows the current bonus.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default One Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
