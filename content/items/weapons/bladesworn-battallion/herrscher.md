**Herrscher** is a sword from Weapons of miracle. It is weapon 33 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

Its tooltip calls it a sharp, light sword that "seems to need something in order to release its full potential". Its partner is the shield [[items/weapons/bulwark-and-brawl/gesetz|Gesetz]]: held one-handed, Herrscher's innate skill **Regierung** performs shield attacks, and Gesetz takes on Herrscher's guard poses. With another sword in the off hand it switches to a dual-wield style instead. The full skill texts are under [[#Moveset]].
