**Milady** is a sword from EpicFight - Dawnday. It is weapon 29 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

Pressing **Z** switches between its stances, as its tooltip says. Its lore describes a light greatsword: as long as a greatsword but light enough to swing like a straight sword. The stances are the extra styles listed under [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a light greatsword. Its default One Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Piercing Strike. It has two other styles, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
