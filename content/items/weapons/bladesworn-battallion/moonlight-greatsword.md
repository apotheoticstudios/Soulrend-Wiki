**Moonlight Greatsword** is a sword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 12 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

One loot table drops it:

| Source | Chance | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[bosses/act-ii/fallen-icon\|Fallen Icon]] (drop) | 100% | 1 |

It can also be crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

**Moonlight** (tooltip under Left Alt): using it fires Moonlight Beams, which deal 8 damage in Soulrend.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default One Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
