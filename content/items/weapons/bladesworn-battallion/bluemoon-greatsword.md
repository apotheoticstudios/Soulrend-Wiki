**Bluemoon Greatsword** is a sword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 40 of 49 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

It has three abilities, listed in the tooltip under Left Alt:

- **Moonlight:** using it fires Moonlight Beams (18 damage in Soulrend).
- **Lunar Essence:** each hit gives 1 Essence; the tooltip shows the current amount.
- **Sealed Potential:** the ability needs Essence (8 in Soulrend) and uses it all up. The **Lunar Herald** effect removes the need.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default One Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
