**Spellbreaker** is a sword from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is weapon 34 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 10 |
| Attack Speed | 1.80 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Cooldown Reduction | +15% |
| Durability | 2,031 |
| Enchantability | 12 |

## Usage

It comes imbued with the spell **Counterspell** (level 1, 6 s cooldown), which interrupts a targeted magical process such as a creature casting a spell or a magical projectile. Its tooltip shows one imbue slot, so the spell can be replaced.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default One Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Heartpiercer.
