**Flamed Dragon Bone Sword** is a sword from Ice and Fire. It is weapon 25 of 49 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

## Usage

It is a Legendary Weapon from Ice and Fire. Its hits set targets on fire and knock them back, and it deals +8 damage to Ice Dragons.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default One Handed style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Heartpiercer.
