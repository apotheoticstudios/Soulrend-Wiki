**Phantasmal Blade** is a sword from Ice and Fire. It is weapon 28 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

It is a Legendary Weapon from Ice and Fire. Swinging it also fires a spinning sword projectile that travels through blocks, and the projectile deals extra damage.
