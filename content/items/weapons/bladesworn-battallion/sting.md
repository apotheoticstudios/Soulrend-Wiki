**Sting** is a sword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 8 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 6 |
| Attack Speed | 1.60 |
| Impact | 1.6 |
| Targets per swing | 2 |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 10 |

## Usage

It has two abilities, listed in the tooltip under Left Alt:

- **Luminescence:** it lights up when hostile mobs are near.
- **Spider's Bane:** it deals 4 bonus damage to arthropods.

In Epic Fight it is a sword. Its default One Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Sweeping Edge. It has one other style, shown below.
