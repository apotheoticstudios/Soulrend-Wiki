**Vanguard Sword** is a sword from EpicFight Nightfall; its in-game name is written "[Vanguard Sword]" and its item id is `efn:sword_of_pioneer`. It is weapon 50 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter, and the last quest in it. It is a saber for the king's vanguard, with a combo that ends in a hilt strike that breaks defenses.

## Obtaining

It is made at a [[item:minecraft:smithing_table]] with Soulrend's upgrade recipe. The base is either of the chapter's two bloodline swords, the [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion/crimson-sovereign|Crimson Sovereign]] or the [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion/crimson-bloodweave|Crimson Bloodweave]]. The template is a [[item:block_factorys_bosses:dragon_banner]] and the addition an [[item:apotheotic_additions:esoteric_material]] (dropped by the Decaying King in the Past; see [[progression/act-v-duties/esoteric-material|Esoteric Material]]). The upgrade keeps the base's enchantments, gems and affixes (see [[progression/prologue/jei-menu#Upgrading Gear|Upgrading Gear]]).

The undead Rotten Knights of the Past carry one ([[enemies/act-v/rotten-soldiers|Rotten Soldiers]]).

## Usage

> [!LORE] [Vanguard Sword] (item tooltip)
> Commissioned by a king for his vanguard troops during their first Nether expedition, this exquisitely crafted saber remains as sharp as ever

Its innate skill is **Active Skill-Breaching Combo**: "Initiate with rapid slashes, then break defenses with a hilt strike". The moves are under [[#Moveset]]. The game has an advancement for obtaining it, "Vanguard's Legacy", whose text says the saber "still bears the crest of the first Nether expedition" and that its edge "has tasted demon blood".

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an uchigatana. Its default One Handed style has a four-hit basic combo and the innate skill Active Skill-Breaching Combo.
