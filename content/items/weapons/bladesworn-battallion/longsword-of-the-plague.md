**Longsword of the Plague** is a sword from Simply Swords. It is weapon 42 of 49 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

**Plague** (unique effect): each hit has a 55% chance to turn one of the target's positive effects into its negative counterpart. The Simply Swords codex text in the pack lists the conversions: Haste to Mining Fatigue, Regeneration to Wither, Strength to Weakness, Speed to Slowness, Invisibility to Glowing, Resistance to Nausea, Saturation to Hunger, Fire Resistance to Poison, and Absorption to damage. The negative effect is half as strong as the positive one.
