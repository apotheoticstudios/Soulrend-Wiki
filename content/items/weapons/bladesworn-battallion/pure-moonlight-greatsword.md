**Pure Moonlight Greatsword** is a sword from Marium's Soulslike Weaponry. It is weapon 30 of 50 in [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion|Bladesworn Battallion]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Attack Damage | 11 |
| Attack Speed | 1.30 |
| Impact | 2.5 |
| Targets per swing | 5 |
| Armor Negation | 10% |
| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 10 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

**Moonlight** (tooltip under Left Alt): using it fires Moonlight Beams, which deal 9 damage in Soulrend.

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is a greatsword. Its default One Handed style has a five-hit basic combo and the innate skill Steel Whirlwind.
