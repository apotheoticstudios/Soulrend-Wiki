**Dreadbow** is a bow from Alex's Caves. It is weapon 15 of 35 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

The Dreadbow's tooltip describes no ability. Its sound subtitles, "Dreadbow released" and "Darkness rains down", suggest that its arrows bring down a rain of darkness. Its own enchantment, **Relentless Darkness**, makes it fire arrows straight ahead in a continuous stream.
