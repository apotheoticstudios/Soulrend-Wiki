**Spectral Whisper** is a bow from Too Many Bows. It is weapon 19 of 35 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pull Time (s) | 1 |
| Arrow Velocity | +150% |
| Projectile Damage | +15 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Durability | 500 |
| Enchantability | 1 |

## Usage

It fires spectral arrows that pass through the first block in their way and still damage entities behind it. The **Spectral** enchantment lets them pass through more obstacles.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
