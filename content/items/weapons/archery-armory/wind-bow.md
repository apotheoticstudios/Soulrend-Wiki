**Wind Bow** is a bow from Too Many Bows. It is weapon 27 of 35 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

## Usage

It has two abilities:

- **Gale Burst:** an arrow releases a gust of wind where it lands, knocking nearby enemies outward. A direct hit deals light magic damage.
- **Tailwind:** hitting an enemy or a surface gives the player Speed II for a short time and Slow Falling, for repositioning in the air.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
