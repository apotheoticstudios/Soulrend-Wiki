**Arc Of The Heavens** is a bow from Too Many Bows. It is weapon 31 of 35 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pull Time (s) | 1 |
| Cast Time Reduction | +15% |
| Projectile Damage | +9 |
| Lightning Spell Power | +0.3 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Durability | 500 |
| Enchantability | 1 |

## Usage

A direct hit strikes the target with lightning. While held, it also adds 15% cast time reduction and Lightning spell power, so it suits a mage who uses a bow.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
