**Wrath of the Desert** is a bow from Cataclysm. It is weapon 23 of 35 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter, and the quest that follows the Cursed Bow on Act III's Cursed Eye line. It is the upgraded [[items/weapons/archery-armory/cursed-bow|Cursed Bow]]: it fires sandstorms instead of plain arrows, powered by skill points you earn by dodging and parrying.

## Obtaining

It is crafted in a crafting table with Soulrend's upgrade recipe: a [[items/weapons/archery-armory/cursed-bow|Cursed Bow]] in the middle, with a [[item:cataclysm:sandstorm_in_a_bottle]] beside it, two [[item:cataclysm:witherite_ingot]] above and below, and three [[item:simplyswords:empowered_remnant]] in the right-hand column. The upgrade keeps the Cursed Bow's enchantments, gems and affixes (see [[progression/prologue/jei-menu#Upgrading Gear|Upgrading Gear]]).

[[bosses/act-iii/ancient-remnant|Ancient Remnant]] always drops one Sandstorm in a Bottle. Empowered Remnants come from smelting Nightfall relic weapons, such as [[items/weapons/path-of-the-samurai/bloodlust|Bloodlust]] or the [[items/weapons/bladesworn-battallion/vanguard-sword|Vanguard Sword]].

## Usage

Its tooltip works on skill points. A Perfect Dodge or Perfect Parry gives you a point, and three attacks spend them:

| Key | Cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 skill point | Shoots three sandstorms forward |
| 2 | 2 skill points | Shoots three cursed sandstorms forward |
| 3 | 4 skill points | Summons three sandstorms, then brings Ancient Desert Steles crashing down around you |

The tooltip's Left Shift line points to an innate skill, but the game gives that skill no text of its own.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
