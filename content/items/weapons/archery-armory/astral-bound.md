**Astral Bound** is a bow from Too Many Bows. It is weapon 16 of 35 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pull Time (s) | 1 |
| Arrow Velocity | +120% |
| Projectile Damage | +12 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Enchantability | 20 |

## Usage

Its arrows ricochet off surfaces. The **Ricochet** enchantment, which only works on this bow, lets them bounce off more surfaces.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
