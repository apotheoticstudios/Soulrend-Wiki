**Ethereal Hunter** is a bow from Too Many Bows. It is weapon 17 of 33 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pull Time (s) | 1 |
| Projectile Damage | +28 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Durability | 950 |
| Enchantability | 15 |

## Usage

It fires radiant arrows paid for with hunger instead of arrows: each shot costs hunger points, and the bow won't fire when the player is too hungry.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
