**Cursed Bow** is a bow from Cataclysm. It is weapon 22 of 35 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter, and one of the item quests on the Cursed Eye line of Act III. It fires several arrows at once, and it is the first step to [[items/weapons/archery-armory/wrath-of-the-desert|Wrath of the Desert]].

## Obtaining

It is crafted in a crafting table with Soulrend's upgrade recipe: a [[items/weapons/archery-armory/twin-shadows|Twin Shadows]] in the middle, surrounded by three [[item:cataclysm:cursium_ingot]] and three [[item:cataclysm:black_steel_ingot]]. The upgrade keeps the Twin Shadows' enchantments, gems and affixes (see [[progression/prologue/jei-menu#Upgrading Gear|Upgrading Gear]]). Cursium Ingots are a guaranteed drop of [[bosses/act-iii/maledictus|Maledictus]], 3–4 at a time.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
