**Necro Flame Bow** is a bow from Too Many Bows. It is weapon 24 of 33 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pull Time (s) | 1 |
| Cast Time Reduction | +5% |
| Fire Spell Power | +0.1 |
| Projectile Damage | +3 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Durability | 500 |
| Enchantability | 1 |

## Usage

Its arrows set enemies on cursed fire. Cursed fire blocks all regeneration and can't be put out with water. The tooltip says it deals a heart of damage per tick.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
