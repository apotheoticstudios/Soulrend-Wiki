**Dark Bow** is a bow from Too Many Bows. It is weapon 20 of 35 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

It fires a sonic shockwave, like a Warden's roar, that pierces through enemies.

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
