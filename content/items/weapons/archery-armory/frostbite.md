**Frostbite** is a bow from Too Many Bows. It is weapon 26 of 33 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pull Time (s) | 1 |
| Cast Time Reduction | +5% |

| Ice Spell Power | +0.1 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Durability | 500 |
| Enchantability | 1 |

## Usage

It fires frost arrows. Fully drawn shots slow and freeze the target. While the bow is drawn, the player also gets the Frost Walker effect.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
