**Dusk Reaper** is a bow from Too Many Bows. It is weapon 21 of 33 in [[items/weapons/archery-armory|Archery Armory (Weapons)]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pull Time (s) | 1 |
| Arrow Velocity | +150% |
| Projectile Damage | +15 |
| Targets per swing | 1 |
| Durability | 950 |
| Enchantability | 20 |

## Usage

Each shot uses up a [[item:too_many_bows:soul_fragment]]; without one the bow won't fire. Its arrows mark enemies. A marked enemy:

- takes more damage and leaves a damaging trail;
- gets Weakness and Slowness and takes damage over time;
- shows through walls;
- explodes when it dies, damaging everything nearby.

A fully drawn shot also creates a spectral zone that damages and slows enemies inside it.

## Moveset

Epic Fight treats it as a ranged weapon, so it has no melee combo.
