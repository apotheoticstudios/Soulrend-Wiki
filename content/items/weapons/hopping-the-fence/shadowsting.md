**Shadowsting** is a rapier from Simply Swords. It is weapon 17 of 19 in [[items/weapons/hopping-the-fence|Hopping the Fence]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

- **Shadowmist** (unique effect): each hit has a 25% chance to deal extra magic damage that scales with the target's armor.
- **On right-click:** the wielder creates a blinding mist at their feet (4-block radius, 3 s of Blindness) and shadowsteps a short distance forward. Cooldown 10 s.

In Epic Fight it is a longsword. Its default Two Handed style has a seven-hit basic combo and the innate skill Void Slash!. It has one other style, shown below. It also has a passive skill.
