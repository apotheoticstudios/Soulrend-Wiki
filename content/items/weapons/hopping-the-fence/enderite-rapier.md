**Enderite Rapier** is a rapier from Rapier For EpicFightMod. It is weapon 19 of 19 in [[items/weapons/hopping-the-fence|Hopping the Fence]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

It is an upgraded rapier; Soulrend adds its own upgrade chain next to the mod's recipe (see [[#Crafting]]). Its innate skill, **The Ender Dance**, kicks the target into an End portal and shatters it with the End's lost energy; it also has a passive skill. See [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an enderite rapier. Its default Guard Stance style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill The Ender Dance. It also has a passive skill.
