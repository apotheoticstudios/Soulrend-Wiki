**Amethyst Rapier** is a rapier from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is weapon 18 of 19 in [[items/weapons/hopping-the-fence|Hopping the Fence]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

## Usage

It comes imbued with the spell **Echoing Strikes** (level 5, 24 s effect, 30 s cooldown). While the spell lasts, non-magical attacks leave an echo of ender energy that explodes shortly after with an echo of the attack's damage. Its tooltip shows one imbue slot, so the spell can be replaced. In Epic Fight it uses Rapier For EpicFightMod's amethyst rapier type, whose innate skill is described under [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an amethyst rapier. Its default Guard Stance style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill Amethyst Crystal Crush.
