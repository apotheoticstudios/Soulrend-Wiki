**Bramblethorn** is a rapier from Simply Swords. It is weapon 15 of 19 in [[items/weapons/hopping-the-fence|Hopping the Fence]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Usage

**Bramblethorn** (unique effect): each hit has a 45% chance to release a swarm of toxic spores that orbit the wielder and afflict nearby enemies with stronger and stronger effects. The spores stay active as long as they have a target in range, and keep working at reduced strength after the wielder swaps weapons.
