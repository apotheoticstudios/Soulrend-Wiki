**Oceanite Rapier** is a rapier from Rapier For EpicFightMod. It is weapon 13 of 19 in [[items/weapons/hopping-the-fence|Hopping the Fence]], a Pursuit of Mastery quest chapter.

## Obtaining

It is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).

| Entity Reach | 3 |
| Enchantability | 15 |
| Fire resistant | Yes |

## Usage

It is an upgraded rapier; Soulrend adds its own upgrade chain next to the mod's recipe (see [[#Crafting]]). Its innate skill, **The Ocean Flow**, kicks the target, rises with the rain and drives the rain on the blade through the target's armor; it also has a passive skill. See [[#Moveset]].

## Moveset

In Epic Fight it is an oceanite rapier. Its default Guard Stance style has a three-hit basic combo and the innate skill The Ocean Flow. It also has a passive skill.
