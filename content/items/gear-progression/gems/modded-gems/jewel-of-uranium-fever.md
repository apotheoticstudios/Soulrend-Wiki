**Jewel of Uranium Fever** is an Apotheosis gem (`apotheotic_additions:modded/uranium`). Gems go into sockets on gear and give a bonus that depends on the gem's rarity and the kind of item it is socketed in; [[items/gear-progression/gems|Gems]] explains sockets and the notation in the table.

## Obtaining

It is one of the random gems, in every dimension. Random gems drop from hostile mobs killed by a player (8% of kills, 58% for bosses) and appear in chests (25% of vanilla chests, 20% of other chests). Its drop weight is 3, which makes it about 0.7% of random gems in the Overworld, the Nether and the End (before Luck). It drops as Rare in the Overworld, Rare–Epic in the Nether, Rare–Mythic in the End and Rare in the Otherside, and as any rarity from Rare to Esoteric elsewhere.

The [[items/gear-progression/gems#Upgrading gems|Gem Cutting Table]] raises a gem one rarity at a time, up to Esoteric.
