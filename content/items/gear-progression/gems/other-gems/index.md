**Other Gems** are 9 gems that Soulrend adds itself (its `soulrend_apotheosis_additions` datapack): spell gems for the Aqua, Geo and Sound schools, which Iron's own gems don't cover, and the Depth, Geolith and Vibranium Shards. All drop at random in every dimension.
