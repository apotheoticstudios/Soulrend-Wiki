**Gem of the Incandescent Inferno** is an Apotheosis gem (`apotheosis:the_nether/inferno`). Gems go into sockets on gear and give a bonus that depends on the gem's rarity and the kind of item it is socketed in; [[items/gear-progression/gems|Gems]] explains sockets and the notation in the table.

## Bonuses

It fits in boots, chestplates, heavy weapons, helmets, pickaxes, shovels, staffs, swords and tridents.

| Socketed in | Rare | Epic | Mythic | Ancient |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light Weapons | +6 Fire Damage | +8 Fire Damage | +10 Fire Damage | +12.5 Fire Damage |
| Chestplates | +1 level to Berserker's Fury | +1 level to Berserker's Fury | +2 levels to Berserker's Fury | +2 levels to Berserker's Fury |
| Mining Tools | +1 level to Efficiency | +2 levels to Efficiency | +3 levels to Efficiency | +4 levels to Efficiency |
| Helmets | +50% Fire Damage | +62.5% Fire Damage | +75% Fire Damage | +90% Fire Damage |
| Boots | +15% Max Mana | +20% Max Mana | +25% Max Mana | +30% Max Mana |
| Staffs | +3% Fire Spell Power | +6% Fire Spell Power | +10% Fire Spell Power | +15% Fire Spell Power |
| Heavy Weapons |  |  | On hit, inflict Flaming Detonation (00:05) [⌛ 01:00] | On hit, inflict Flaming Detonation II (00:05) [⌛ 01:00] |

## Obtaining

It is one of the random gems, only in the Nether. Random gems drop from hostile mobs killed by a player (8% of kills, 58% for bosses) and appear in chests (25% of vanilla chests, 20% of other chests). Its drop weight is 5, which makes it about 1.1% of random gems in the Nether (before Luck). It drops as Rare–Epic in the Nether.

The [[items/gear-progression/gems#Upgrading gems|Gem Cutting Table]] raises a gem one rarity at a time, up to Ancient.
