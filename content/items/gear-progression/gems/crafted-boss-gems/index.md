**Crafted Boss Gems** are Apotheotic Additions' 13 Eyes. Each is crafted with a Sigil of Awakening and a boss's materials, comes out at Artifact rarity and can be cut up to Esoteric. They never drop at random. The [[progression/side-chapters/reforging|Reforging]] quest chapter has a quest for each.
