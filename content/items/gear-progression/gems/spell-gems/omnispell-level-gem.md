**Omnispell Level Gem** (`fallen_gems_affixes:compat/spell_general`) is a Fallen Gems & Affixes gem that would raise the level of every spell school. Soulrend's `soulrend_apotheosis_rebalance` datapack disables it with a condition that never passes, so it doesn't exist in game: it never drops, and no recipe makes it.
