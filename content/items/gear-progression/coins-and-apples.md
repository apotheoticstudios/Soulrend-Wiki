**Coins & Apples** covers Apotheotic Additions' three trading coins and its five rarity apples.

## Usage

### Coins

Apotheotic Coins are a currency for the Wandering Trader, who gets extra trades from Apotheotic Additions.

- **Getting them.** Apotheotic Coins come from the chests of rogue spawners, one or two per chest. The trader also buys Common, Uncommon and Rare rarity materials and copper blocks for coins.
- **Higher coins.** Infusing an Apotheotic Coin in an enchanting table (Eterna 20, Quanta 10%) makes an **Infused Coin**, and infusing that (Eterna 50, Quanta 35%, Arcana 50%) makes an **Ascended Coin**.
- **Rare offers.** Trades marked "Rare offer" fill the trader's rare slot, so only some traders have them.

### Rarity apples

The rarity apples are plain food: an apple surrounded by eight rarity materials of one rarity. Higher rarities restore more hunger and saturation (see the table above), and none of them give effects.
