**Epic Knights Armor** lists 71 armor pieces (Every Epic Knights armor set on one page.). Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

Epic Knights armor has no set bonuses or abilities; its pieces differ in stats and looks. A few tooltip notes:

- **Jousting armor** and the **Frog-Mouth Helm** slow the wearer down ("Slow movement speed").
- Brigandine, crusader, cuirassier, gambeson, greathelm, Norman, coif, pantyhose and the Crown and Flowercrown decorations **can be dyed** in any color.
- **Decorations** (Crown, Flowercrown, Minicrown) attach to a helmet rather than being worn alone.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
