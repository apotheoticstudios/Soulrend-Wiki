**Fire Dragon Scale Armor** is a family of armor sets from Ice and Fire. It is set 1 of 15 on [[items/armor/strength-path|Strength Path]]. The quest accepts any color of the element (`iceandfire:armor_bronze_*`, `iceandfire:armor_gray_*`, `iceandfire:armor_green_*`, `iceandfire:armor_red_*`), so every matching set is listed.

## Usage

Ice and Fire dragon scale armor protects better against dragon breath attacks, as its tooltip says. The page covers every fire dragon color (Bronze, Gray, Emerald, Red); the color changes only the look.

## Epic Fight

Together, its 16 pieces add **240 Weight** and **24 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
