**Polished Fire Dragon Scale Armor** is a family of armor sets from dungeons and combat. It is set 2 of 15 on [[items/armor/strength-path|Strength Path]]. The quest accepts any color of the element (`dungeons_and_combat:black_dragon_*`, `dungeons_and_combat:bronze_dragon_*`, `dungeons_and_combat:green_dragon_*`, `dungeons_and_combat:red_dragon_*`), so every matching set is listed.

## Usage

The page covers Dungeons and Combat's dragon armor in every color (Black, Black Electric, Bronze, Emerald, Grey, Red); the color changes only the look. The set has no set bonus or ability of its own: its tooltips list only stats (see [[#Stats]]).

## Epic Fight

Together, its 20 pieces add **280 Weight** and **36 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".

Every piece is crafted (see [[#Crafting]]).
