## Usage

This armor goes on mounts, not on the player, and none of it has an ability. Ice and Fire dragon armor comes in four pieces per material (Head, Neck, Body and Tail, as each tooltip says). Hippogryph armor and Epic Knights horse barding protect hippogryphs and horses.
