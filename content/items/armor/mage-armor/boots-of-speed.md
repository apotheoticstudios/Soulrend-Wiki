## Usage

It has no ability of its own: its tooltip lists only stats (see [[#Stats]]).

## Epic Fight

It adds **7.5 Weight** and **1.13 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
