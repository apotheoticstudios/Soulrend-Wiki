**Misc Mage Robes** is an armor set from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is set 31 of 30 on [[items/armor/mage-armor|Mage Armor]].

## Usage

These netherite mage robes add the spell attributes in their tables. The chestplate has one empty imbue slot for an Iron's Spellbooks spell. They have no set bonus.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
