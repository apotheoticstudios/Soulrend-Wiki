**Wandering Magician Armor** is an armor set from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is set 20 of 30 on [[items/armor/mage-armor|Mage Armor]].

## Usage

The set has no set bonus or ability of its own: its tooltips list only stats (see [[#Stats]]).

## Epic Fight

Together, its 4 pieces add **12.6 Weight** and **3.15 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
