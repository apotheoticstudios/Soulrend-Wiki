## Usage

**Full set bonus:** while the wearer is Bleeding, they get Spell Strength III, Resistance III, Speed III and Cast Speed III. The robe has one empty imbue slot.

## Epic Fight

Together, its 4 pieces add **72.5 Weight** and **10.88 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
