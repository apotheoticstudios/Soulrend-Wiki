**Lightbringer Chestplate** is a piece of armor from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is set 15 of 30 on [[items/armor/mage-armor|Mage Armor]].

## Stats

Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece. The full-set row adds them up.

| [[item:irons_spellbooks:paladin_chestplate]] | Chestplate | +8 | +4 | +40% | +150 | +10% | +3 | +20 | 640 |

## Usage

It is a mage set: it adds the spell attributes listed in [[#Stats]], and its chestplate has one empty imbue slot for an Iron's Spellbooks spell. It has no set bonus.

## Epic Fight

It adds **20 Weight** and **3 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
