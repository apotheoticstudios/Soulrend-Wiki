## Usage

It is a mage set: it adds the spell attributes listed in [[#Stats]], and its chestplate has one empty imbue slot for an Iron's Spellbooks spell. It has no set bonus.

## Epic Fight

Together, its 4 pieces add **82.5 Weight** and **12.38 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
