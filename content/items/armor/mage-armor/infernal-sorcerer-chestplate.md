**Infernal Sorcerer Chestplate** is a piece of armor from Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It is set 9 of 30 on [[items/armor/mage-armor|Mage Armor]].

## Stats

Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece. The full-set row adds them up.

| Piece | Slot | Armor | Max Mana | Spell Power | Stun Armor | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[item:irons_spellbooks:infernal_sorcerer_chestplate]] | Chestplate | +8 | +150 | +10% | +3 | +20 |

## Usage

**Passive ability:** fire spell damage adds a stack of Immolation to the creature hit. At 3 stacks, the creature explodes for 10 damage, spreading Immolation. It has one empty imbue slot.

## Epic Fight

It adds **20 Weight** and **3 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
