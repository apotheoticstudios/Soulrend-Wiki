**Polished Ice Dragon Scale Armor** is a family of armor sets from dungeons and combat. It is set 2 of 13 on [[items/armor/wisdom-path|Wisdom Path]]. The quest accepts any color of the element (`dungeons_and_combat:blue_dragon_*`, `dungeons_and_combat:sapphire_dragon_*`, `dungeons_and_combat:silver_dragon_*`, `dungeons_and_combat:white_dragon_*`), so every matching set is listed.

## Usage

The page covers Dungeons and Combat's dragon armor in the ice colors (Blue, Sapphire, Silver, White); the color changes only the look. The set has no set bonus or ability of its own: its tooltips list only stats (see [[#Stats]]). The Blue Dragon Shield on this page is a shield, and like every shield in Soulrend it can parry with Soulslike Weaponry's "Parry with shield" key (2 s cooldown).

## Epic Fight

Together, its 16 pieces add **176 Weight** and **24 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
