**Ice Dragon Scale Armor** is a family of armor sets from Ice and Fire. It is set 1 of 13 on [[items/armor/wisdom-path|Wisdom Path]]. The quest accepts any color of the element (`iceandfire:armor_blue_*`, `iceandfire:armor_sapphire_*`, `iceandfire:armor_silver_*`, `iceandfire:armor_white_*`), so every matching set is listed.

## Usage

Ice and Fire dragon scale armor protects better against dragon breath attacks, as its tooltip says. The page covers every ice dragon color (Blue, Sapphire, Silver, White); the color changes only the look. It also lists Ice and Fire's low-tier silver armor pieces, which have no ability.

## Epic Fight

Together, its 20 pieces add **210.5 Weight** and **30.15 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
