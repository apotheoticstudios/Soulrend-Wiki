**Polished Lightning Dragon Scale Armor** is a family of armor sets from dungeons and combat. It is set 15 of 17 on [[items/armor/agility-path|Agility Path]]. The quest accepts any color of the element (`dungeons_and_combat:amethyst_dragon_*`, `dungeons_and_combat:black_dragon_electric_*`, `dungeons_and_combat:copper_dragon_*`, `dungeons_and_combat:electric_dragon_*`), so every matching set is listed.

## Usage

The page covers Dungeons and Combat's dragon armor in the lightning colors (Amethyst, Copper, Electric Blue); the color changes only the look. The set has no set bonus or ability of its own: its tooltips list only stats (see [[#Stats]]). The Amethyst Dragon Shield on this page is a shield, and like every shield in Soulrend it can parry with Soulslike Weaponry's "Parry with shield" key (2 s cooldown).

## Epic Fight

Together, its 12 pieces add **120 Weight** and **36 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
