**Lightning Dragon Scale Armor** is a family of armor sets from Ice and Fire. It is set 14 of 17 on [[items/armor/agility-path|Agility Path]]. The quest accepts any color of the element (`iceandfire:armor_amythest_*`, `iceandfire:armor_black_*`, `iceandfire:armor_copper_*`, `iceandfire:armor_electric_*`), so every matching set is listed.

## Usage

Ice and Fire dragon scale armor protects better against dragon breath attacks, as its tooltip says. The page covers every lightning dragon color (Amethyst, Black, Copper, Electric Blue); the color changes only the look. It also lists Ice and Fire's low-tier copper armor pieces, which have no ability.

## Epic Fight

Together, its 20 pieces add **189.25 Weight** and **53.48 Stun Armor** in Epic Fight (per piece in [[#Stats]]). In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
