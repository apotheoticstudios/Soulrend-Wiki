**Samurai Dynasty Armor** lists 146 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

Samurai Dynasty sets have no set bonuses. Two pieces stand out:

- **Mechanical Samurai Helmet:** works like Create's Engineer's Goggles, showing extra information about placed kinetic components, stress and block contents.
- **Warden Samurai Boots:** their tooltip says they dampen vibrations.

The colored samurai sets come in standard, light and master versions with different stats.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
