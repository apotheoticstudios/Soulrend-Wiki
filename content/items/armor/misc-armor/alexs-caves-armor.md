**Alex's Caves Armor** lists 18 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

- **Cloak of Darkness:** when empowered, the Special Ability key turns the wearer into Darkness Incarnate. It can't be used in light.

The other Alex's Caves pieces (diving, gingerbread, hazmat, primordial, Hood of Darkness, Rainbounce Boots) have no ability text; their effects are the attributes in the tables.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
