**Minecraft Armor** lists 30 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

These are the vanilla armor pieces, with stats rebalanced by Soulrend's attribute config. The **Elytra** enables elytra flight. None of them has a set bonus.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
