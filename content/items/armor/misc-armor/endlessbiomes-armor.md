**EndlessBiomes Armor** lists 6 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

- **Anklor shell armor:** makes the wearer lighter and absorbs radiation, as its tooltip says.
- **Void-touched boots and leggings:** the wearer falls much slower than normal; holding Shift falls normally.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
