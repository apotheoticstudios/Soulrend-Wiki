**Deeper and Darker Armor** lists 5 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

- **Soul Elytra:** elytra flight, plus a boost on a key with a cooldown shown in its tooltip.
- **Resonarium** pieces: no ability text.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
