**Dungeons and Combat Armor** lists 95 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

Set bonuses need all four pieces (or every piece the set has). From the tooltips:

- **Acid Blazing** and **Cursed Blazing:** Fire Resistance, and attackers are set on fire for 10 s.
- **Ammit:** an attack damage bonus.
- **Blessed Rogue:** extra movement speed and attack.
- **Bloodymancer:** while Bleeding, +20% Blood spell power per level of Bleeding.
- **Molten Knight:** Man of Dark Steel (+2 armor, +2 armor toughness) and Heart of the Forge (Resistance II while on fire), shared with the molten dark steel set.
- **Neptunium:** in water, Night Vision, Water Breathing and faster swimming.
- **Royal Steel** and the **Prisoner Helmet:** Man of Steel, +3 armor.

Single pieces with a bonus: the **Avarice Mask** gives Luck II, the **Bear Hood** improves the Hunter Fury effect, the **Bronze Barbarian Helmet** gives bonus attack damage, and the **Hood of Shame** gives the Forgotten Entity effect. The **Monarch** set is marked as a Creative Item. The other sets have no bonus.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
