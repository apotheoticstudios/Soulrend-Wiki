**Fantasy Armor** lists 104 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

Fantasy Armor sets carry only lore in their tooltips. None has a set bonus or ability; they differ only in stats and looks.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
