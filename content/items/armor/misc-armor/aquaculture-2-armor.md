**Aquaculture 2 Armor** lists 4 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

Each neptunium piece has its own ability (under Shift):

- **Helmet, Neptune's Gaze:** better underwater vision.
- **Chestplate, Neptune's Lungs:** breathing underwater.
- **Leggings, Neptune's Buoyancy:** weightless underwater.
- **Boots, Neptune's Swiftness:** faster swimming.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
