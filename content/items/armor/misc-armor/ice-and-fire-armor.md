**Ice and Fire Armor** lists 66 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

- **Tide Guardian armor** (every color): Water Breathing, and Strength while wet, stronger with the full set.
- **Troll leather armor** (forest, frost and mountain): less projectile damage, -10% from the helmet, -30% from the chestplate, -20% from the leggings and -10% from the boots.
- **Wood Earplugs:** a joke line ("Oh my God Steve has AirPods in he cant hear us!").
- **Death worm chitin, myrmex chitin, sheep disguise** and the **Blindfold:** no ability text.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
