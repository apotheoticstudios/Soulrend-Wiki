**Alex's Mobs Armor** lists 15 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

Each Alex's Mobs piece has its own effect, from its tooltip:

- **Centipede Leggings:** wall climbing.
- **Emu Leggings:** a chance to avoid projectile damage.
- **Flying Fish Boots:** gliding by jumping out of water.
- **Frontier Cap:** +0.1 speed while sneaking.
- **Froststalker Helmet:** Froststalkers follow the wearer.
- **Roadrunner Boots:** +0.1 speed on sand.
- **Rocky Chestplate:** rolling by sprinting.
- **Unsettling Kimono:** most undead creatures become neutral.
- **Novelty Hat** and **Sombrero:** cosmetic lines only.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
