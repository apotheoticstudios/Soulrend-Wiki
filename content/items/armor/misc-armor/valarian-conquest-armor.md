**Valarian Conquest Armor** lists 84 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

Valarian Conquest armor has no set bonus or ability; the pieces differ only in stats and looks. The chestplates come in numbered variants (II, III, IV) with the same stats.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
