**Aquamirae Armor** lists 13 armor pieces. Values are the tooltip modifiers of each piece.

## Usage

Aquamirae's sets have half-set and full-set abilities (under Shift):

- **Abyssal armor:** with half the set, +25% armor and +4 armor toughness. The full set with the **Heaume** delays the wearer's death when they take fatal damage, but they deal 80% less damage while the effect lasts. The full set with the **Tiara** blinds nearby hostile creatures and slows them by 50%.
- **Terrible armor:** with half the set, taking damage in water raises swimming speed for a while. With the full set, enemies that attack the wearer are poisoned.
- **Three-Bolt armor:** the helmet and suit together supply oxygen underwater. The full set protects against freezing in the waters of the Ice Maze.

## Epic Fight

The Stun Armor and Weight columns are Epic Fight stats. In Epic Fight's words, "Weight shortens stun, raises skill stamina use, and slows attack speed, especially on fast items", and "Stun Armor extends the time between stuns".
