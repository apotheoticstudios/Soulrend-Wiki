**Released:** Sep 8, 2025

About 8% faster load times and fewer leveling mobs.

## Gameplay Changes

- Stopped more mobs from gaining levels and restored leveling to a few bosses.
- Fixed missing textures in quests.

## Backend Changes

- Improved general performance, with about 8% faster load times and a boost to FPS in testing.
- Mostly fixed a fatal crash caused by the counterattack skill.
- Fixed various bugs and updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Excalibur (an Apotheosis compatibility addon by LowPolyCat), BadOptimizations, Sodium/Embeddium Extras, Ring of Repair, FTB Teams, Fallen Gems and Affixes

**Removed:** Fastload (abandoned and unnecessary), Chloride (Embeddium++) (replaced), Apotheosis Ascended (discontinued and replaced)
