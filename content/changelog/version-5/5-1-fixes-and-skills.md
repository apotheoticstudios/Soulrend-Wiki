**Released:** Aug 30, 2025

The skill tree was overhauled.

## Gameplay Changes

- Overhauled the skill tree.
- Stopped many passive mobs and bosses from leveling up automatically.
- Lowered how often Battle Tower structures spawn.

## Backend Changes

- Improved overall performance.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Structurify, Item Obliterator, Warrior Rage, Insanity Shaders, Dungeons and Combat X Iron's Spells

**Removed:** Item Banning (replaced), Hysteria Shaders (replaced), GroovyModLoader (caused crashes and was unnecessary), Chunk Activity Tracker (unnecessary), Faster Random (unnecessary), DragN's Livestock Overhaul (unnecessary, buggy, and hurt performance)
