**Released:** Sep 13, 2025

The mob level cap rose to 1000.

## Gameplay Changes

- Raised the mob level cap to 1000.
- Mobs no longer spawn with equipment that was removed.
- Fallen Legends boss quests now award Seal of the Underworld shards.

## Backend Changes

- Container checks are more aggressive to improve TPS.
- Fixed a text duplication bug with Explorer's and Nature's Compass.
- Updated mods and resource packs.

### Mods and resource packs

**Removed:** Skin Layers 3D (caused a bug with Epic Fight armor compatibility)
