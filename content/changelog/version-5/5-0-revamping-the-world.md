**Released:** Jul 15, 2025

Major worldgen changes, new mob animations and no more auto-leveling bosses.

## Gameplay Changes

- Major worldgen changes and improvements.
- Added Nether Remastered and End Remastered quests.
- Mobs received new animations.
- Bosses no longer auto-level, and many passive mobs no longer level up.
- Improved compatibility across many EFM-based mods.

## Backend Changes

- Greatly reduced TPS lag spikes.
- Fixed broken menu assets and auto-sizing.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Luminous: Beasts, Nature's Spirit, Embeddium, Chloride (Embeddium++), Climate Rivers, Animalium, F.M.R.P, Fresh Animations, Excalibur | Fresh Animations Patch

**Removed:** Tectonic (replaced), Aquamirae (unnecessary), Biome Makeover (unnecessary), Better Biome Reblend (unnecessary), Xenon (replaced), Better Chunk Loading (unnecessary), Explosive Enhancement (unnecessary), Epic Fight - Super Golem (caused incompatibilities and crashes)
