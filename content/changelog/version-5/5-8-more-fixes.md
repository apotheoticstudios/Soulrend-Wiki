**Released:** Sep 25, 2025

Bosses can level up, and Alex's Mobs stop equipping weapons.

## Gameplay Changes

- Let bosses level up, and stopped Bandits from leveling up.
- Stopped Alex's Mobs from equipping weapons, and stopped mobs from taking items from chests.
- Added loot tables and rewards to Life Of An Adventurer.
- Corrected the dodge chance bug again and strengthened Ring of Repair again.
- Adjusted several skills.
- Fixed missing sign textures.

## Backend Changes

- Updated mods.
