**Released:** Sep 16, 2025

Origins replaced the starter kit.

## Gameplay Changes

- Origins replaces the starter kit.
- Added an Apotheosis Enchanting quest chapter (credit: Odd_Turtle).
- Raised the skill point cap to 100.
- Capped evasion at 75%.
- Summoned mobs no longer level up.

## Backend Changes

- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** XP Tome, Origins, Medieval Origins Revival, Second Chance, Yung's Better Nether Fortress, Yung's Better Ocean Monuments, Cinderscapes

**Removed:** Enchantment Limiter (to be replaced later)
