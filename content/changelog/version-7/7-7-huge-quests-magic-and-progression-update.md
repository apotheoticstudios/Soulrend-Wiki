**Released:** Apr 5, 2026

About 200 new quests, three armor paths and Cryolite.

## Gameplay Changes

- About 200 new quests, including new questlines for Dungeons and Combat, The Watching Silence, The Sky Arena, Rise of the Champion and Simply Swords (Nesil).
- Added three armor paths: Red (warrior), Green (rogue) and Blue (battle mage).
- Simply Swords unique weapons are now craftable instead of lootable from chests.
- Ice Keys can no longer open chests and can be traded for Sky Arena rewards.
- Added 32 disabled Epic Fight skill book recipes, and changed quests in the Epic Fight and Magic Gear questlines.
- Buffed Cataclysm weapons, and added Cryolite, Molten Cryolite and a Cryolite shard dropped by the Frostmaw.
- Updated Iron's Spellbooks and its addons, which add new mobs, spells, bosses, items and textures.
- Reduced mob level increases per block slightly and capped Cataclysm boss levels at 250. Villagers now level up, faster than other mobs, and mobs that don't level have their level hidden.
- Waystone teleportation now costs a flat 1 XP level.
- The Deep Dark's Ancient Temple can be found with an Explorer's Compass.
- Increased the Ball Lightning spell cooldown from 2.5 to 3.5 seconds.
- Added many new items and recipes, and renamed some items.
- Fixed a Cataclysm boss duplication glitch, though Cataclysm dimension structures no longer reset. Fixed a bow animation bug.

## Backend Changes

- Improved performance in particle-heavy scenes.
- Disabled Quark's Shift Lock feature and removed several enchantments considered useless.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Attribute Modify, Mixin Extras NeoForge on Forge Fix, Astral Calamity Extras, Particle Core

**Removed:** Wizard Samurai (deprecated API support and render bugs)
