**Released:** Jan 5, 2026

Level raises for the Ender Dragon and Cataclysm bosses, and Sky Arena fixes.

## Gameplay Changes

- Ender Dragon and Cataclysm boss base levels raised to 250, and the Wither's to 150.
- Mobs no longer drop items in Sky Arenas, and Sky Arena mob spawns were adjusted with trolls removed.
- Fish no longer level up.
- Spiders now have legs, and a horse armor model bug was fixed.
- Simply Swords unique weapons no longer give unbalanced spell power.
- Epic Knights weapons no longer count as shields under Apotheosis.
- Added Simply Swords Epicfied movesets to translucent and warden weapons.
- Removed gun enchantments and changed the lock-on icon.

## Backend Changes

- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Removed:** Recipe Essentials (possible crash with Apoli)
