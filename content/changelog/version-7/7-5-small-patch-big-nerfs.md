**Released:** Feb 28, 2026

Nightfall 3.0 content and heavy nerfs to spell power gems.

## Gameplay Changes

- Added new weapons, movesets and features from Epic Fight - Nightfall 3.0.
- Heavily nerfed most spell power gems (20% to 80%), and many of them can now only be equipped in curios slots (e.g. Sunbird Gem, most Apotheotic Additions gems).
- XP toward skill points now resets on death.
- Bosses heal when players die during a fight.
- Fixed the max health value of the Perfect Brawlers gem, a broken Ender Chest model, and the Infernal Forge custom moveset.

## Backend Changes

- Fixed a server crash caused by Dragon Radar.
- Added Apothic Attributes Excalibur texture support (thanks to Anikiller4444).
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** AAA Particles, Luki's Woodland Mansions, Boss Ultimatum, Apothic Attributes Excalibur
