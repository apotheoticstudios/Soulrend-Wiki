**Released:** Feb 22, 2026

Boss respawns, Apotheosis tagging for Epic Knights weapons and the End update.

## Gameplay Changes

- All Cataclysm and naturally spawning bosses now respawn after 30 minutes.
- Apotheosis now properly tags all Epic Knights and Epic Knights: Ice and Fire weapons (430+) (thanks thecannuckleheadedviking).
- Apotheosis rarity tiers per dimension were rebalanced: more common in the Overworld, rarer in later dimensions. Apotheosis bosses no longer spawn in the Overworld.
- Removed netherite equipment from battle tower loot.
- Bonus experience per mob level increased by 15%. Nether mobs level 50% faster, Guard Villagers 30% faster, and Villagers level at 30% of regular mob speed.
- Heavily nerfed current HP damage (again), the Sepia Globe Eterna bonus (0.2), and the Ball Lightning spell (+20% mana cost, -20% spell power).
- Waystones should now cost XP (hopefully), and waystones now appear on the map.
- Changed the Hellshelf of Sight and Masterful Sight recipes.
- Reduced Spiral Village generation frequency.
- Improved the Epic Fight skills GUI.
- Fixed eyeless fresh animations mobs (thanks Anikiller1001), invisible hanging signs, and broken unique wandering trader llama textures.

## Backend Changes

- Fixed a Whisperwind weapon crash (thanks RobertOn).
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Anti Mob Farm, The Outer End, End Villager Outpost, Entity Respawner, Epic Fight - Better Skill Menu, Epic Fight - Skillbook Descriptions, EntityModelShaderFix, Xaero's Map x Waystones, Dragon Radars (thanks Zula)

**Removed:** Towers of the Wild Modded (duplicate structures), Xaero's Minimap & World Map - Waystones Compatibility (replaced)
