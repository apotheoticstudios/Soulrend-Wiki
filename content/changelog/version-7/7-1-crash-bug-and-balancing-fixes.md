**Released:** Jan 4, 2026

A fatal crash fix and Apotheosis affixes nerfed by about 25%.

## Gameplay Changes

- Nerfed Apotheosis affixes by about 25% and balanced various gem attributes.
- Increased reforging XP cost by about 30%.
- Disabled non-functional shield parrying from the Soulslike Weaponry mod.
- Added improved trails to Simply Swords weapons, and new movesets to many Soulslike Weaponry weapons.
- Added a crafting recipe for the Orb of Origin.
- Added a base level of 20 to Battle Tower bosses, and lowered Nether leveling per distance.
- Improved the third-person perspective and fixed an Epic Fight camera bug when climbing.
- Disabled cockatrice due to fatal crashes, and refined the mob equipment pool.

## Backend Changes

- Fixed a fatal crash involving Epic Fight and Shoulder Surfing.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Removed:** Shoulder Surfing - Pehkui Compat (unnecessary)
