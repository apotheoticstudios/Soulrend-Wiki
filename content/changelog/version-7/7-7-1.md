**Released:** Apr 6, 2026  
**CurseForge title:** Few Fixes

Duplicate recipe fixes and level caps for several bosses.

## Gameplay Changes

- Set a level cap of 60 for Frostmaw, Wroughtnaut, Umvuthi and Sunleia, and of 30 for Kamath and Grave Watcher.
- Removed a redundant questline.

## Backend Changes

- Fixed duplicate recipes and a bug with sign text rendering.
- Updated mods.
