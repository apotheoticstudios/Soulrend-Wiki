**Released:** Mar 3, 2026  
**CurseForge title:** Crash Fix and Adjustments

A crash fix for the ground slam attack.

## Gameplay Changes

- Lowered the sprint Z-offset of the third-person camera.
- Improved the Dragonslayer questline and added Dragon Radar quests.
- Improved weapon trails on many weapons.

## Backend Changes

- Fixed a crash when rendering the ground slam attack.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** EFN Enhance, Fancy Trail

**Removed:** Epic Fight - Impactful
