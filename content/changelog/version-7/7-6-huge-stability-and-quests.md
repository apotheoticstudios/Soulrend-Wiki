**Released:** Mar 15, 2026

Huge stability and quest additions, with about 230 new quests.

## Gameplay Changes

- About 230 new quests in total, including Geomancy, Sonomancy and Runic Weapons quests, new End quests, and a large Magic Gear and Epic Fight questline (Nesil).
- Changed the NightFall quest tab. Nearly all NightFall weapons now need a Nether Star to craft (9 new recipes).
- Changed the Duskfire and Ruinfighter armor recipes, and removed several broken or redundant recipes.
- Changed Tyrannical gem stats (current HP damage removed), and added new item drops from the Sunleia boss.
- Lowered max movement speed from 1024000% to 400%, and max flying speed from 1024000% to 1000%.
- Added Epic Fight mob support for Valarian Conquest (credit: Anikiller1001) and Epic Fight tooltip stats.
- Added new Retro Shaders and Excalibur support for Dragon Radars (thanks to Zula).
- Cataclysm Dimensions structures now reset after leaving.
- Fixed Dragon Radars progression in the Dragonslayer questline.
- Adjusted skill point ramp-up values (new worlds only).

## Backend Changes

- Performance improvements that reduce required RAM (thanks to Anikiller).
- Fixed log spam and a potential crash in the sky color caching optimization.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Icterine, Gnetum, Fast NBT, Enhanced Block Entities, Flerovium, Not Enough Recipe Book (NERB)

**Removed:** Let Me Despawn (unnecessary), Redirected (unnecessary), Cerulean (replaced), Vanillin (replaced)
