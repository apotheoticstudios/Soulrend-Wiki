**Released:** Jul 27, 2026  
**CurseForge title:** Emergency Patch

An emergency patch for mob combat AI and progression.

## Gameplay Changes

- Improved mob combat AI for better guarding, dodging and fleeing.
- Skill tree, progression and quest fixes.
- Keres and Ungoliant origin tweaks and fixes.

## Backend Changes

- Removed the max_health_damage affix again, and updated Simply Swords Epicfied and its dependencies.
- Updated mods.
