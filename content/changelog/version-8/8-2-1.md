**Released:** Jun 18, 2026  
**CurseForge title:** Emergency Progression Fix

Restored the old quest chapters.

## Gameplay Changes

- Restored the old quests (chapters), and renovated Act 3 - Deeper and Darker (optional act).
- Buffed all armors from the Rise of The Champion chapter: Red Path has more armor, and Dark Metal Armor now gives attack damage; Green Path swapped Attack Speed for Crit Damage; Blue Path has more armor.
- Nerfed the Warlord and Tyrannical gems (Crit Damage).
- Added a Dungeons and Combat quest chapter, and a new scythe in the Simply Nightfall chapter.
- Expanded the overworld chapter with extra quests again.
- Removed Alshanex Familiars and Echo's Trinkets from loot chests, and removed Agony and Torment from loot chests, so they are now craftable.

## Backend Changes

- Updated mods.
