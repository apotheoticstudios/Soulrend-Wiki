**Released:** Apr 1, 2025

A beta overhaul with a custom loading screen and title menu.

## Gameplay Changes

- Added a custom loading screen and title menu.
- Reduced the campfire heal radius and duration.
- Removed the stamina system from the paraglider.
- Decreased maggot health.
- Fixed occasional startup crashes.

## Backend Changes

- Fixed EMI overlapping the quest book in the inventory menu.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Extra Bounties, Quark Delight, Cherished Worlds, Better Clouds Reforged, Demi's Sky Arena, The Ink Arena, The Endergetic Expansion, Immersive Optimisation, Pet Cemetery, Realm RPG: Fallen Adventurers, Realm RPG: Quests and Rewards, DarkSmelting, The Orcs!, Nyf's Spiders, Dave's Potioneering, Al's Spiders Revamped

**Removed:** Stellarity (caused duplication bugs with Quark), DoesPotatoTick (replaced), Armor of the Ages (unnecessary), Fusion, Iron's RPG Tweaks (too many config bugs)
