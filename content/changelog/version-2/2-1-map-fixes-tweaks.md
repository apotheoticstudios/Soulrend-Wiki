**Released:** Apr 3, 2025

Added a playable 1:1 scale map of Skyrim.

## Gameplay Changes

- Added a large, 1:1 scale map of Skyrim that can be played in-game.
- Mobs now spawn with a variety of weapons and armor.
- Disabled Krampus and Krampus Henchman spawning.

## Backend Changes

- Fixed the Runic Grimoire Disabler, which wasn't working.
- Updated several mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Medieval Paintings, Improved Mobs, KubeJS Iron's Spells

**Removed:** Excalibur | Aether Support (redundant), Excalibur | Alloy Forgery Support (redundant), Dark Paintings (replaced), Blueprint Texture Issue Fix (redundant), Realm RPG: Quests and Rewards (unnecessary)
