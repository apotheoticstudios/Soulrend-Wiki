**Released:** Nov 25, 2025

Mobs level faster, with more health and less armor.

## Gameplay Changes

- Rebalanced mob levelling: mobs level faster, have more health, and less armor.
- Gave Soulslike Weaponry bosses base levels.
- Added a custom moveset for Ice and Fire longswords.
- Adjusted the mob equipment pool.
- Fixed a long delay when completing quests.

## Backend Changes

- Fixed a recipe conflict between the Cryptic Eye and Apotheosis enchanting (credit: Muonr).
- Improved general performance and updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Removed:** Monster Expansion (caused mob render crashes and had incompatible armor models), EFM Mod Weapon Compatibilities (migrated to KubeJS data), FTB Quests Optimizer (negligible gains and possibly causing issues)
