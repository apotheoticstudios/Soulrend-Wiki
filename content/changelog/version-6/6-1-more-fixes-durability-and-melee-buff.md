**Released:** Oct 15, 2025

Items became much easier to repair, and bosses can level past their base level.

## Gameplay Changes

- Made items much easier to repair, and added an enchantment that makes items unbreakable.
- Allowed boss mobs to level past their base level.
- Prevented mobs from breaking blocks.
- Buffed various melee skill nodes and replaced Vampiric Magic with Vampiric Blades.
- Replaced gem-related skills from ores and removed PST gems from loot tables.
- Fixed the invisible Yeti boss.

## Backend Changes

- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** NaNny Reforked, Unbreakable Enchantment, Repair Workbench: Durability System

**Removed:** Ring of Repair (replaced, unnecessary), Cataclysm: Spellbooks (caused fatal model bugs)
