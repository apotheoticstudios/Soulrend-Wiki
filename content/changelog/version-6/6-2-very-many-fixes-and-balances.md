**Released:** Oct 26, 2025

Mob health and damage per level were rebalanced, and waystones gained an XP cost.

## Gameplay Changes

- Mob health +20% and damage -20% per mob level.
- Waystones cost 1 XP level per 500 blocks, and the warp stone cooldown is 5 minutes. Waystones from worldgen are unbreakable.
- Base mob levels: Nether 75, End 150, Otherside 150. Ignis, Netherite Monstrocity, Ancient Remnant, Leviathan, Maledictus, Ender Guardian and Harbinger have base level 200. Warden has base level 50. Non-boss mobs in boss dimensions have base level 50.
- Summoned Kamath no longer auto-levels.
- Added Ignited Revenants to the Burning Arena dimension.
- The Sky Arena no longer sets time to night, and its protection radius was fixed to prevent farming.
- Fixed the Scylla damage cap, and a few mobs no longer spawn with equipment or bugged armor.
- Disabled Quark Pickarangs and Ancient Tomes for balance.
- Nerfed the Root, Heart Stop, Oak Skin, Spectral Hammer and Magic Missile spells, and the Magic attribute's mana bonus and cooldown reduction.

## Backend Changes

- Adjusted several structure set spacing and spawn conditions.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Cataclysm - Amethyst Crab Temple, Dynamic FPS

**Removed:** Campfire Spawn and Tweaks (unnecessary), Animalium (unnecessary), Epic Etherium Fixes (caused mid-air crashes), FPS Reducer (replaced), Memory Usage Screen (unnecessary)
