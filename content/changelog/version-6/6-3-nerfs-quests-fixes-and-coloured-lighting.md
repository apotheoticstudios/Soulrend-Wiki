**Released:** Nov 13, 2025

Nerfs, coloured lighting in shaders and about 20% more FPS in testing.

## Gameplay Changes

- Nether Crystal drops from Wardens (30% chance), and the Seal of the Underworld is no longer consumed on use.
- Spell amplification nerfed by 30%, and current health damage % gems nerfed.
- Cataclysm boss level raised to 300, and boss minions to 100.
- Added a Backpack quest chapter (credit: Odd_Turtle), modified several quest rewards and fixed several broken ones.
- Removed some small useless structures, and adjusted structure weights and biome spawns.
- Renamed many items to differentiate between mods.
- Added coloured lighting to shaders, and made small adjustments to all shader packs.
- Fixed a Blessed Amulet crash.

## Backend Changes

- General performance improvements (about 20% FPS gain in testing).
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Origins (Fabric), Ixeris, Colorwheel, Vanillin, Shoulder Surfing - Pehkui Compat, Iris/Oculus Shader Folder, Epic Fight Bow Tweak

**Removed:** Iris/Oculus & Geckolib Compat (redundant), Origins (Forge) (replaced with the official version), Enhanced Block Entities (replaced), Noisium (archived by its author)
