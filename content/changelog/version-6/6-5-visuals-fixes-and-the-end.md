**Released:** Dec 12, 2025

New End content, Skyrim music and more skill points.

## Gameplay Changes

- New biomes, mobs and other End improvements.
- Max skill points raised to 300, with custom level-up costs.
- Spell power buffed slightly (about 15%).
- Added the Dragonbane, Supernova, Mehrunes' Razor, Tonitrus and Bloodlust weapons.
- Added custom Skyrim music to the menu and game ambience.
- Changed the Bottle O'Lightning recipe, and improved the Explorer's Compass.
- Visual tooltip improvements, and adjusted default shaders (Insanity).
- Fixed Waystones not spawning in Nature's Spirit Villages, the Apotheosis AOE mining affix crash, and Quark mobs with bugged equipment.

## Backend Changes

- General performance improvements, and an updated ModernFix structure cache.
- Added Excalibur texture support to a few mods.
- Fixed antivirus false positives that sometimes blocked downloads.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Explorer's Compass Edited, Endless Biomes, Small End Features, Ixeris, Chef's Delight, Epic Fight NBT Integration, TooFast

**Removed:** Explorer's Compass (replaced), The Endergetic Expansion (caused frequent crashes)
