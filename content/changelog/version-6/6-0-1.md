**Released:** Oct 13, 2025  
**CurseForge title:** Quick Fixes and Skill Tree

Quick fixes and a weaker skill tree.

## Gameplay Changes

- Significantly weakened many skill tree options (credit: Traylon).
- Fixed equipment so armor no longer loses durability.
- The Lightning Bottle now has a craftable recipe.
- Restored the Traveler's Backpack's previous GUI.
- Corrected the reward tables for Magic Academy quests.

## Backend Changes

- Updated several mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Removed:** Neruina (excessive issues)
