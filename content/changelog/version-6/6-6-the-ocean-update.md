**Released:** Dec 15, 2025

Deeper oceans with new ocean structures, and familiars for Iron's Spells. The notes warn of terrain shifts where old underwater chunks meet newly generated ones.

## Gameplay Changes

- Made oceans deeper and added new ocean structures and islands.
- Added familiars (companions) for Iron's Spells and Spellbooks.
- Added 20+ new Create recipes.
- Increased the enchanting table's detection range for bookshelves and enchanting blocks.
- Weapons in hotbar slots 1, 2 and 3, plus shields, now appear on the player model.
- Added new 3D item models for Mjolnir and the Leviathan Axe.
- Corrected katana weapon tagging, and changed infested deepslate and stone textures so they no longer show fake diamond or iron ores.
- Improved visual tooltips, and adjusted shaders for better underwater gameplay.

## Backend Changes

- Made general performance improvements.
- Fixed resource pack load order, and a spruce leaves visual bug.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Realm RPG: Sea Dwellers, Underwater Village - Oceanic Structures, Deeper Oceans, Unnamed Sea, Create: Mechanical Extruder, GPUTape, Bookshelf Extended Enchanting Reach, Alshanex's Familiars, YDM's Weapon Master, Chloride (Embeddium++)

**Removed:** Epic Fight NBT Integration, Sodium/Embeddium Extras (replaced)
