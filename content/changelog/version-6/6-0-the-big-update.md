**Released:** Oct 12, 2025

The Big Update, with Magic Academy and boss dimension quest chapters and heavy Apotheosis nerfs.

## Gameplay Changes

- Heavy nerfs to Apotheosis gems, affixes and enchanting.
- Added custom leveling for different dimensions and bosses (credit: Traylon), and more mobs on the leveling blacklist.
- Added a Magic Academy quest chapter (credit: Odd_Turtle), and an Aquamirae and Cataclysmic Dimensions chapter for the new boss dimensions.
- Added a crafting recipe for the Lighting Bottle.
- Revamped the third-person perspective and added better custom menus.
- Equipment no longer loses armor with durability.
- Various nerfs to the Blacksmith and Enchanter skill trees, and removed recipe skill dependencies.
- Increased spawner spawn amount and XP Tome storage amount.
- Removed various weapons that mobs can spawn with, and buffed Wizard Samurai gear.
- Fixed neutral mobs becoming randomly aggressive, the Paraglider animation, and Iron Chests barrels not being mineable.

## Backend Changes

- Added EFM compatibility to Cataclysm: Spellbooks, GTBC's Geomancy Plus, Bosses'Rise and Monster Expansion.
- Fixed various quest bugs and updated mods. The notes add "And much more..." without itemizing it.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Storage Drawers, Cataclysm Dimensions, Monster Expansion, Aquamirae, Bosses'Rise, Medieval Buildings [Nether Edition], Overflowing Bars, Modern World Creation, Epic Etherium Fixes, Cataclysm: Spellbooks, Ancient Reforging, Esoteric Reforging, Shoulder Surfing Reloaded, Lios Spiral Tower Villages, GTBC's Geomancy Plus - Iron's Spells Addon, Epic Fight - Better Lock On, Abridged, FastEvent

**Removed:** Advanced Compass (unnecessary, caused crashes), Leawind's Third Person (replaced), Disable Structures (redundant)
