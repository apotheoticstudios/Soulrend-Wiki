**Released:** Mar 29, 2025

Replaced the Aether with Twilight Forest.

## Gameplay Changes

- Removed every Aether mod and addon, and Waystones: Aether Villages.
- Added Twilight Forest, with Twilight Forest Delight and Twilight Forest: Lost Blocks.
- Turned off Ice and Fire's Mausoleum feature.

## Backend Changes

- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Twilight Forest, Twilight Forest Delight, Twilight Forest: Lost Blocks, Just Zoom

**Removed:** All Aether mods and addons, Epic Fight: Knuckles Extended (unnecessary), Waystones: Aether Villages
