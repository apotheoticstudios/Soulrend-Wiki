**Released:** May 27, 2025

Polish, Dragonslayer quests and about 20% faster launch.

## Gameplay Changes

- Mobs no longer occasionally spawn with a mount.
- Removed aptitude requirements for items.
- Added more prologue quests and the Dragonslayer quests, and adjusted several quests.
- Increased mountain scales.
- Added more shader support.

## Backend Changes

- Disabled the Apotheosis potion and garden modules.
- Further tick time improvements; launch time improved by about 20%.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Immersive Optimisation, Dynamic Crosshair, Particular Reforged, Better Clouds Reforged, Biome Music, Better Biome Reblend

**Removed:** Does It Tick? (replaced), Terralith, Twilight Forest (unnecessary), Spartan Weaponry - Twilight Forest, Twilight Treehouses, Twilight's Flavors and Delight, Simply More (incompatible with Epic Fight, unnecessary), Spartan Weaponry + adding (incompatible with Epic Fight, unnecessary), Epic Fight - Wukong Moveset (unnecessary), Cataclysm Apotheosis Addon (unnecessary; contained AI assets)
