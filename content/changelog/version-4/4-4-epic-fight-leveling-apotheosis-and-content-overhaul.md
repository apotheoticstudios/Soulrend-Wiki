**Released:** Jun 4, 2025

Epic Fight support for more mods, Auto Leveling and a remade progressive difficulty system.

## Gameplay Changes

- Remade the progressive difficulty system.
- Made weapon affix chances scale per level.
- Remade the loading screen and added a custom cursor.
- Restored the default sheep and pig models.
- Added the remaining Simply Swords quests, and Nether structure quests (based on a Discord poll).
- Added Epic Fight support for Mowzie's Mobs, Ice and Fire gear, Valarian Conquest, Graveyard and more.
- Fixed Target Dummy giving skill points.

## Backend Changes

- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Auto Leveling, Otherworld - Apotheosis, Quest Kill Task Tweaks, Excalibur | Advancement Plaques, Integrated Stronghold (plus addons), Nether Remastered, Yung's Better End Island, Nullscape, Better End Cities Vanilla, Enhanced Celestials

**Removed:** Majrusz's Progressive Difficulty (replaced), Embellished Stone (replaced), AsteorBar (replaced), Explorer's Compass (unnecessary), Yung's Stronghold (replaced), Remove Reloading Screen (caused bugs), Shifu - Craftable Epic Fight Skill Books, Souls-Like Universe (caused bugs, unnecessary, outdated models)
