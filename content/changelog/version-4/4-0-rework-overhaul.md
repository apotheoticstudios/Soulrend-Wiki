**Released:** May 17, 2025

A custom leveling system and skill tree replaced the old progression.

## Gameplay Changes

- Added a custom leveling system and skill tree, and made skill points harder to obtain.
- Added new introduction quests and descriptions for backgrounds.
- Mobs no longer drop Apotheotic gems, Apotheotic bosses no longer spawn, and the Apotheosis spawner module is disabled.
- Removed the bleeding mechanic and disabled creeper spawns for theming.
- Fixed a bug that prevented using weapon abilities.

## Backend Changes

- Added Excalibur compatibility with Simply Swords and Fallen Adventurers.
- Blacklisted many useless or conflicting items.
- Tweaked some particle effects and reworked the loading bar again.
- Updated mods.

### Mods and resource packs

**Added:** Souls-Like Universe, Dungeons and Combat, Panda's Falling Tree, Clean Swing through Grass, You Shall Not Spawn!, MOTP - RPG Player Leveling System, Incendium, DragN's Livestock Overhaul, Structory, Epic Fight - Super Golem, Guard Epic

**Removed:** Do a Barrel Roll (unnecessary), HT's Treechop (replaced), Medieval Buildings (bloat), Explorify - Dungeons and Structures (unnecessary), The Ink Arena (unnecessary), Born in Chaos (unnecessary), Born in Configuration (redundant), Animation Overhaul (redundant), Progressive Difficulty - Chaos Integration (redundant), Sky Villages (unnecessary), Legendary Monsters (unnecessary), Weapons of Legendary Monsters (redundant), Jaden's Nether Expansion (replaced)
