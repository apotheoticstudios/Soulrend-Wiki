**Released:** May 20, 2025

A new attribute and leveling system, plus terrain and performance work.

## Gameplay Changes

- Replaced and overhauled the attribute and leveling system.
- Added a Wisdom attribute that increases max mana.
- Removed the level requirement for using weapons.
- Added a new Noble starter kit.
- Blacklisted Bandits from spawning.

## Backend Changes

- Improved tick rates and cleaned up the config.
- Blacklisted more items.
- Made resource packs visible on CurseForge.
- Updated mods.
