**Defense** is one of the three branches of the [[skill-trees/mage/druid|Druid]] school node in the Mage tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Sentience". It takes 7 points from the Druid node to reach its hub, which gives +2.5% Spell Resistance per 25% Nature Spell Power. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Spell Resistance and Nature Spell Power.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-nt_defense-nature"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Druid node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×6 | 1–6 | +1% Spell Resistance per 100% Nature Spell Power (each) |
| **Hub** | 7 | +2.5% Spell Resistance per 25% Nature Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +10% Nature Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +15% Nature Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +20% Nature Spell Power |

## Totals

Taking all 10 nodes (10 points) gives:

- +2.5% Spell Resistance per 25% Nature Spell Power
- +45% Nature Spell Power
- +6% Spell Resistance per 100% Nature Spell Power
