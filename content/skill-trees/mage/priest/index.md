**Priest** is the Holy school node of the [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]. It connects directly to the Mage root and costs 1 point. A player can take at most two of the twelve school nodes. Taking it opens three branches and three keystones.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-holy, mage-nt_defense-holy, mage-ks_defense-holy, mage-ks_offense-holy, mage-ks_utility-holy, mage-nt_offense-holy, mage-nt_utility-holy"}}

## School node

The Priest node gives:

- +100% Holy Spell Power

## Branches

Each branch is a path of six Minor nodes that ends in a hub (titled "Sentience" in game) with three leaves.

| Branch | Points to hub | Hub effect | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[skill-trees/mage/priest/defence\|Defense]] | 7 | +2.5% Holy Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/priest/offence\|Offense]] | 3 | +2.5% Holy Spell Power (total) | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/priest/utility\|Utility]] | 7 | +2.5% Holy Spell Power | 10 |

## Keystones

The Priest node connects to three keystones, of which a player can take one. They need a picked-up [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]]. See [[skill-trees/mage/priest/keystones|Mage – Priest – Keystones]].
