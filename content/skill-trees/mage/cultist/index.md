**Cultist** is the Blood school node of the [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]. It connects directly to the Mage root and costs 1 point. A player can take at most two of the twelve school nodes. Taking it opens three branches and three keystones.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-blood, mage-nt_defense-blood, mage-ks_defense-blood, mage-ks_offense-blood, mage-ks_utility-blood, mage-nt_offense-blood, mage-nt_utility-blood"}}

## School node

The Cultist node gives:

- +100% Blood Spell Power

## Branches

Each branch is a path of six Minor nodes that ends in a hub (titled "Sentience" in game) with three leaves.

| Branch | Points to hub | Hub effect | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[skill-trees/mage/cultist/defence\|Defense]] | 7 | +1% Spell Resistance per 25% Blood Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/cultist/offence\|Offense]] | 7 | +15% Summon Damage per 25% Blood Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/cultist/utility\|Utility]] | 7 | +15% Blood Spell Power | 10 |

## Keystones

The Cultist node connects to three keystones, of which a player can take one. They need a picked-up [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]]. See [[skill-trees/mage/cultist/keystones|Mage – Cultist – Keystones]].
