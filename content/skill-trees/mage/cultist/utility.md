**Utility** is one of the three branches of the [[skill-trees/mage/cultist|Cultist]] school node in the Mage tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Sentience". It takes 7 points from the Cultist node to reach its hub, which gives +15% Blood Spell Power. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Blood Spell Power and Mana Regeneration.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-nt_utility-blood"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Cultist node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×6 | 1–6 | +0.17% Mana Regeneration per 100% Blood Spell Power (each) |
| **Hub** | 7 | +15% Blood Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +20% Blood Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +20% Blood Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +15% Blood Spell Power |

## Totals

Taking all 10 nodes (10 points) gives:

- +70% Blood Spell Power
- +1.02% Mana Regeneration per 100% Blood Spell Power
