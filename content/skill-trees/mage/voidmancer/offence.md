**Offense** is one of the three branches of the [[skill-trees/mage/voidmancer|Voidmancer]] school node in the Mage tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Sentience". It takes 7 points from the Voidmancer node to reach its hub, which gives +5% Ender Spell Power. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Ender Spell Power, Attack Damage and Max Mana.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-nt_offense-ender"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Voidmancer node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×6 | 1–6 | +1% Max Mana per 100% Ender Spell Power (each) |
| **Hub** | 7 | +5% Ender Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +15% Ender Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +10% Attack Damage per 100% Ender Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +20% Ender Spell Power |

## Totals

Taking all 10 nodes (10 points) gives:

- +40% Ender Spell Power
- +10% Attack Damage per 100% Ender Spell Power
- +6% Max Mana per 100% Ender Spell Power
