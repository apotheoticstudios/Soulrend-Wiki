The **Mage Skill Tree** is the spellcasting tree. Its core, the Mind, raises mana, spell power and cooldown reduction, and twelve school nodes each specialize in one spell school. It has 493 nodes. Every node costs 1 skill point.

{{skilltree mage height=520}}

## Layout

- **Root:** the Mage node, the tree's starting point. See [[skill-trees/mage/mind|Mage – Mind]].
- **Mind:** six spokes of Minor nodes, each ending in a hub with four leaves.
- **Mind keystones:** six keystones on the root; take up to two. Each needs a picked-up [[item:nether_remastered:seal_crystal]].
- **School nodes:** twelve nodes on the root, one per spell school; take up to two. Each has three branches and three keystones (take one), which need a picked-up [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]].
