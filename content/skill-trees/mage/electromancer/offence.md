**Offense** is one of the three branches of the [[skill-trees/mage/electromancer|Electromancer]] school node in the Mage tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Sentience". It takes 7 points from the Electromancer node to reach its hub, which gives +5% Lightning Spell Power. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Lightning Spell Power, Lightning School Level and Max Mana.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-nt_offense-lightning"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Electromancer node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×6 | 1–6 | +1% Max Mana per 100% Lightning Spell Power (each) |
| **Hub** | 7 | +5% Lightning Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +1 Lightning School Level |
| Leaf | 8 | +10% Lightning Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +20% Lightning Spell Power |

## Totals

Taking all 10 nodes (10 points) gives:

- +35% Lightning Spell Power
- +1 Lightning School Level
- +6% Max Mana per 100% Lightning Spell Power
