**Electromancer** is the Lightning school node of the [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]. It connects directly to the Mage root and costs 1 point. A player can take at most two of the twelve school nodes. Taking it opens three branches and three keystones.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-lightning, mage-nt_defense-lightning, mage-ks_defense-lightning, mage-ks_offense-lightning, mage-ks_utility-lightning, mage-nt_offense-lightning, mage-nt_utility-lightning"}}

## School node

The Electromancer node gives:

- +100% Lightning Spell Power

## Branches

Each branch is a path of six Minor nodes that ends in a hub (titled "Sentience" in game) with three leaves.

| Branch | Points to hub | Hub effect | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[skill-trees/mage/electromancer/defence\|Defense]] | 7 | +5% Lightning Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/electromancer/offence\|Offense]] | 7 | +5% Lightning Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/electromancer/utility\|Utility]] | 7 | +5% Lightning Spell Power | 10 |

## Keystones

The Electromancer node connects to three keystones, of which a player can take one. They need a picked-up [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]]. See [[skill-trees/mage/electromancer/keystones|Mage – Electromancer – Keystones]].
