**Defense** is one of the three branches of the [[skill-trees/mage/aquamancer|Aquamancer]] school node in the Mage tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Sentience". It takes 7 points from the Aquamancer node to reach its hub, which gives +25% Aqua Magic Resistance. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Aqua Magic Resistance, Aqua Spell Power and Spell Resistance.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-nt_defense-aqua"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Aquamancer node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×6 | 1–6 | +1% Spell Resistance per 100% Aqua Spell Power (each) |
| **Hub** | 7 | +25% Aqua Magic Resistance |
| Leaf | 8 | +10% Aqua Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +15% Aqua Spell Power |
| Leaf | 8 | +20% Aqua Spell Power |

## Totals

Taking all 10 nodes (10 points) gives:

- +25% Aqua Magic Resistance
- +45% Aqua Spell Power
- +6% Spell Resistance per 100% Aqua Spell Power
