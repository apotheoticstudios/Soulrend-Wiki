**Aquamancer** is the Aqua school node of the [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]. It connects directly to the Mage root and costs 1 point. A player can take at most two of the twelve school nodes. Taking it opens three branches and three keystones.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-aqua, mage-nt_defense-aqua, mage-ks_defense-aqua, mage-ks_offense-aqua, mage-ks_utility-aqua, mage-nt_offense-aqua, mage-nt_utility-aqua"}}

## School node

The Aquamancer node gives:

- +100% Aqua Spell Power

## Branches

Each branch is a path of six Minor nodes that ends in a hub (titled "Sentience" in game) with three leaves.

| Branch | Points to hub | Hub effect | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[skill-trees/mage/aquamancer/defence\|Defense]] | 7 | +25% Aqua Magic Resistance | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/aquamancer/offence\|Offense]] | 7 | +5% Aqua Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/aquamancer/utility\|Utility]] | 7 | +5% Aqua Spell Power | 10 |

## Keystones

The Aquamancer node connects to three keystones, of which a player can take one. They need a picked-up [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]]. See [[skill-trees/mage/aquamancer/keystones|Mage – Aquamancer – Keystones]].
