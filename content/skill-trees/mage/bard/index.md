**Bard** is the Sound school node of the [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]. It connects directly to the Mage root and costs 1 point. A player can take at most two of the twelve school nodes. Taking it opens three branches and three keystones.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-sound, mage-nt_defense-sound, mage-ks_defense-sound, mage-ks_offense-sound, mage-ks_utility-sound, mage-nt_offense-sound, mage-nt_utility-sound"}}

## School node

The Bard node gives:

- +100% Sound Spell Power

## Branches

Each branch is a path of six Minor nodes that ends in a hub (titled "Sentience" in game) with three leaves.

| Branch | Points to hub | Hub effect | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[skill-trees/mage/bard/defence\|Defense]] | 7 | +5% Sound Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/bard/offence\|Offense]] | 7 | +5% Sound Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/bard/utility\|Utility]] | 7 | +5% Sound Spell Power | 10 |

## Keystones

The Bard node connects to three keystones, of which a player can take one. They need a picked-up [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]]. See [[skill-trees/mage/bard/keystones|Mage – Bard – Keystones]].
