The **Pyromancer school keystones** are three nodes, of which a player can take at most one. Each one can only be learned by a player who has picked up a [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]] at least once. Each keystone costs 1 point and connects directly to the Pyromancer node.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-ks_defense-fire, mage-ks_offense-fire, mage-ks_utility-fire"}}

## Keystones

| Effect |
| --- |
| +1% Spell Resistance per 25% Fire Spell Power |
| +1% Max Mana per 25% Fire Spell Power |
| +1 Fire School Level |
