**Pyromancer** is the Fire school node of the [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]. It connects directly to the Mage root and costs 1 point. A player can take at most two of the twelve school nodes. Taking it opens three branches and three keystones.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-fire, mage-nt_defense-fire, mage-ks_defense-fire, mage-ks_offense-fire, mage-ks_utility-fire, mage-nt_offense-fire, mage-nt_utility-fire"}}

## School node

The Pyromancer node gives:

- +100% Fire Spell Power

## Branches

Each branch is a path of six Minor nodes that ends in a hub (titled "Sentience" in game) with three leaves.

| Branch | Points to hub | Hub effect | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[skill-trees/mage/pyromancer/defence\|Defense]] | 7 | +5% Fire Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/pyromancer/offence\|Offense]] | 7 | +5% Fire Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/pyromancer/utility\|Utility]] | 7 | +5% Fire Spell Power | 10 |

## Keystones

The Pyromancer node connects to three keystones, of which a player can take one. They need a picked-up [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]]. See [[skill-trees/mage/pyromancer/keystones|Mage – Pyromancer – Keystones]].
