**Max Mana** is one of the six spokes of the [[skill-trees/mage/mind|Mind]], the core of the Mage tree. It takes 9 points from the Mage node to reach its hub, which gives +15% Max Mana. Four leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Max Mana.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-mm"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Mage node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×8 | 1–8 | +15 Max Mana (each) |
| **Hub** | 9 | +15% Max Mana |
| Leaf | 10 | +50 Max Mana |
| Leaf | 10 | +50 Max Mana |
| Leaf | 10 | +50 Max Mana |
| Leaf | 10 | +25% Max Mana |

## Totals

Taking all 13 nodes (13 points) gives:

- +40% Max Mana
- +270 Max Mana
