The **Warpmancer school keystones** are three nodes, of which a player can take at most one. Each one can only be learned by a player who has picked up a [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]] at least once. Each keystone costs 1 point and connects directly to the Warpmancer node.

{{skilltree mage focus="mage-ks_defense-eldritch, mage-ks_offense-eldritch, mage-ks_utility-eldritch"}}

## Keystones

| Effect |
| --- |
| +1% Spell Resistance per 25% Eldritch Spell Power |
| +1% Max Mana per 25% Eldritch Spell Power |
| +2 Eldritch School Level |
