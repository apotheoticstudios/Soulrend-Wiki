**Warpmancer** is the Eldritch school node of the [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]. It connects directly to the Mage root and costs 1 point. A player can take at most two of the twelve school nodes. Taking it opens three branches and three keystones.

In game the node's title is obfuscated (drawn as scrambling characters); the underlying name is Warpmancer.

## School node

The Warpmancer node gives:

- +100% Eldritch Spell Power

## Branches

Each branch is a path of six Minor nodes that ends in a hub (titled "Sentience" in game) with three leaves.

| Branch | Points to hub | Hub effect | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[skill-trees/mage/warpmancer/defence\|Defense]] | 7 | +5% Eldritch Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/warpmancer/offence\|Offense]] | 7 | +5% Eldritch Spell Power | 10 |
| [[skill-trees/mage/warpmancer/utility\|Utility]] | 7 | +1% Cooldown Reduction per 100% Eldritch Spell Power | 10 |

## Keystones

The Warpmancer node connects to three keystones, of which a player can take one. They need a picked-up [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]]. See [[skill-trees/mage/warpmancer/keystones|Mage – Warpmancer – Keystones]].
