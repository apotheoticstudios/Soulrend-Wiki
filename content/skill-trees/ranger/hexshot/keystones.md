The **Hexshot subclass keystones** are three nodes, of which a player can take at most two. Each one can only be learned by a player who has picked up a [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]] at least once. Each keystone costs 1 point and connects directly to the Hexshot node.

{{skilltree ranger focus="ranger_18, ranger_19, ranger_20"}}

## Keystones

| Effect |
| --- |
| +25% Speed |
| 10% chance to set the target on fire for 10 s on hit |
| +25% Draw Speed |
