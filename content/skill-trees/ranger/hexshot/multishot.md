**Multishot** is one of the four branches of the [[skill-trees/ranger/hexshot|Hexshot]] subclass of the Ranger tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Notable". It takes 6 points from the Hexshot node to reach its hub, which gives +2% Draw Speed per enchantment on your weapon. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Draw Speed and extra projectiles.

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Hexshot node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×5 | 1–5 | +2% Draw Speed (each) |
| **Hub** | 6 | +2% Draw Speed per enchantment on your weapon |
| Leaf | 7 | 10% chance to fire an additional projectile with a ranged weapon equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | 10% chance to fire an additional projectile with a ranged weapon equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | 10% chance to fire an additional projectile with a ranged weapon equipped |

## Totals

Taking all 9 nodes (9 points) gives:

- +2% Draw Speed per enchantment on your weapon
- +10% Draw Speed
- 30% chance to fire an additional projectile with a ranged weapon equipped
