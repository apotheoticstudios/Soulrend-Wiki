**Piercing** is one of the four branches of the [[skill-trees/ranger/sharpshooter|Sharpshooter]] subclass of the Ranger tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Notable". It takes 6 points from the Sharpshooter node to reach its hub, which gives +20% Armor Pierce with a ranged weapon in hand; ×1.2 Armor Shred with a ranged weapon in hand. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Armor Pierce, Armor Shred, damage avoidance, spells, Max Mana and Armor.

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Sharpshooter node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path | 1 | +3% Armor Pierce; ×1.03 Armor Shred; +1% Armor Shred; +0.01 Armor Pierce |
| Path ×4 | 2–5 | +3% Armor Pierce; ×1.03 Armor Shred (each) |
| **Hub** | 6 | +20% Armor Pierce with a ranged weapon in hand; ×1.2 Armor Shred with a ranged weapon in hand |
| Leaf | 7 | 10% chance to avoid damage with a ranged weapon equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | Grants the Gust spell (level 5); +200 Max Mana with a ranged weapon equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | ×1.4 Armor Shred with a ranged weapon equipped; +40% Armor with a ranged weapon equipped |

## Totals

Taking all 9 nodes (9 points) gives:

- +20% Armor Pierce with a ranged weapon in hand
- ×1.2 Armor Shred with a ranged weapon in hand
- 10% chance to avoid damage with a ranged weapon equipped
- Grants the Gust spell (level 5)
- +200 Max Mana with a ranged weapon equipped
- ×1.4 Armor Shred with a ranged weapon equipped
- +40% Armor with a ranged weapon equipped
- +15% Armor Pierce
- ×1.15 Armor Shred
- +1% Armor Shred
- +0.01 Armor Pierce
