The **Sharpshooter subclass keystones** are three nodes, of which a player can take at most two. Each one can only be learned by a player who has picked up a [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]] at least once. Each keystone costs 1 point and connects directly to the Sharpshooter node.

{{skilltree ranger focus="ranger_15, ranger_16, ranger_17"}}

## Keystones

| Effect |
| --- |
| ×1.2 Crit Chance with a ranged weapon in hand |
| ×1.2 Crit Damage with a ranged weapon in hand |
| +25% Arrow Damage |
