**Sharpshooter** is one of the two subclasses of the [[skill-trees/ranger|Ranger skill tree]]. The subclass node connects directly to the Ranger root, costs 1 point, and a player can take only one Ranger subclass. Taking it opens four branches and three keystones.

{{skilltree ranger focus="ranger_9, ranger_77, ranger_15, ranger_16, ranger_17, ranger_78, ranger_76, ranger_75"}}

## Subclass node

The Sharpshooter node gives:

- +30% Arrow Velocity
- −40% Draw Speed
- Gain Night Vision for 1.5 s every 1 s, while crouching

## Branches

| Branch | Points to hub | Hub effect | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[skill-trees/ranger/sharpshooter/velocity\|Velocity]] | 6 | +35% Arrow Velocity with a ranged weapon equipped | 9 |
| [[skill-trees/ranger/sharpshooter/precision\|Precision]] | 6 | +5% Arrow Damage per 0.5 blocks between you and the target, if the target is at most 10 blocks away | 9 |
| [[skill-trees/ranger/sharpshooter/critical\|Critical]] | 6 | ×1.15 Crit Chance with a ranged weapon equipped | 9 |
| [[skill-trees/ranger/sharpshooter/piercing\|Piercing]] | 6 | +20% Armor Pierce with a ranged weapon in hand; ×1.2 Armor Shred with a ranged weapon in hand | 9 |

## Keystones

The Sharpshooter node connects to three keystones, of which a player can take two. They need a picked-up [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]]. See [[skill-trees/ranger/sharpshooter/keystones|Ranger – Sharpshooter – Keystones]].
