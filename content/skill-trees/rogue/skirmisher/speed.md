**Speed** is one of the four branches of the [[skill-trees/rogue/skirmisher|Skirmisher]] subclass of the Rogue tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Notable". It takes 6 points from the Skirmisher node to reach its hub, which gives +20% Speed with light armor equipped. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Speed, Knockback Resistance, Offhand Attack Speed and Attack Speed.

{{skilltree rogue focus="rogue_103"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Skirmisher node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×5 | 1–5 | +10% Knockback Resistance (each) |
| **Hub** | 6 | +20% Speed with light armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | +30% Speed with light armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | +0.25 Offhand Attack Speed with light armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | +0.25 Attack Speed with light armor equipped |

## Totals

Taking all 9 nodes (9 points) gives:

- +50% Speed with light armor equipped
- +50% Knockback Resistance
- +0.25 Offhand Attack Speed with light armor equipped
- +0.25 Attack Speed with light armor equipped
