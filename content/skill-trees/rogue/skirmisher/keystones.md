The **Skirmisher subclass keystones** are three nodes, of which a player can take at most two. Each one can only be learned by a player who has picked up a [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]] at least once. Each keystone costs 1 point and connects directly to the Skirmisher node.

{{skilltree rogue focus="rogue_90, rogue_91, rogue_92"}}

## Keystones

| Effect |
| --- |
| ×1.3 Dodge Chance with light armor equipped |
| +50% Max Stamina with light armor equipped |
| 30% chance to inflict Posture Break for 10 s on hit, with light armor equipped |
