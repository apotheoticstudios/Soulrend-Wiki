**Armor Pierce** is one of the four branches of the [[skill-trees/rogue/skirmisher|Skirmisher]] subclass of the Rogue tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Notable". It takes 6 points from the Skirmisher node to reach its hub, which gives +25% Armor Pierce with light armor equipped. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Armor Pierce, Slowness and Step Height.

{{skilltree rogue focus="rogue_102"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Skirmisher node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path | 1 | +5% Armor Pierce; +0.1 Armor Pierce |
| Path ×4 | 2–5 | +5% Armor Pierce (each) |
| **Hub** | 6 | +25% Armor Pierce with light armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | 30% chance to inflict Slowness for 5 s (stacks up to 5 times) on melee hit, with light armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | +1 Step Height with light armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | 30% chance to inflict Slowness for 5 s (stacks up to 5 times) on melee hit, with light armor equipped |

## Totals

Taking all 9 nodes (9 points) gives:

- +25% Armor Pierce with light armor equipped
- +25% Armor Pierce
- +0.1 Armor Pierce
- 30% chance to inflict Slowness for 5 s (stacks up to 5 times) on melee hit, with light armor equipped (×2)
- +1 Step Height with light armor equipped
