**Armor Negation** is one of the six spokes of the [[skill-trees/rogue/core|Rogue core]]. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Notable". It takes 10 points from the Rogue node to reach its hub, which gives +6 Armor Negation. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Armor Negation.

{{skilltree rogue focus="rogue_2"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Rogue node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×9 | 1–9 | +1 Armor Negation (each) |
| **Hub** | 10 | +6 Armor Negation |
| Leaf | 11 | +20 Armor Negation |
| Leaf | 11 | +15 Armor Negation |
| Leaf | 11 | +10 Armor Negation |

## Totals

Taking all 13 nodes (13 points) gives:

- +60 Armor Negation
