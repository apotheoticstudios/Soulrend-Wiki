The **Rogue core keystones** are five nodes, of which a player can take at most three. Each one can only be learned by a player who has picked up a [[item:nether_remastered:seal_crystal]] at least once. Each keystone costs 1 point and connects directly to the Rogue node.

{{skilltree rogue focus="rogue_8, rogue_9, rogue_10, rogue_11, rogue_12"}}

## Keystones

| Effect |
| --- |
| ×1.4 Crit Chance while holding weapons in both hands |
| +3 Luck with light armor equipped |
| +30% Attack Damage while holding weapons in both hands |
| +20% Stamina Regen (total) with light armor equipped |
| +35% Armor Negation while holding weapons in both hands |
