**Critical** is one of the four branches of the [[skill-trees/rogue/assassin|Assassin]] subclass of the Rogue tree. The name is the wiki's; in game the hub is titled "Notable". It takes 6 points from the Assassin node to reach its hub, which gives ×1.25 Crit Chance while holding melee weapons in both hands. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Crit Chance, Weakness and Decay.

{{skilltree rogue focus="rogue_94"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Assassin node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×5 | 1–5 | ×1.04 Crit Chance (each) |
| **Hub** | 6 | ×1.25 Crit Chance while holding melee weapons in both hands |
| Leaf | 7 | ×1.3 Crit Chance while affected by Invisibility |
| Leaf | 7 | 20% chance to inflict Weakness for 10 s on hit, while affected by Invisibility |
| Leaf | 7 | 20% chance to inflict Decay II for 5 s on hit, while affected by Invisibility |

## Totals

Taking all 9 nodes (9 points) gives:

- ×1.25 Crit Chance while holding melee weapons in both hands
- ×1.3 Crit Chance while affected by Invisibility
- 20% chance to inflict Weakness for 10 s on hit, while affected by Invisibility
- 20% chance to inflict Decay II for 5 s on hit, while affected by Invisibility
- ×1.2 Crit Chance
