The **Warrior core** is the part of the [[skill-trees/warrior|Warrior skill tree]] that every Warrior build starts from: the root node, six spokes and the core keystones. The root is the tree's starting point and costs 1 point like every other node.

{{skilltree warrior focus="warrior, warrior_6, warrior_2, warrior_5, warrior_80, warrior_81, warrior_82, warrior_83, warrior_84, warrior_4, warrior_3, warrior_7"}}

## Root

The Warrior root gives:

- +5 Attack Damage
- +10 Max Health
- +0.5 Attack Speed if your Offhand Attack Speed is at most 1.5

## Spokes

Each spoke is a path of Minor nodes that ends in a hub; three leaves branch off each hub.

| Spoke | Points to hub | Hub effect | Nodes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[skill-trees/warrior/core/experienced\|Experienced]] | 10 | 10% chance to gain 5 experience points on hit; +20% experience from ores | 13 |
| [[skill-trees/warrior/core/steadfast\|Steadfast]] | 10 | ×1.1 Knockback Resistance (total) | 13 |
| [[skill-trees/warrior/core/offense\|Offense]] | 10 | +5% Attack Damage | 13 |
| [[skill-trees/warrior/core/healthy\|Healthy]] | 10 | +10 Max Health | 13 |
| [[skill-trees/warrior/core/defence\|Defence]] | 10 | +10 Armor | 13 |
| [[skill-trees/warrior/core/sturdy\|Sturdy]] | 10 | +5% Armor Toughness | 13 |

## Keystones

The root also connects to five keystones, of which a player can take three. They need a picked-up [[item:nether_remastered:seal_crystal]]. See [[skill-trees/warrior/core/keystones|Warrior – Core – Keystones]].
