The **Warrior core keystones** are five nodes, of which a player can take at most three. Each one can only be learned by a player who has picked up a [[item:nether_remastered:seal_crystal]] at least once. Each keystone costs 1 point and connects directly to the Warrior node.

{{skilltree warrior focus="warrior_80, warrior_81, warrior_82, warrior_83, warrior_84"}}

## Keystones

| Effect |
| --- |
| +25% Max Health |
| 10% chance to inflict Bleed for 30 s on hit |
| ×1.2 Armor Shred |
| 33% of melee damage is converted to fire damage; 33% of melee damage is converted to magic damage |
| +25% Spell Resistance |
