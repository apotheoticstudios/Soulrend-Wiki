**Warding** is one of the four branches of the [[skill-trees/warrior/templar|Templar]] subclass of the Warrior tree. It takes 6 points from the Templar node to reach its hub, which gives +30% Armor Toughness with heavy armor equipped. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Armor Toughness, Knockback Resistance, Impact and Armor Negation.

{{skilltree warrior focus="warrior_91"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Templar node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path | 1 | ×1.05 Knockback Resistance |
| Path | 2 | +5% Impact |
| Path ×2 | 3–4 | ×1.05 Knockback Resistance (each) |
| Path | 5 | +5% Impact |
| **Hub** | 6 | +30% Armor Toughness with heavy armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | ×1.25 Knockback Resistance with heavy armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | +25% Impact with heavy armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | +30% Armor Negation with heavy armor equipped; +1 Armor Negation |

## Totals

Taking all 9 nodes (9 points) gives:

- +30% Armor Toughness with heavy armor equipped
- ×1.25 Knockback Resistance with heavy armor equipped
- +25% Impact with heavy armor equipped
- +30% Armor Negation with heavy armor equipped
- +1 Armor Negation
- +10% Impact
- ×1.15 Knockback Resistance
