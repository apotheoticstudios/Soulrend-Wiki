The **Templar subclass keystones** are three nodes, of which a player can take at most two. Each one can only be learned by a player who has picked up a [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]] at least once. Each keystone costs 1 point and connects directly to the Templar node.

{{skilltree warrior focus="warrior_162, warrior_163, warrior_164"}}

## Keystones

| Keystone | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Paladin** | +25% Healing Received; +25% Spell Resistance; +10% Attack Damage per 25% Holy Spell Power, with heavy armor equipped |
| **Hoplite** | +60% Armor with a shield in hand; +30% Armor Toughness with a shield in hand; +1 Attack Damage per 50 Armor, with a shield in hand |
| **Knight** | +60% Max Health with heavy armor equipped; +40% Armor with heavy armor equipped; +1 Attack Damage per 50 Max Health, with heavy armor equipped |
