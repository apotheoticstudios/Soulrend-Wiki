**Unflinching** is one of the four branches of the [[skill-trees/warrior/templar|Templar]] subclass of the Warrior tree. It takes 6 points from the Templar node to reach its hub, which gives +5 Stun Armor with heavy armor equipped. Three leaves branch off the hub, one point each.

Its nodes give Stun Armor, Impact and damage reduction.

{{skilltree warrior focus="warrior_92"}}

## Nodes

The Points column counts the points spent in this branch to reach a node, starting from the Templar node. Path nodes must be taken in order; each leaf needs only the hub.

| Node | Points | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path ×5 | 1–5 | +1 Stun Armor (each) |
| **Hub** | 6 | +5 Stun Armor with heavy armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | +25% Impact with heavy armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | +10 Stun Armor with heavy armor equipped |
| Leaf | 7 | −10% damage taken per 20% missing health, with heavy armor equipped |

## Totals

Taking all 9 nodes (9 points) gives:

- +15 Stun Armor with heavy armor equipped
- +25% Impact with heavy armor equipped
- −10% damage taken per 20% missing health, with heavy armor equipped
- +5 Stun Armor
