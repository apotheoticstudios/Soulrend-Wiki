The **Shattered** is a sculk-infested humanoid from Deeper and Darker and the most common hostile mob of the Otherside, the dimension beyond the Deep Dark that [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker|Act III-b]] explores. It notices players only from 10 blocks away. A Fabled Realms quest asks for one kill.

## Spawning

| Biome | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Echoing Forest | 17 |
| Deeplands | 5 |

Some Otherside structures also hold Shattered.

## Stats

The Shattered has no level setting, so its level matches the Soulrend level of the nearest player (see [[leveling/mob-levels|Mob Levels]]).
