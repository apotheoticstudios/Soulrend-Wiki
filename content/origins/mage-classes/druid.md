The **Druid** is a mage class of the Nature school. In-game description: "Uses nature magic to poison enemies".

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Magic Affinity | +50% max mana. |
| Nature mastery | +30% Nature Spell Power. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, 8 Bread, and a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] (5 slots) with [[items/magic/spells/nature-spells#Poison Arrow\|Poison Arrow]] III and [[items/magic/spells/nature-spells#Oakskin\|Oakskin]] I. |

## Playstyle

The Druid opens with Poison Arrow from its starting book and learns more [[items/magic/spells/nature-spells|Nature spells]] from scrolls as it progresses. The +30% applies to every Nature spell. Like every mage class, it has no penalty with weapons, but it has no melee bonus either. The [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]'s Nature school node is [[skill-trees/mage/druid|Druid]].

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/forest-elf|Forest Elf]]: +100% Nature Spell Power of its own; the Druid's +30% reaches the threshold for a Grizzly Bear in Call of the Wild.
- [[origins/races/corrosid|Corrosid]]: Nature Spell Power raises the level of its Acid Fireball.
- [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]]: +50 max mana (multiplied by Magic Affinity) and +25% magic damage.
- [[origins/races/faded|Faded]]: +30% Spell Power and more mana, at the cost of half its health; it can only take a mage class.
