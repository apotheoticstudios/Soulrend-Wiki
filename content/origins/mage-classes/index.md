The **mage classes** are the twelve classes of the Mage tab, one per spell school. They all work the same way:

- **Magic Affinity:** +50% max mana.
- **School mastery:** +30% spell power in the class's school.
- **Starting kit:** leather armor, 8 Bread (Darkened Apples for the Eldritch Cultist) and a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] with the class's first spells.

They have no penalty with weapons and no melee bonus. A [[origins/races/faded|Faded]] can only pick one of these classes.
