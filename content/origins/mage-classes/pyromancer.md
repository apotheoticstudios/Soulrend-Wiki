The **Pyromancer** is a mage class of the Fire school. In-game description: "Uses fire magic to burn down enemies".

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Magic Affinity | +50% max mana. |
| Fire mastery | +30% Fire Spell Power. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, 8 Bread, and a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] (5 slots) with [[items/magic/spells/fire-spells#Firebolt\|Firebolt]] IV and [[items/magic/spells/fire-spells#Burning Dash\|Burning Dash]] IV. |

## Playstyle

The Pyromancer opens with Firebolt from its starting book and learns more [[items/magic/spells/fire-spells|Fire spells]] from scrolls as it progresses. The +30% applies to every Fire spell. Like every mage class, it has no penalty with weapons, but it has no melee bonus either. The [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]'s Fire school node is [[skill-trees/mage/pyromancer|Pyromancer]].

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]]: +50 max mana (multiplied by Magic Affinity) and +25% magic damage.
- [[origins/races/faded|Faded]]: +30% Spell Power and more mana, at the cost of half its health; it can only take a mage class.
