The **Bard** is a mage class of the Sound school. In-game description: "Uses music to vanquish enemies".

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Magic Affinity | +50% max mana. |
| Sound mastery | +30% Sound Spell Power. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, 8 Bread, and a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] (5 slots) with [[items/magic/spells/sound-spells#Chord Blast\|Chord Blast]] III and [[items/magic/spells/sound-spells#Rhapsody\|Rhapsody]] I. |

## Playstyle

The Bard opens with Chord Blast from its starting book and learns more [[items/magic/spells/sound-spells|Sound spells]] from scrolls as it progresses. The +30% applies to every Sound spell. Like every mage class, it has no penalty with weapons, but it has no melee bonus either. The [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]'s Sound school node is [[skill-trees/mage/bard|Bard]].

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]]: +50 max mana (multiplied by Magic Affinity) and +25% magic damage.
- [[origins/races/faded|Faded]]: +30% Spell Power and more mana, at the cost of half its health; it can only take a mage class.
