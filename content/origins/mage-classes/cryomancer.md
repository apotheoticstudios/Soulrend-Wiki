The **Cryomancer** is a mage class of the Ice school. In-game description: "Uses ice magic to freeze and shatter enemies".

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Magic Affinity | +50% max mana. |
| Ice mastery | +30% Ice Spell Power. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, 8 Bread, and a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] (5 slots) with [[items/magic/spells/ice-spells#Icicle\|Icicle]] IV and [[items/magic/spells/ice-spells#Frost Step\|Frost Step]] I. |

## Playstyle

The Cryomancer opens with Icicle from its starting book and learns more [[items/magic/spells/ice-spells|Ice spells]] from scrolls as it progresses. The +30% applies to every Ice spell. Like every mage class, it has no penalty with weapons, but it has no melee bonus either. The [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]'s Ice school node is [[skill-trees/mage/cryomancer|Cryomancer]].

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/yeti|Yeti]]: +100% Ice Spell Power of its own; together they raise Glacial Slam toward level 5.
- [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]]: +50 max mana (multiplied by Magic Affinity) and +25% magic damage.
- [[origins/races/faded|Faded]]: +30% Spell Power and more mana, at the cost of half its health; it can only take a mage class.
