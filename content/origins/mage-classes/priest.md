The **Priest** is a mage class of the Holy school. In-game description: "Use holy magic to heal and support your allies in battle".

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Magic Affinity | +50% max mana. |
| Holy mastery | +30% Holy Spell Power. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, 8 Bread, and a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] (5 slots) with [[items/magic/spells/holy-spells#Wisp\|Wisp]] I and [[items/magic/spells/holy-spells#Blessing of Life\|Blessing of Life]] II. |

> [!NOTE]
> The in-game text says +50% Holy Spell Power. The Priest actually gets +30%, like every other mage class.

## Playstyle

The Priest opens with Wisp from its starting book and learns more [[items/magic/spells/holy-spells|Holy spells]] from scrolls as it progresses. The +30% applies to every Holy spell. Like every mage class, it has no penalty with weapons, but it has no melee bonus either. The [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]'s Holy school node is [[skill-trees/mage/priest|Priest]].

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/valkyrie|Valkyrie]]: +100% Holy Spell Power of its own, plus Divine Smite.
- [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]]: +50 max mana (multiplied by Magic Affinity) and +25% magic damage.
- [[origins/races/faded|Faded]]: +30% Spell Power and more mana, at the cost of half its health; it can only take a mage class.
