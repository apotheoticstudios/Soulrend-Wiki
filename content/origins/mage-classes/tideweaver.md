The **Tideweaver** is a mage class of the Aqua school. In-game description: "Uses aqua magic to drown enemies".

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Magic Affinity | +50% max mana. |
| Aqua mastery | +30% Aqua Spell Power. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, 8 Bread, and a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] (5 slots) with [[items/magic/spells/aqua-spells#Hydroshot\|Hydroshot]] III and [[items/magic/spells/aqua-spells#Tidal Grasp\|Tidal Grasp]] I. |

## Playstyle

The Tideweaver opens with Hydroshot from its starting book and learns more [[items/magic/spells/aqua-spells|Aqua spells]] from scrolls as it progresses. The +30% applies to every Aqua spell. Like every mage class, it has no penalty with weapons, but it has no melee bonus either. The [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]'s Aqua school node is [[skill-trees/mage/aquamancer|Aquamancer]].

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/siren|Siren]]: a water race that heals in water and rain.
- [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]]: +50 max mana (multiplied by Magic Affinity) and +25% magic damage.
- [[origins/races/faded|Faded]]: +30% Spell Power and more mana, at the cost of half its health; it can only take a mage class.
