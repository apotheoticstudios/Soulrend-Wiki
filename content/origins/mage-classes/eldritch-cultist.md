The **Eldritch Cultist** is a mage class of the Eldritch school. In-game description: "An esoteric cult... Little is known about those who follow this path...".

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Magic Affinity | +50% max mana. |
| Eldritch mastery | +30% Eldritch Spell Power. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, 8 [[item:alexscaves:darkened_apple\|Darkened Apples]], and a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] (5 slots) with [[items/magic/spells/eldritch-spells#Abyssal Blast\|Abyssal Blast]] I. |

> [!NOTE]
> Eldritch spells normally have to be learned with an [[item:irons_spellbooks:eldritch_manuscript]] before they can be cast or crafted; see [[items/magic/spells/eldritch-spells|Eldritch Spells]].

## Playstyle

The Eldritch Cultist opens with Abyssal Blast from its starting book and learns more [[items/magic/spells/eldritch-spells|Eldritch spells]] from scrolls as it progresses. The +30% applies to every Eldritch spell. Like every mage class, it has no penalty with weapons, but it has no melee bonus either. The [[skill-trees/mage|Mage skill tree]]'s Eldritch school node is [[skill-trees/mage/warpmancer|Warpmancer]].

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]]: +50 max mana (multiplied by Magic Affinity) and +25% magic damage.
- [[origins/races/faded|Faded]]: +30% Spell Power and more mana, at the cost of half its health; it can only take a mage class.
