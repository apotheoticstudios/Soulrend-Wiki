The **Blade Dancer** is a whirlwind of exotic weapons that cuts through armor. The class picker lists it under **Other**.

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Precision | +25% damage with a Blade Dancer weapon in the main hand: the 46 items in `soulrend:class_blade_dancer`, which include chakrams, twinblades, warglaives, battlestaffs and staffs. |
| Focus | +10 Epic Fight Armor Negation (ignores 10% of the target's armor). |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, a [[item:wom:wooden_staff]], and 8 Steak. |

## Playstyle

Armor negation matters most against heavily armored enemies and bosses. The weapon list is broad, so the Blade Dancer can switch between fast chakrams and longer staffs.

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/moon-elf|Moon Elf]] and [[origins/races/goblin|Goblin]]: fast races for a mobile fighter.
- [[origins/races/human|Human]] for a plain start.
