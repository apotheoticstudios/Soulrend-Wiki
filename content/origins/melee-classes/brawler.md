The **Brawler** fights with its fists. Gloves, claws and knuckles deal double damage in its hands, and it attacks faster with everything.

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | +100% damage with a fist weapon in the main hand (the 17 items in `soulrend:class_shootfighter`). |
| Technique | +1 attack speed. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, two [[item:epicfight:glove\|Gloves]], a [[items/skill-books/tier-1/shooting-style\|Shooting Style]] skill book, and 8 Steak. |

## Fist weapons

{{items tag=soulrend:class_shootfighter}}

## Playstyle

The Brawler has the biggest weapon bonus of any class. Shooting Style is an Epic Fight skill book; learn it to use its style with gloves.

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/orc|Orc]] and [[origins/races/yeti|Yeti]] for a durable brawler.
- [[origins/races/keres|Keres]]: life steal and bleeding on every fast hit.
