The **Beast Master** wields a battlestaff and leads a host of summoned allies. Every summon it owns hits harder and lasts longer.

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Battle Mastery | +15% damage with battlestaff melee attacks (the 14 items in `soulrend:class_beast_master`). |
| Summon Mastery | All the Beast Master's summons deal +30% damage. |
| Fortified Summons | All its summons get +5% max health and +20 armor. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, an [[item:epicfight_dd:iron_battlestaff]], and a [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] with [[items/magic/spells/ice-spells#Summon Polar Bear\|Summon Polar Bear]] I and [[items/magic/spells/evocation-spells#Summon Horse\|Summon Horse]] I. |

Summons include Iron's Spellbooks summon spells, a [[origins/races/revenant|Revenant]]'s undead squad and a [[origins/races/forest-elf|Forest Elf]]'s eagle, Lupus Rex and Call of the Wild pack.

## Playstyle

The Beast Master fights in melee next to its summons. Summon Polar Bear gives it a tough ally from the start, and every new summon spell or race companion benefits from the class bonuses.

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/revenant|Revenant]]: its undead squad gets every Beast Master bonus.
- [[origins/races/forest-elf|Forest Elf]]: the eagle, the Lupus Rex and the summoned wolves and bears all count.
