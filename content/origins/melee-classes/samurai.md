The **Samurai** fights with a katana and little else. Katanas deal half again as much damage in its hands, and it attacks faster with every weapon.

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Katana Mastery | +50% damage with a katana in the main hand. Katanas are the 50 items in `soulrend:class_samurai` (katanas, tachis and wakizashi); they show a "Katana Mastery" line in their tooltip. |
| Agility | +20% attack speed. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, a Soulbound [[item:epicfight:bokken]] with Unbreaking V (it counts as a katana), and 8 Steak. |

> [!NOTE]
> The in-game text lists Speed (+20% movement speed) and a −50% penalty with other weapons. Neither is part of the Samurai in Soulrend.

## Playstyle

The Samurai's bonus is the largest single weapon bonus among the melee classes, but only with katanas, so its gear path follows katana drops and recipes.

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/ungoliant|Ungoliant]]: Silken Blade adds +25% with katanas.
- [[origins/races/human|Human]] or [[origins/races/moon-elf|Moon Elf]] for a fast, light build.
