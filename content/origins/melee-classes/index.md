Martial classes (the in-game Melee tab).

## In this section

- [[origins/melee-classes/warrior|Warrior]] — +15% attack damage, +30% crit damage. Starts with chainmail and a wooden greatsword.
- [[origins/melee-classes/berserker|Berserker]] — +20% max HP, +25% crit chance. Starts with chainmail and an iron axe.
- [[origins/melee-classes/rogue|Rogue]] — +20% dodge, silent footsteps. Starts with leather and a flint knife.
- [[origins/melee-classes/tank|Tank]] — +20% armor, +30% max HP. Starts with chainmail, shield and sword.
- [[origins/melee-classes/samurai|Samurai]] — +50% katana damage, +20% attack speed. Starts with leather and a bokken.
- [[origins/melee-classes/lancer|Lancer]] — +25% damage with lances and spears. Starts with a wooden spear.
- [[origins/melee-classes/beast-master|Beast Master]] — +15% battlestaff damage, stronger summons. Starts with summon spells.
- [[origins/melee-classes/brawler|Brawler]] — +100% glove damage, +1 attack speed. Starts with two gloves.
- [[origins/melee-classes/blade-dancer|Blade Dancer]] — +25% with chakrams, twinblades and warglaives, +10 armor negation. Listed under "Other" in game.
- [[origins/melee-classes/wildheart|Wildheart]] — Feral Beast only: fist weapons, Frenzy stacks, heal on fist kills.
