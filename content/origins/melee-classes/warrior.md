The **Warrior** is a weapons specialist: every melee weapon hits harder and critical hits hit much harder. It has no drawbacks and no weapon restrictions.

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Superior Mastery | +15% attack damage. |
| Combat Wisdom | +30% Critical Damage (150% → 180%). |
| Basic Items | Chainmail armor, a Soulbound [[item:epicfight:wooden_greatsword]] with Unbreaking V, and 8 Steak. |

## Playstyle

The Warrior's bonuses apply to any weapon, so it can follow whatever drops or quests provide. Critical Damage is worth more with crit chance from gear, gems or the [[skill-trees/warrior|Warrior skill tree]].

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/orc|Orc]], [[origins/races/yeti|Yeti]] and [[origins/races/dwarf|Dwarf]]: sturdy races that keep a melee fighter alive.
- [[origins/races/human|Human]] for a plain start.
