The **Rogue** is a stalker who avoids hits instead of absorbing them. One attack in five misses it entirely, and its footsteps don't make vibrations.

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Evasive | +20% Dodge Chance. |
| Silent Steps | Footsteps don't create vibrations, so they don't set off sculk sensors or alert the Warden. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, two Soulbound [[item:farmersdelight:flint_knife\|Flint Knives]] with Unbreaking V, and 8 Cooked Chicken. |

> [!NOTE]
> The in-game text promises two cutlasses and a Weapon Mastery power; in Soulrend the kit has Flint Knives and the Rogue has no weapon mastery or weapon penalty.

## Playstyle

The Rogue wears light armor and relies on dodging. Silent Steps makes the Deep Dark and other sculk areas much safer to explore.

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/moon-elf|Moon Elf]]: the Flint Knives count as daggers for Moonlight Rogue (+50%) and for triple-damage stealth attacks.
- [[origins/races/goblin|Goblin]] and [[origins/races/pixie|Pixie]]: small, fast races that dodge rather than tank.
