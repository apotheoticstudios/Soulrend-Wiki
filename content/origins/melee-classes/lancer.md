The **Lancer** fights with long armaments: spears, lances, glaives and halberds.

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Lance Mastery | +25% damage with a polearm in the main hand: the 88 items in `soulrend:class_lancer`, plus any weapon Medieval Origins counts as a Valkyrie weapon. |
| Basic Items | Leather armor, a [[item:epicfight:wooden_spear]], and 8 Steak. |

## Polearms

{{items tag=soulrend:class_lancer}}

## Playstyle

Polearms keep enemies at a distance. Lance Mastery is the Lancer's only power, so race powers carry more of the build.

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/valkyrie|Valkyrie]]: flight and Divine Smite on every third hit.
- [[origins/races/orc|Orc]] and [[origins/races/yeti|Yeti]] for a durable front-liner.
