**Humans** have no racial powers and no drawbacks. The race plays exactly like vanilla Minecraft, so everything about the character comes from its [[origins|class]], [[leveling|stats]] and [[skill-trees|skill tree]].

## Powers

None. The in-game text reads: "A human's fate is more open than most - venture forth with no benefits or drawbacks."

## Playstyle

Human suits players who want to learn Soulrend's combat without extra rules to track: no diet limits, no size change, no weaknesses. Every class is available.

## Pairs well with

Any class. Classes that change how a character fights the most, such as [[origins/melee-classes/samurai|Samurai]] or the [[origins/mage-classes|mage classes]], are easiest to learn on a Human because nothing else changes.
