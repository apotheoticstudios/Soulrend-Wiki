**Goblins** are small, quick and obsessed with gold. Golden gear works far better in their hands, they find more loot, and they outrun most threats. They have 4 fewer hearts than a human.

## Powers

| Power | Effect | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greedy: weapons | A golden weapon in the main hand gives +50% attack damage (+35% in the off hand), plus an extra loot roll and an extra rare loot roll on kills. | — |
| Greedy: armor | Each golden armor piece gives +2 max health; the golden chestplate and boots also give +15% armor. A full golden set reduces damage taken by 20%. | — |
| Greedy: tools | Golden tools give +1 reach and faster mining, and can harvest blocks that need an iron tool. | — |
| Cunning | +1 luck. | — |
| Nimble | +30% movement speed. | — |

## Drawbacks

| Drawback | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Frail | −8 max health (4 hearts). |
| Stunted | 60% of human height. |

## Playstyle

The Goblin turns weak golden gear into a real option early on and rewards keeping a golden weapon for its loot rolls. Its speed lets it avoid fights it can't win.

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/melee-classes/rogue|Rogue]]: dodge chance covers for Frail.
- [[origins/melee-classes/berserker|Berserker]] and [[origins/melee-classes/tank|Tank]]: their percentage max-health bonuses offset Frail.
