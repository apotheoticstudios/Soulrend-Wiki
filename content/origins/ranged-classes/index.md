The **Ranged** tab of the [[origins|class]] picker has one class.

| Class | Powers | Starting gear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[origins/ranged-classes/hunter\|Hunter]] | +30% arrow damage, extra arrows per craft, camouflage in leaves, Looting II on every kill. | Leather, Bow, a Quiver with 32 arrows |

Other classes can use bows too, since no class has weapon penalties. The [[origins/races/forest-elf|Forest Elf]] race adds its own archery powers.
