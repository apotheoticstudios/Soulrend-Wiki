The **Spellblade** weaves magic into every strike. It gets a smaller bonus to both melee and spells than the specialist classes, but its spell power applies to every school.

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Weapon Mastery | +15% attack damage. |
| Magic Prowess | +25% Spell Power (all schools). |
| Mana Reserve | +20% max mana. |
| Basic Items | Chainmail armor, a Soulbound [[item:minecraft:wooden_sword]] with Unbreaking V, a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] with [[items/magic/spells/ender-spells#Magic Missile\|Magic Missile]] II and [[items/magic/spells/ender-spells#Teleport\|Teleport]] I, and 8 Steak. |

## Playstyle

Teleport closes or opens distance, and Magic Missile softens targets before the Spellblade closes in. Because Magic Prowess covers every school, any spell found later benefits.

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]]: +50 max mana and +25% magic damage.
- [[origins/races/human|Human]] for a plain start: no race penalty gets in the way of either side of the class.
