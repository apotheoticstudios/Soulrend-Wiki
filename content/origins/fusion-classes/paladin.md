The **Paladin** mixes armor and holy magic, protecting itself and its allies while clearing the way forward.

## Powers

| Power | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Protector | +20% Holy Spell Power and +20% armor. |
| Divine Will | +20% healing received. |
| Basic Items | Chainmail armor, a [[item:dungeons_and_combat:wooden_shield]], a Soulbound [[item:minecraft:wooden_sword]] with Unbreaking V, a Soulbound [[item:irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]] with [[items/magic/spells/holy-spells#Fortify\|Fortify]] I and [[items/magic/spells/holy-spells#Heal\|Heal]] I, and 8 Steak. |

> [!NOTE]
> The in-game text lists Shield of Light (+50% armor with a shield). That power isn't part of the Paladin in Soulrend.

## Playstyle

Heal and Fortify are both boosted by Holy Spell Power and Divine Will, so the Paladin heals itself well while fighting in melee.

## Pairs well with

- [[origins/races/valkyrie|Valkyrie]]: +100% Holy Spell Power and Divine Smite.
- [[origins/races/high-elf|High Elf]]: more mana and +25% magic damage.
