"Own the ancient arcane of the endermen."

 When triggering this skill, you can choose
 between 4 different type of actions:
  - in neutral and rather close to target, you'll do an [Ender Assault]
    while alternating between left swing and right swing. 
  - While sprinting, you will do an [Ender Assault] and,
    if the attack connect, trigger positive [Ender Snatch].
  - While rising in the air, you will trigger aerian
    [Ender Assault] and, trigger negative [Ender Snatch].
  - While sneaking and with 10 stored stack,
    you'll summon Ender Hands under targets around you.
   Then the hands grasps their respective targets,
    Damagind them and inflicting them with mining fatigue and slowness.

 [Ender Assault] - Teleport you around your target depending
 on the direction you are look at.
