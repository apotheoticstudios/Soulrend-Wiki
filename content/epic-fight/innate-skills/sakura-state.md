"Sharpest blade forged in absolute serenity"

 On cast, you'll perform a Sakura slash.
 Sakura slashes can be declined in 2 form
  - while sprinting,
  you will fastly pass through enemys in front of you, slashing them along the way.  - while sprinting, (cost 2 stack)
  you will fastly pass through enemys in front of you,
  slashing them along the way.
  - while standing or walking, (cost 1 stack)
  you'll fastly slash in front of you.
 Sakura slashes automaticaly sheath your blade afterward.

 You can stack a total of 12 sakura slashes and Sweeping edge
 on your blade will grant you extra stack depending
 on the amount of stack you have on the first cast.
([initial amont of stack] * 1/3 of the sweeping edge levels)

 After the innitial draw, you'll enter sakura state.
 If you don't draw a Sakura slash again within 4 seconds, you loose all your
 stacks and get out of sakura state.

 Slashing a target with a Dash attack or a Sakura slash,
 will stack Perpetual slashes on the target.
 (3 on dashes and sakura slash.
 6 on sakura dash slash
 this value is then multiply by 1/3 of the Sweeping edge lvl).
 If no action is performed for a set of time after landing perpetual slashes,
 All Perpetual slashes stacked on the target are triggered,
 slashing it in sequence for each stacks of Perpetual slashes
