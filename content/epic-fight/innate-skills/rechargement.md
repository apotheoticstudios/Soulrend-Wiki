"Il n'y a que deux puissances au monde, le sabre et l'esprit : à la longue, le sabre est toujours vaincu par l'esprit."

 After attacking performing skill will reload ammunition
 depending on the attack made before it.

  - 1 ammo after first attack.
  - 2 ammo after the second attacks.
  - 3 ammo afer the third one.

 The last attack of the normal combo will also realod 3 ammo.

 To shoot your ammo, you must perform an attack or a reload first.
 You can also shoot right after sprinting or if you are rising in the air (jumping).

 Dealing damage with a shot will increase [Heatdrive] depending on the bullet type.
 [Heatdrive] slowly decrease if no shot damage is dealt within 4 sec.
 When fully charged, [Heatdrive] become [Souldrive]:
  during that state, the drive will quickly decrease but,
  every second during that state will reload 1 ammo.

 Ammo can be overcharged if rechargement is performed when 6 ammo is allready loaded.
 Overcharged Ammo deal more damage and bypass guards.

 Reloading to reach exactly 6 ammo or 6 overcharged ammo will double
 the reload ammount for as long as you keep your reloads to reach exactly 6.
