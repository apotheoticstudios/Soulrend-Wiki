"Unbreakable defense combined with overwhelming offense rules over battle."

 On cast, you'll perform shield attacks in front of you.
 There is 3 Basic attacks, 1 Dash attack and
 1 Jump attack for both the sword and the shield.

 Some attacks (shields or sword attacks) will move your in front of you.
 While this occure, the shield will have a slight red field around it as an indicator.
 During this short period,you will block incoming attacks and
 perform an automatic counter attack.

 Depending on how you block an attack, you will increase you Shield Charges by a certain amount.
 (increased with sweeping edge lvl)
 Shield Charges can be stored for up to 90 and used in 2 ways:
  - With the Shield, by holding guard and casting a shield attack.
    You will convert all Shield charge into shield Overcharge,
    granting you damage resistance.
  - With the Sword, by holding guard and casting a sword attack.
    You will charge your sword and consume your
     shield charges slowly to perform a spin attack.

 While your shield is Overcharged, your shield attack will do
 increase damage after a sword attack based on the Overcharge amount.

 Getting damaged reduce your Shield Overcharge by 5.
 ( Sweeping edge on your Sword reduce this by 1 per level )
