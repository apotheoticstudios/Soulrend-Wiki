Use the preset the game recommends. Open **Options → Video Settings**; the Graphics Preset tooltip names your hardware and its recommended preset. If fights stutter, go down one step, since Epic Fight effects and particles cost the most. Only High and Ultra turn on shaders.

`/fsoclient preset auto` applies the recommended preset. See [[features/settings-and-performance/graphics-presets|Graphics Presets]] and [[features/settings-and-performance/first-launch-setup|First-Launch Setup]], which also offers better Java arguments.
