Send the crash report file, not a screenshot, in the crash-reports channel of the [official Discord](https://discord.gg/pBDKC2Ttx8). Read the fine print at the top of the report first. A crash can be caused by one mod rather than the pack. Try the steps in [[faq/performance-and-troubleshooting/why-does-soulrend-crash-on-launch|Why does Soulrend crash on launch?]] before posting.
