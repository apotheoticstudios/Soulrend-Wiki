Soulrend's main quest line, **The Exalted Path**, is a Prologue and five acts, and each act ends with a quest that opens the next. Open the quest book with the **Quests** tab at the top left of the inventory screen.

A quest can't be started until the quests it depends on are done, and most stay hidden until then. Item tasks check your inventory and don't take the items, unless a quest says so. Side chapters for bosses and gear branch off the acts. Quests also tell you what you need and where to find it, so check the book first when you're stuck.

Mob levels follow your own level rather than the act. See [[progression|Progression]] for the acts and [[leveling/mob-levels|Mob Levels]].
