<!-- sources: maintainer-provided answer (2026-11-02) -->
There is no best race or class. They are all roughly balanced, and the choice only changes how you play. Pick the one whose abilities sound fun. You can change it later with the Orb of Origin ([[faq/getting-started/how-do-i-change-my-origin|How do I change my origin?]]).

Browse the [[origins|Origins]] section to compare them.
