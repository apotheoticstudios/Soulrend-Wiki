Press **O**, or open your inventory (I) and click the **Skill Tree** tab. Skill points come from Soulrend levels, which you earn by dealing damage; you get one point per level. See [[features/character/inventory-tabs|Inventory Tabs]] and [[faq/skills-and-levelling/how-do-i-earn-skill-points|How do I earn skill points?]].

The tree has hundreds of nodes, and the [[skill-trees|Skill Trees]] section has a page for each branch. Both keys can be changed in Controls.
