The official servers are run by Craft Down Under in three regions (AU, EU and US). The Soulrend Companion mod in the pack replaces the Multiplayer screen with an **Official Soulrend Realms** menu: pick a region to join, and use **Switch region** in the pause menu to change later. One character is shared across all three.

See [[features/multiplayer/soulrend-realms|Official Servers]] for the addresses.
