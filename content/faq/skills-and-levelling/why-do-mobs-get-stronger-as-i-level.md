A new mob takes the **Soulrend level** of the nearest player in its dimension, averaged with any players within 60 blocks of that player. Auto Leveling then turns that level into stats: +30% max health and +10% attack damage per level.

The level is set once, when the mob spawns. Many bosses have a fixed level or a floor, so they don't follow you down. See [[leveling/mob-levels|Mob Levels]].
