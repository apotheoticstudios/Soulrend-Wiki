Deal damage to mobs. Damage fills your level progress, and every Soulrend level gives one skill point, up to 200 points at level 200. A [[item:skilltree:wisdom_scroll]] gives one more; experience levels can't be traded for points.

See [[leveling/earning-levels|Earning Levels]] for what damage counts and how much each level needs, and [[skill-trees|Skill Trees]] for spending the points.
