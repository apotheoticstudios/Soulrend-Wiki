Yes. Use an [[item:skilltree:amnesia_scroll]] to take back every skill you've learned. Soulrend sets its penalty to 0%, so no points are lost. The Prologue quest [[progression/prologue/the-skill-tree|The Skill Tree]] gives you one and the Ender Dragon drops another.

An operator can also reset a player with `/skilltree reset`. See [[features/commands/skilltree-command|/skilltree]].
