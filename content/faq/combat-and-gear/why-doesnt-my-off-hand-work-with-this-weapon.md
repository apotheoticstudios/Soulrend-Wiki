350 weapons, such as greatswords, great axes and halberds, count as two-handed. While you hold one in your main hand, you aren't dual wielding, so skill tree bonuses that need dual wielding stop working. The off-hand item can still be held and used: a shield still blocks.

[[skill-trees/warrior/berserker|Berserker]] nodes work the other way and need a two-handed weapon. See [[features/combat/two-handed-weapons|Two-Handed Weapons]].
