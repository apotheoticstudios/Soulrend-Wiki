Epic Fight is probably in **mining mode**, where attacks are vanilla and dodging and guarding are off. Switch to battle mode with the **Toggle Battle/Mining Mode** key. Soulrend leaves it unbound, so set it in Controls first.

See [[epic-fight|Epic Fight]] for the combat controls and [[epic-fight/dodging-and-parrying|Dodging & Parrying]].
