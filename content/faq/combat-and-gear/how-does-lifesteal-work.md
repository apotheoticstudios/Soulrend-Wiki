Soulrend's Lifesteal attribute heals you by a share of your **own maximum health** on each hit, scaled by how big the hit was compared with the target's health, and capped at 15% of your max HP per hit. It isn't a percentage of the damage dealt.

It currently does nothing in normal play: no item, skill node or origin grants the attribute, and the Vampiric and Soulflare affixes are disabled. See [[features/combat/lifesteal|Lifesteal]] for the formula and [[bugs#Lifesteal attribute has no source|Known Bugs]].
