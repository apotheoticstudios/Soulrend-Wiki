Short answers about Epic Fight controls, weapons, bosses and gear.

## In this section

- [[faq/combat-and-gear/why-cant-i-dodge-or-block|Why can't I dodge or block?]] — You are in mining mode.
- [[faq/combat-and-gear/why-cant-i-use-the-combat-style-manual|Why can't I use the Combat Style Manual?]] — It is disabled.
- [[faq/combat-and-gear/why-doesnt-my-off-hand-work-with-this-weapon|Why doesn't my off-hand work with this weapon?]] — Two-handed weapons cancel dual-wield bonuses.
- [[faq/combat-and-gear/why-cant-i-burst-down-a-boss|Why can't I burst down a boss?]] — Bosses have damage limits per second.
- [[faq/combat-and-gear/how-does-lifesteal-work|How does lifesteal work?]] — It heals a share of your max HP, but is currently unobtainable.
- [[faq/combat-and-gear/do-upgrades-keep-my-enchantments|Do upgrades keep my enchantments?]] — Yes.
- [[faq/combat-and-gear/why-does-nothing-drop-anything|Why does nothing drop anything?]] — Knowledge of the Ages converts drops to experience.
