Soulrend Core limits how much health 33 bosses can lose in one second: 5% of max HP for the eight Cataclysm bosses, 4% for the Nightwarden and 20% for the rest. Damage beyond the limit is blocked, and the limit is shared by every player, pet, projectile and spell, so a bigger group doesn't raise it.

That sets the fastest possible kill at 20 s, 25 s or 5 s. Steady damage works better than one huge hit. See [[features/combat/boss-damage-limits|Boss Damage Limits]].
