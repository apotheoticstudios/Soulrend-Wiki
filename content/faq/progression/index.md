Short answers to common questions about the main quest line. Each links to the full [[progression|Progression]] pages.

## In this section

- [[faq/progression/how-do-i-get-into-the-nether|How do I get into the Nether?]] — Use the Seal of the Underworld, made from four shards, the Nether Crystal and four essences.
- [[faq/progression/what-unlocks-act-iv|What unlocks Act IV?]] — Any five Legendary Eye quests, then entering the End.
- [[faq/progression/how-do-i-reach-the-past|How do I reach the Past?]] — Right-click an Emblem of the Past.
- [[faq/progression/is-the-nightwarden-required|Is the Nightwarden required?]] — No, its quest is optional.
- [[faq/progression/where-is-the-deeper-and-darker-act|Where is the Deeper and Darker act?]] — It opens from Act II's The Otherside quest.
