It opens from **The Otherside**, a quest in [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II]] that asks for a Heart of the Deep. Its reward is a button into [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker|Act III-b – Deeper and Darker]], which the quest book titles "Act III - Deeper and Darker".

The Otherside needs The Warden and the Dark Iron Ingot quests first, and the Heart of the Deep's recipe uses a Withered Demon Heart from the Decaying King in the Nether, so in practice the act opens during Act III. It is optional: nothing in the main line depends on it. See [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-otherside|The Otherside]].
