Use a [[item:nether_remastered:seal_of_the_underworld_item]]: hold right-click with it for 5 seconds, and Nether portals start working for you. Until then a portal only shows a message.

The seal is crafted from the four seal shards, the [[item:nether_remastered:seal_crystal]] (a 50% Warden drop) and four [[item:nether_remastered:bottled_nether_essence]]. Act II's quests lead to each piece; see [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II – First Steps]] and [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-seal-of-the-underworld|The Seal of the Underworld]]. The seal isn't used up, so a team can share one.
