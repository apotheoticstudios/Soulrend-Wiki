Completing any **five** of the sixteen Legendary Eye quests in [[progression/side-chapters/journey-to-the-end|Journey To The End]] opens the last quest of Act III, and entering the End completes it and opens [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]].

The Eight Sins quest says four Cataclysm bosses are needed, but no boss kill is part of the gate. Getting into the End is the harder half: the stronghold portal needs twelve different Legendary Eyes, one per frame.

See [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/act-iv-beyond-reality-gate|Act IV – Beyond Reality Gate]] for the details and where each eye comes from.
