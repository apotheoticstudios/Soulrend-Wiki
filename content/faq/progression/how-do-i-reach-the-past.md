Right-click an [[item:kubejs:emblem_of_the_past]] anywhere outside the Past. It drops you into the Past from y=200 with Slow Falling.

The Emblem is crafted from four Astral Ingots, four Nightmare Ingots and a Manifestation of Infinity. Both ingots are made from Universium, which needs a Dragon Egg, so the route runs through the Ender Dragon in [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]]. See [[progression/act-v-duties/emblem-of-the-past|Emblem of the Past]].

To leave the Past, warp out with a Warp Stone or Warp Scroll, or die. The Emblem doesn't work inside the Past. See [[progression/act-v-duties/visit-the-past|Visit the Past]].
