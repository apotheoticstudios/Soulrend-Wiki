No. The Nightwarden's quest, "Your test begins.", is marked optional, and the quest that opens [[progression/act-v-duties|Act V]] only needs [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/end-highlands|End Highlands]], which comes right after the Ender Dragon.

The fight is still worth it later: it gives 250 XP levels, two Universium Ingots, two Perfect Gems and a Wisdom Scroll. See [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/defeat-the-nightwarden|Defeat The Nightwarden]].
