Get a scroll of the spell and use it at an **Inscription Table** to write it into a spellbook in your Spell Book slot. The scroll is used up and the spell stays in the book. Scrolls come from loot, boss drops, wandering traders and the Scroll Forge, and each school page under [[items/magic/spells|Spells]] says where its spells come from.

**Eldritch spells** must be learned first: right-click an [[item:irons_spellbooks:eldritch_manuscript]] and hold the spell in its research screen. Unlearned ones show as "Unlearned Scroll". See [[features/magic/casting-spells|Casting Spells]].
