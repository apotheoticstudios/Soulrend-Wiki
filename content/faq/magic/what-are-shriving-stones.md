A [[item:irons_spellbooks:shriving_stone]] does two things:

- **At an Arcane Anvil,** it removes an item's imbued spells and upgrades.
- **In a crafting grid,** combined with one of 35 unique weapons, staffs and spellbooks, it gives the item back with all its locked spells unlocked. Soulrend adds this recipe for each of them.

See [[items/magic/magic-stations|Magic Stations]] and [[items/magic/spellbooks|Spellbooks]].
