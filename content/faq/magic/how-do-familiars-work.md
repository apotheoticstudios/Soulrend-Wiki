Familiars are small spellcasting companions from Alshanex's Familiars. Using a class **shard** summons a familiar of that class, and it casts that class's spells. Six shards are made in rituals on Origin Island, which Memory Fragments lead to.

A **familiar trinket** in the Familiar Trinket curio slot makes familiars of its class cast at a higher level. The Pandora Box summons or dismisses several at once. See [[items/magic/familiars|Familiars]] for the ritual recipes.
