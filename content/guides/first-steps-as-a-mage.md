This guide takes a spellcaster from a fresh world to the gear needed to beat Act II, then to the gem, reforging and augmenting routine that carries a mage to the end of the pack. It is based on a community guide written for Soulrend's Discord and is meant as a follow-up to the general starting guide there.

The order below is a suggestion. Several steps happen at the same time, and a class with an easier start can skip ahead. Which gear drops and which structures are nearby depend on the world seed.

> [!NOTE]
> The quantities that depend on class (the number of Stuffed Pumpkins and armor sets) are given as "one set for most classes, two for some". Check the quest book for your class.

## Before you start

- Pick a magic class. See [[origins]] and the Magic Equipment quest chapter ([[progression/side-chapters/magic-equipment|Magic Equipment]]).
- Bring an Explorer's Compass to find the village and tower below.

## First steps

### Find a Mossy Mounds village

Use the Explorer's Compass to locate a **Mossy Mounds** village. This kind of village has many crops and useful crafting materials. Loot these:

1. Rice (if the village has none, look in swamps).
2. Carrots, tomatoes or beetroots.
3. Onions.
4. Brown mushrooms.
5. Potatoes.
6. Sweet berries or any other berries.
7. Pumpkins.
8. Melons.
9. Flax, which crafts into string.

Plant what you took in a small greenhouse so the crops keep coming.

{{image guides/mage-greenhouse-layout.jpg caption="Greenhouse layout, 17 × 15 blocks: pumpkins along the left, melons along the right, and a 3 × 3 grid of carrots, onions, brown mushrooms, potatoes, rice, berries, flax and two flex plots." width=560}}

{{image guides/mage-greenhouse.jpg caption="A finished greenhouse." width=720}}

### Cook Stuffed Pumpkins

Find or craft a [[item:farmersdelight:cooking_pot]] and place it on a campfire. Cook a [[item:farmersdelight:stuffed_pumpkin]] from:

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Rice | 4 |
| Carrots, tomatoes or beetroots | 4 |
| Onions | 4 |
| Brown mushrooms | 4 |
| Potatoes | 4 |
| Berries | 4 |
| Pumpkins | 4 |

That makes 4 Stuffed Pumpkins. Some classes need 8.

## First gear

Handing in the Stuffed Pumpkins unlocks the Magic Gear questline. The route is [[items/armor/mage-armor/wandering-magician-armor|Wandering Magician]] → [[items/armor/mage-armor/scarecrow-armor|Scarecrow]] → [[items/armor/mage-armor/wizard-armor|Wizard]].


{{image guides/mage-magic-gear-quests.jpg caption="The Magic Gear questline, which opens once the Stuffed Pumpkins are handed in." width=560}}

### Wandering Magician set

{{image guides/mage-quest-wandering-magician.jpg caption="The Wandering Magician Hood quest." width=412}}

Craft one set (two for some classes) from:

- 16 Leather
- 7 [[item:irons_spellbooks:arcane_essence]], which is crafted from lapis and amethyst shards
- 1 [[item:irons_spellbooks:cinder_essence]]

**Cinder Essence.** The author gets the first one from a mage tower, which also supplies much of the rest of the early magic loot. There are other towers for most magic classes; the Pyromancer tower is the one used here.

{{image guides/mage-pyromancer-tower.jpg caption="A Pyromancer Tower seen from above." width=720}}

{{image guides/mage-pyromancer-tower-tooltip.jpg caption="The structure tooltip for a Pyromancer Tower." width=532}}


1. Open the chest at the top of the tower, which holds gunpowder and charcoal.
2. Go down to the basement. If the Pyromancer inside carries Legendary spells, consider leaving it alive.
3. Loot everything useful. Required: gunpowder, a ghast tear, a blaze rod, paper, candles, ink, Apothic gear (items with colored names), an [[item:irons_spellbooks:inscription_table]], books, cauldrons, scrolls, runestones, gems and Arcane Cloth. Charcoal, a brewing stand and bottles are optional.
4. Kill sheep on the way for wool and meat.

{{image guides/mage-tower-basement.jpg caption="Inside the tower's basement, where its Pyromancer mage waits." width=720}}

Blasting an Arcane Essence in a blast furnace also makes Cinder Essence.

{{recipes irons_spellbooks:cinder_essence}}

### Scarecrow set

{{image guides/mage-quest-scarecrow.jpg caption="The Scarecrow Hat quest." width=407}}

Craft these, then combine them with the Wandering Magician set at a smithing table:

- 4 [[item:astral_calamity_extras:grotesque_ingot]]. These need the 4 Stuffed Pumpkins and 8 [[item:irons_spellbooks:magic_cloth]] (craft one, then claim the 8 Arcane Cloth reward from the questline; the cloths cost 32 Arcane Essence and 4 wool).
- 4 Hay Bales.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Crafting the Scarecrow set gives a reward. Choose the **rune of the class you want to play**. The reward can also roll a Mithril Ingot or Scrap.

{{image guides/mage-scarecrow-reward.jpg caption="The Scarecrow reward menu: pick the rune of your school." width=218}}

{{recipes irons_spellbooks:pumpkin_chestplate}}

### Wizard set

{{image guides/mage-quest-wizard.jpg caption="The Wizard Hood quest." width=412}}

The next set needs:

- 4 Arcane Cloth (32 Arcane Essence and 4 wool).
- 4 [[item:astral_calamity_extras:enchanted_ingot]] (160 Arcane Essence, 16 wool, 4 [[item:irons_spellbooks:arcane_ingot]] and 4 iron, gold or copper ingots).

Crafting an Arcane Ingot also unlocks a quest reward that can give Mithril.

{{image guides/mage-quest-arcane-ingot.jpg caption="The Arcane Ingot quest." width=412}}

These three sets are the first gear, enough to finish Act II – First Steps.

### Inscription Table

Slot a spell into your spellbook at the Inscription Table to use it. Click a slotted spell and take it out of the right-hand box to remove it for free.

{{image guides/mage-inscription-table.jpg caption="The Inscription Table with a spellbook and three spells slotted." width=519}}

### Alchemist Cauldron and ink

Ink comes in five tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary.

| Ink | Recipe |
| --- | --- |
| Common | A bottle, an ink sac and 2 Arcane Essence. |
| Uncommon | 4 Common inks in the cauldron plus a copper ingot. |
| Rare | 4 Uncommon inks plus an iron ingot. |
| Epic | 4 Rare inks plus a gold ingot. |
| Legendary | 4 Epic inks plus an amethyst shard. |

A better source is **recycling spells**. Fill the cauldron with water and drop in a spell scroll to extract its ink, with a 75% chance of success. Right-click the cauldron with a bottle to take the ink out.

{{image guides/mage-ink-recycling.jpg caption="A cauldron full of water recycling a scroll for ink." width=500}}

Caster mobs (Aquamancers, Pyromancers, Geomancers and so on) sell spells you can recycle and drop spells and ink when killed. The Sky Arena is one of the best places to farm them.

### Arcane Anvil

Use the Arcane Anvil to upgrade spells. It also takes **Upgrade Orbs**, which add to the item's Spell Power and other stats by up to +3 per orb. Every orb is in the Magic Gear questline, and an item holds at most 3 orbs.

{{image guides/mage-arcane-anvil.jpg caption="A Triple Fireball Scroll upgraded to Level 5 (Legendary) at the Arcane Anvil." width=586}}

{{image guides/mage-upgrade-orbs.jpg caption="The upgrade orb quests in the Magic Gear questline." width=500}}

Different orbs combine on one item, for example +10% Fire Spell Power with +5% Cooldown Reduction. This is how to build a school combination with no gear of its own, such as Fire and Lightning or Holy and Blood. 
{{image guides/mage-robe-orbs.jpg caption="A Pyromancer Robe +3 after several orbs: +5% Aqua, Ender and Lightning Spell Power and +10% Fire Spell Power." width=700}}

See [[items/magic/runes-orbs-and-inks|Runes, Orbs & Inks]].

## Enchantments

Follow the Max Apotheosis Enchanting Table guide to reach these levels ([[guides/enchanting-guide|Enchanting Guide]]). Blast Protection is favored because some endgame Epic Fight gear scales with it. The Critical Knowledge skill book is one example: its crit chance grows with Fire Protection and its crit damage with Blast Protection, so keep a mix of both.

{{image guides/mage-critical-knowledge.jpg caption="The Critical Knowledge skill book: crit chance scales with Fire Protection and crit damage with Blast Protection." width=600}}

| Slot | Enchantments |
| --- | --- |
| Helmet | Fire Protection IX, Invigoration IX, Protection VIII, Respiration VII, Thorns III, Aqua Affinity. Featherweight for [[origins/races/valkyrie\|Valkyrie]] or [[origins/races/keres\|Keres]]; Mirroring for Keres. |
| Chestplate | Blast Protection IX (Fire Protection works too, but use a mix of both across the set), Invigoration IX, Protection VIII, Thorns III. Featherweight for Valkyrie or Keres. |
| Leggings | Blast Protection IX, Invigoration IX, Protection VIII, Thorns III. Featherweight for Valkyrie or Keres. |
| Boots | Feather Falling X, Blast Protection IX, Invigoration IX, Protection VIII, Depth Strider VII, Thorns III, Stable Footing. Featherweight for Valkyrie or Keres. |

Staves take no enchantments.

Notes on the less familiar ones:

- **Invigoration** adds 2 stamina per level.
- **Stable Footing** removes the mining-speed penalty while flying.
- **Featherweight** stops heavy armor from slowing winged races and Elves. It comes from Medieval Origins, as does **Mirroring**, which protects a helmet's wearer from a Gorgon's petrifying gaze and lets Dwarves see in daylight.

## Reforging

Reforging has many tiers; the Reforging chapter of the quest book covers them ([[progression/side-chapters/reforging|Reforging]]).

{{image guides/mage-reforging-quests-b.jpg caption="The Reforging chapter of the quest book." width=560}}
 This guide covers **Epic** reforging, which progression needs, and **Ancient** reforging, which is enough to beat the pack. See [[items/gear-progression/reforging-tables|Reforging Tables]].

### Setup

You need a [[item:apotheosis:salvaging_table]] and a [[item:apotheosis:reforging_table]], plus a [[item:apotheosis:sigil_of_rebirth]] for each reforge.

{{recipes apotheosis:salvaging_table}}

{{recipes apotheosis:reforging_table}}

{{recipes apotheosis:sigil_of_rebirth}}

1. Loot Apothic gear. Its name color shows its rarity.
2. Break it down at the Salvaging Table. Salvaged gear gives rarity materials and gem dust; salvaged gems give gem dust.
3. Put the item, the rarity material you want and a Sigil of Rebirth into the Reforging Table. You can reforge the same item as often as you like.

Mobs with colored names are more likely to drop rare items.

{{image guides/mage-colored-mobs.jpg caption="A dungeon fight: the colored name above a mob shows how rare its drops can be." width=720}}

{{image guides/mage-loot-chest.jpg caption="Dungeon chest loot: Apothic gear shows its rarity by its colored border." width=514}}

### Epic reforging

The reforging table offers three options, each using more material than the last. Choose the one that gives the most gem sockets; at this tier all three usually give 4. Do not chase better stats: at this point sockets and skill points matter more than any affix, and the materials are better spent upgrading gems.

Reforge all your gear and curios so every piece has sockets.

{{image guides/mage-reforging-table.jpg caption="The Reforging Table with a sword, rarity materials and sigils." width=420}}

With these steps you can kill the Cataclysm bosses you need to open the End.

### Ancient reforging

Killing the Ender Dragon unlocks the Ancient tier, and affixes now matter. When reforging Ancient items, pick the option with at least **5 sockets and 4 affixes**. Which affixes you get does not matter. Two details:

- *Ignores Durability* does not count as one of the 4 affixes.
- Subtract any socket that an affix itself adds. An item showing 5 sockets plus 4 affixes where one affix adds a socket has 6 sockets in total.

{{image guides/mage-sockets.jpg caption="A sword with an additional socket, shown beside the Reforging Table." width=640}}

{{image guides/mage-six-sockets.jpg caption="A weapon with six empty sockets." width=640}}

Take gear and weapon with at least 5 sockets and 4 affixes to the Augmenting Table.
