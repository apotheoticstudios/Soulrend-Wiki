**Getting Started** covers the first hour of Soulrend, before any fighting. The order matters: choosing an origin and class is the hardest decision to undo, key bindings are easiest to fix before the first fight, and the quest book explains most of the pack from there.

## Step 1: Pick an origin and class

Every player chooses a **race** and a **class** when first joining. Read the pages first. There are many of both, and each one plays differently.

- [[origins|Origins]] lists every [[origins/races|race]] and class: [[origins/melee-classes|melee]], [[origins/mage-classes|mage]], [[origins/ranged-classes|ranged]] and [[origins/fusion-classes|fusion]]. A class is a style, not a lock: a mage class can still carry a sword, and combinations are possible.
- **Choose with care.** The [[progression/prologue/origin-and-class|Origin and Class]] quest gives one [[item:origins:orb_of_origin]], so the choice can be changed once.
- **For a first character, choose a race that flies.** The [[origins/races/valkyrie|Valkyrie]] has wings from the start, which makes travel and escapes much easier in the first days.

> [!NOTE]
> Resetting the skill tree is separate and cheap. An [[item:skilltree:amnesia_scroll]] returns every point, because Soulrend sets the scroll's point penalty to 0%. See [[skill-trees#Resetting|Skill Trees]].

## Step 2: Set your key bindings

Find a safe place and open **Options → Controls**. Soulrend leaves several keys unbound, and a few defaults overlap.

| Key | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Epic Fight **Switch Mode** | Bind it. It is unbound by default, and it toggles between vanilla and [[epic-fight\|Epic Fight]] combat. If a large mob grabs you, Epic Fight can switch itself off; press the key again. |
| Iron's Spellbooks **Quick Cast** 1–15 | Optional. Each key casts the spell in that position of the spell bar without the spell wheel. See [[features/magic/casting-spells\|Casting Spells]]. |
| **Zoom** (C by default) | Move it to another key. Several mods also use C by default: the Cataclysm helmet ability, Traveloptics' third ability and trade cycling. |
| **Open Screen** (F10, Raw's Visual Keybinder) | Press F10 to see every binding on a keyboard layout. It is the quickest way to find and fix two actions sharing a key. |

To find a spell's slot number, open the spell wheel (E) and count clockwise from the top; the top spell is slot 1.

Other keys to know: **I** opens the inventory with its [[features/character/inventory-tabs|tabs]], **O** the skill tree, **K** the Epic Fight skill editor, and **V** casts the selected spell.

## Step 3: Open the quest book

Do this before anything else. The quest book leads the player to almost everything in the pack and gets polished with each update. There are three ways in:

- The **Quests** tab on the inventory screen (**I**).
- **J**, which opens Soulrend's redesigned quest screen. See [[progression/prologue/new-quest-ui|New Quest UI]].
- Right-clicking a [[item:ftbquests:book]].

If you joined a world before reading the first quest, some quests may need to be completed again, so check them as you progress.

### The Prologue

Start with the [[progression/prologue|Prologue]]. Its quests are short and each takes a minute or two to read. Do these first:

- [[progression/prologue/epic-combat|Epic Combat]], the top quest, which gives four skill books for parrying, guarding and dodging (Parry, Guard, Soul-like Dodge and Step). Right-click them to learn the skills.
- [[progression/prologue/origin-and-class|Origin and Class]], which explains the Orb of Origin above.
- [[progression/prologue/leveling-up|Leveling Up]] and [[progression/prologue/the-skill-tree|The Skill Tree]], which explain how your character grows.

### Act I

Finishing the Prologue unlocks the first chapter, [[progression/act-i-awakening|Act I – Awakening]], in the list on the left. It works as the tutorial and points to the quest lines for your gear:

| If you play | Follow |
| --- | --- |
| Melee | [[progression/side-chapters/rise-of-the-champion\|Rise of the Champion]] (melee gear and weapons) |
| Magic | [[progression/side-chapters/magic-equipment\|Magic Equipment]] and Magic Academy (mage gear, spellbooks, accessories, staffs and weapons). A [[origins/fusion-classes/spellblade\|Spellblade]] needs both the melee and the magic lines. |
| Bows | Archery Armory, opened by The Ranger's Path. Pair it with a magic line, a melee line, or both. |
| Epic Fight weapons and skills | [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight\|Epic Fight]], opened by SkillBooks. It covers weapons and both active and passive skills. |

Killing three zombies ([[progression/act-i-awakening/kill-3-zombies|Kill 3 Zombies]]) unlocks the way into [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II – First Steps]].

## Step 4: Prepare for Act II

Once Act II is open, make these before setting out:

- At least **three backpacks**. The first is the [[progression/act-i-awakening/your-first-backpack|Your First Backpack]] quest; see [[features/travel-and-storage/backpacks|Backpacks]] for how they work.
- An [[item:explorerscompass:explorerscompass]], which finds structures. See [[progression/act-i-awakening/explorers-compass|Explorer's Compass]].
- A [[item:naturescompass:naturescompass]], which finds biomes.

Both compasses are cheap to craft. Many later quests begin with "Find Structure", so they get used constantly. From here the real journey begins.
