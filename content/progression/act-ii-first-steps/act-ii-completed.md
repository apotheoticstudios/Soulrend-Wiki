**Act II Completed** is the octagon quest that closes [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II – First Steps]]. In the quest book it is titled **Act III - Into The Flames**. It opens once [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-seal-of-the-underworld|The Seal of the Underworld]] is complete.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-seal-of-the-underworld|The Seal of the Underworld]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Act III - Into The Flames**.

## Rewards

- "Move into Act III", which opens [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/into-the-flames|Into the Flames]].
