**Bosses' Rise** is the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II]] branch for the three Overworld bosses of the Bosses'Rises mod: the [[Infernal Dragon]], the [[Sandworm]] and the [[bosses/act-ii/yeti|Yeti]]. Each has a Find Structure quest, a kill quest and a drop quest. Killing **any two** of the three completes the Bottom-Right Shard quest, which gives the [[item:nether_remastered:seal_piece_4]] needed for the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-seal-of-the-underworld|Seal of the Underworld]].

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/estus-flask|Estus Flask]] opens the three Find Structure quests.

## Rewards

The drop quests point ahead to armor: the Dragon Bone quest says "T4 Armor (red path) requires this!" and the Sandworm Scale quest says "T3 Armor (green path) requires this!" See [[progression/side-chapters/rise-of-the-champion|Rise of the Champion]] for the armor paths. The Titanium Bar quest gives a Titanium ore to smelt for titanium gear.

Two of the bosses also drop [[progression/side-chapters/journey-to-the-end|Legendary Eyes]] every time:

- the Infernal Dragon drops an [[item:endrem:evil_eye]];
- the Yeti drops a [[item:endrem:cold_eye]].

## Walkthrough

1. Use an [[progression/act-i-awakening/explorers-compass|Explorer's Compass]] to find the Dragon Tower, Sandworm Nest and Yeti Hideout. Each boss page has its biome.
2. Pick the two bosses that suit your gear. Each boss page has its stats, attacks and strategy:
   - [[Infernal Dragon]] (Dragon Tower)
   - [[Sandworm]] (Sandworm Nest)
   - [[bosses/act-ii/yeti|Yeti]] (Yeti Hideout)
3. After the second kill, claim the Bottom-Right Shard quest.
4. Killing the third boss as well is worth it for its drop quest and its Legendary Eye.
