**Act II – First Steps** is the second chapter of The Exalted Path and the biggest act in the quest book. Its goal is to open the Nether. Soulrend keeps the Nether closed until a player has used the [[item:nether_remastered:seal_of_the_underworld_item]], which is crafted from four seal shards, a Nether Crystal and four Bottled Nether Essence. Each shard comes from a different group of bosses, so the act sends the player through most of the Overworld's boss content.

## Branches

The act starts at [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-real-journey-begins|The Real Journey Begins]], which opens six branches:

| Branch | First quest | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bosses' Rise | [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/estus-flask\|Estus Flask]] | Estus Flasks, then the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/bosses-rise\|Infernal Dragon, Sandworm and Yeti]] and the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/defeat-the-dead-king\|Dead King]] |
| Dungeons and Combat | [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/dungeons-and-combat-bosses\|Dungeons and Combat]] | Sunleia, Kamath, The GraveWatcher and Ernos, plus links to Fallen Myths, Soulslike Champions and Aquamirae |
| Ice and Fire | [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/dread-citadel\|Ice and Fire]] | The Dread Citadel and Summoning Crystals, dragon bones, and optional Ice and Fire monsters |
| Nether essence | [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/bottled-nether-essence\|Crying Obsidian]] | The bottles and essence, then the Warden and the Otherside |
| Dungeon Crawl | [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/dungeon-crawl\|Dungeon Crawl]] | A first dungeon, a free steel weapon, and the Dark Iron Ingot that the Otherside needs |
| Magical Eyes | [[progression/side-chapters/journey-to-the-end\|Magical Eyes]] | A link to the Legendary Eyes chapter, which is needed for Act IV |

## Optional parts

- [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-otherside|The Otherside]] opens the optional [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker|Act III-b – Deeper and Darker]].
- Several quests only link to side chapters: Fallen Myth, Soulslike Champions, Aquamirae (from the Dungeons and Combat branch), Sky Arena (after the Warden), Dragonslayer (after a Dragon Bone) and Magical Eyes.
- The quests marked "(optional)" in their subtitle, such as Ernos, Cyclops and Hydra, are not needed for anything.

## Bosses in this act

See [[bosses/act-ii|Act II Bosses]] for every boss page. The quest descriptions give Sunleia and Kamath as level 30 and Frostmaw as level 60.
