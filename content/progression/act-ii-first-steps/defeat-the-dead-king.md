**Defeat The Dead King** is a three-quest line in [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II]] about the Catacombs, an Iron's Spellbooks dungeon. The first fight is [[The Dead King]] (level 40). The last quest asks for the scroll that summons the [[Enraged Dead King]], a level 200 version of the same boss. Nothing else in the act depends on this line.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/estus-flask|Estus Flask]]

## Objectives

| Quest | Requires | Tasks | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Find Structure: Catacombs ("A lot of undeads...") | Estus Flask | Find `irons_spellbooks:catacombs` | 2 XP levels |
| Defeat The Dead King ("The throne does not make the king...") | Catacombs | Kill [[The Dead King]] | A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Magic tables\|Ink_irons]] |
| "...the king makes the throne." | Dead King | Have a **Call Forth The Dead King** scroll, and kill the [[Enraged Dead King]] | One upgrade orb of your choice, one rune stack of your choice |

> [!LORE] Defeat The Dead King (Act II)
> Once a great leader, The Dead King descended into madness along with thousands of his people...

> [!LORE] "...the king makes the throne." (Act II)
> Alas, the crazed servant of Mortusest has returned...

## Rewards

- Defeat The Dead King: common, uncommon, epic or legendary ink.
- The scroll quest: a choice from the [[progression/reward-tables#Magic tables|Orb]] table and a choice from the [[progression/reward-tables#Magic tables|Magic Runes]] table.

## Walkthrough

1. Find the Catacombs with an Explorer's Compass and fight through to the Dead King.
2. Search the Catacombs chests for a Call Forth The Dead King scroll. It only appears there.
3. Keep the scroll in your inventory until the quest's scroll task ticks off, then cast it to summon the Enraged Dead King. Casting a scroll uses it up.

> [!CAUTION]
> The Enraged Dead King is level 200, far above any other Act II boss. Read [[Enraged Dead King#Strategy]] before casting the scroll.
