The **Dungeons and Combat** branch of [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II]] covers the bosses of the Dungeons and Combat mod. Its two required fights are [[Sunleia]] and [[Kamath]], both level 30: killing both completes the Bottom-Left Shard quest, which gives the [[item:nether_remastered:seal_piece_3]]. The branch also has [[The GraveWatcher]], the optional [[enemies/act-ii/ernos|Ernos]], gear from each boss, and the links to three Tales of Soulrend chapters.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-real-journey-begins|The Real Journey Begins]] opens the **Dungeons and Combat** checkmark. Its "Move to Dungeons and Combat Chapter" button points at a chapter that isn't in the quest book.

## Walkthrough

1. Find the Pantheon of Sunleia (deserts) and the Ruins of Kamath (forests and plains) with an Explorer's Compass. See [[Sunleia#Location]] and [[Kamath#Location]].
2. Kill both bosses, then claim the Bottom-Left Shard. Both are level 30, so they are the easiest seal bosses.
3. To fight Sunleia again, visit the Forgotten Sands and make a Blessed Beetle:

4. Sunleia's essence goes into the Hand of Sunleia and the two necklaces. Soulrend changes these recipes:
