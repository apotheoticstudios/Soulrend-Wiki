The **Top-Right Shard** quest asks for the [[item:nether_remastered:seal_piece_2]], one of the four pieces of the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-seal-of-the-underworld|Seal of the Underworld]]. The shard's main source is [[Frostmaw]], a level 60 ice beast: killing it completes The Frostmaw quest in the [[progression/side-chapters/fallen-myths|Fallen Myths]] chapter, which hands out the shard. Witches drop it too, rarely.

## Requirements

Both of:

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/act-i-complete|Act I Complete]]
- the **Fallen Myths** checkmark, the first quest of the Fallen Myths chapter. It is reached from Act II through Dungeons and Combat → Fallen Myth (see [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/dungeons-and-combat-bosses#Side-chapter links|Dungeons and Combat Bosses]]).

## Objectives

Have a [[item:nether_remastered:seal_piece_2]] (in game it is called "Mysterious Shard").

> [!LORE] Top-Right Shard (Act II)
> Can be obtained by defeating the Frostmaw (see Fallen Myths quest chapter), or a rare drop from Witches, slay and loot to find one.

## Rewards

- [[item:dungeons_and_combat:blessed_gold_ingot]]
- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Flawed Gems]] (one rare gem)

## Walkthrough

1. Open Fallen Myths from the Fallen Myth link quest and click its first checkmark.
2. Find a sleeping Frostmaw in a snowy biome. See [[Frostmaw#Strategy]]: stealing its Ice Crystal first removes its ranged attacks.
3. Kill it. The Frostmaw quest gives the shard, a Wisdom Scroll, an Ice Sickle and a spell scroll.
4. The Top-Right Shard quest completes as soon as the shard is in your inventory.

> [!TIP]
> Witches have two separate 10% chances to drop the shard: one from Nether Remastered and one from Soulrend's extra mob drops. Witch farming is an alternative to fighting Frostmaw.
