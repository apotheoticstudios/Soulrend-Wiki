**The Warden** is an [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II]] quest to kill the [[Warden]], the level 60 guardian of the Deep Dark. The Warden is the only source of the [[item:nether_remastered:seal_crystal]], the centerpiece of the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-seal-of-the-underworld|Seal of the Underworld]]. The quest also opens the route to the Otherside and a Sky Arena link.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/bottled-nether-essence|Crying Obsidian]]

## Objectives

Kill the Warden.

> [!LORE] The Warden (Act II)
> The Warden is the guardian of the Deep Dark. A spooky and awful place filled with all sorts of new blocks and mobs.

## Rewards

- 10 XP levels
- [[item:minecraft:echo_shard]]
- 5 [[item:dungeons_and_combat:estus_flask]]
- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Rare Loot|Rare Loot]]

## The Nether Crystal

The **Nether Crystal** quest ("The Final Piece") opens after either The Warden or the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/bottled-nether-essence|Obsidian Infused Bottle]] quest. It asks for a [[item:nether_remastered:seal_crystal]] and gives 5 XP levels, a Flawed Gem of your choice and a roll on Rare Loot.

> [!LORE] Nether Crystal (Act II)
> The Nether Crystal can be obtained by defeating the Warden (30% chance).

Soulrend's extra mob drops give the Warden a **50%** chance to drop the crystal, not the 30% in the quest text.

## Walkthrough

1. Find a Deep Dark biome deep underground. The Warden appears when Sculk Shriekers are triggered there. See [[Warden#Location]].
2. Kill it. Every kill also drops a [[item:efn:deepdark_heart]].
3. If no crystal drops, summon and kill another Warden. Each kill is a separate 50% roll.
