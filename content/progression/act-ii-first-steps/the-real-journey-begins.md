**The Real Journey Begins** is the first quest of [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II – First Steps]]. It is a checkmark with no reward. Its description sets the goal of the act: complete the other Act II quests to unlock the hidden shards that open the Nether.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/act-i-complete|Act I Complete]] (the "Act II - First Steps" quest at the end of Act I)

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **The Real Journey Begins**.

> [!LORE] The Real Journey Begins (Act II)
> So, you've proven that you can survive.
>
> The undead were merely the beginning. Beyond these first trials lies a vast world waiting to be explored, filled with opportunity, danger, and adventure.
>
> It's time to take your first true steps into the Realms of Soulrendia.
>
> To unlock the hidden shards required to access the Nether, you must complete dependencies quests in Act 2
