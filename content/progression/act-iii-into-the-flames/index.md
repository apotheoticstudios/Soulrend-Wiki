**Act III – Into The Flames** is the third chapter of The Exalted Path and the first one in the Nether. It opens after the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-seal-of-the-underworld|Seal of the Underworld]]. The act has two halves: Nether bosses and structures, reached through the Nether itself, and **the Eight Sins**, the Cataclysm bosses that each live in their own dimension. Despite its text, the act's last quest doesn't need any Sin: it opens [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]] once five [[progression/side-chapters/journey-to-the-end|Legendary Eye]] quests are done and the player has entered the End.

All of these depend only on [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/into-the-flames|Into the Flames]], the act's first quest.

Other Act III quests:

- **Empowered Remnant** ("A Pain to Make..."): have an [[item:simplyswords:empowered_remnant]] for 1 XP level. See [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/into-the-flames#Empowered Remnant|Into the Flames]].
- **Nautilus Shell**: have a [[item:minecraft:nautilus_shell]] for a [[item:cataclysm:coral_chunk]]. Its text says Nautilus Shells come from the Sunken City, the Eye of Abyss dimension.
- **The Nether** and **The Caverns**: checkmarks that link to the Nether and Caverns chapters of [[progression/side-chapters/fabled-realms|Fabled Realms]].

## The way to Act IV

The last quest of the act is an octagon titled **Act IV - Beyond Reality**. See [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/act-iv-beyond-reality-gate|Act IV – Beyond Reality Gate]]: it needs any five of the sixteen eye quests in [[progression/side-chapters/journey-to-the-end|Journey To The End]], and then a visit to the End.

> [!NOTE]
> The Eight Sins quest says "Defeat at least Four Sins to unlock Act IV", but no Sin kill is required. The gate only checks the Legendary Eye quests and the End.

## Bosses in this act

See [[bosses/act-iii|Act III Bosses]]. The Caverns chapter adds the five Alex's Caves bosses, such as [[Hullbreaker]] and [[Magnetron]].
