**Burning Ashes** is an [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames|Act III]] quest for a [[item:cataclysm:burning_ashes]]. It follows **Find Structure: Nether Fortress**, which completes with the vanilla advancement for entering a fortress. Soulrend's extra mob drops give [[Ignited Berserker]]s a 25% chance to drop Burning Ashes.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/into-the-flames|Into the Flames]], then Find Structure: Nether Fortress

## Objectives

| Quest | Task | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find Structure: Nether Fortress | Enter a Nether Fortress (the "A Terrible Fortress" advancement) | 2 XP levels, Structure Loot |
| Burning Ashes | Have a Burning Ashes | 3 Estus Flasks |

> [!LORE] Burning Ashes (Act III)
> You'll need Burning Ashes for one of Eyes, dropped from Ignited Berserkers in Nether Fortresses with a 25% chance.

## Walkthrough

1. Find a Nether Fortress.
2. Kill Ignited Berserkers until one drops Burning Ashes.
