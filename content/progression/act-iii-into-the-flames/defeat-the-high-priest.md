**Defeat The High Priest** is a three-quest line in [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames|Act III]]: find the Cathedral, kill the [[High Priest]] there, and collect his [[item:dungeons_and_combat:fleshy_cloth]].

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/into-the-flames|Into the Flames]]

## Objectives

| Quest | Task | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find Structure: The Cathedral | Find `dungeons_and_combat:the_cathedral` | 2 XP levels, Structure Loot |
| Defeat The High Priest | Kill the [[High Priest]] | 3 XP levels, a roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables\|Flawed Gems]] |
| Fleshy Cloth | Have a [[item:dungeons_and_combat:fleshy_cloth]] | 1 XP level, 2 [[item:dungeons_and_combat:crimson_flask]] |

## Walkthrough

1. Find the Cathedral with an Explorer's Compass. See [[High Priest#Location]].
2. Kill the High Priest; see [[High Priest#Strategy]].
3. Keep spare Fleshy Cloth: Soulrend's recipe for the Forlorn Hollows cave key uses four (see [[progression/side-chapters/fabled-realms#The Caverns|The Caverns]]).
