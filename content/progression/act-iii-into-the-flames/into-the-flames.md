**Into the Flames** is the first quest of [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames|Act III]]. It is a checkmark that gives a [[item:simplyswords:runic_tablet]] and opens every other quest in the act. Its text sets the scene for the Nether and hints at reforging gear.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/act-ii-completed|Act II Completed]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Into the Flames**.

> [!LORE] Into the Flames (Act III)
> You've conquered the challenges of the Overworld and grown stronger with every battle.
>
> But beyond the safety of familiar lands lies a realm untouched by mercy. Rivers of lava, ancient fortresses, and monstrous beings await those brave enough to enter.
>
> The Nether is no place for the weak. Step forward and face the flames.
>
> Perhaps it is time to reforge your arsenal.

## Rewards

- [[item:simplyswords:runic_tablet]]
- "Move to The Nether Chapter", which opens the Hellish Dungeons quest (see [[progression/side-chapters/fabled-realms#The Nether|Fabled Realms]]).
- "Move to Simply NightFall Chapter", which points at a chapter that isn't in the quest book.

The Runic Tablet is the template for Soulrend's Runefused Gem smithing recipes. More tablets cost 20 Ice Keys each in the [[progression/side-chapters/sky-arena|Sky Arena]] shop.

## Empowered Remnant

The **Empowered Remnant** quest ("A Pain to Make...") also depends on Into the Flames. It asks for an [[item:simplyswords:empowered_remnant]] and gives 1 XP level.

> [!LORE] Empowered Remnant (Act III)
> Empowered Remnants are created in Mechanical Infusion Anvil or by melting down unique weapons.
>
> For better usage, follow Simply NightFall Chapter

The Simply NightFall chapter it mentions isn't in the quest book. See [[features/gear-and-crafting/empowered-remnants|Empowered Remnants]].
