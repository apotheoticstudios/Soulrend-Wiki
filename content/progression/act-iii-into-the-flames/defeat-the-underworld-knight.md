**Defeat The Underworld Knight** is a three-quest line in [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames|Act III]]: find the Underworld Arena, kill the [[Underworld Knight]], and get his [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]]. The sword matters beyond the quest: picking one up is what unlocks the subclass keystones of the [[skill-trees|skill trees]].

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/into-the-flames|Into the Flames]]

## Objectives

| Quest | Task | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find Structure: Underworld Area | Find `block_factorys_bosses:underworld_arena` | 2 XP levels, Structure Loot |
| Defeat The Underworld Knight | Kill the [[Underworld Knight]] | 3 XP levels, a roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables\|Flawed Gems]] |
| Knight Sword | Have a [[item:block_factorys_bosses:knight_sword]] | 1 XP level, 2 [[item:dungeons_and_combat:crimson_flask]] |

## Walkthrough

1. Find the Underworld Arena in the Nether with an Explorer's Compass. See [[Underworld Knight#Location]].
2. Kill the Underworld Knight; see [[Underworld Knight#Strategy]].
3. Pick up the Knight Sword. The skill trees count it once it has been picked up.
