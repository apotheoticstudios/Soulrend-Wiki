**Act I Complete** is the octagon-shaped quest that closes [[progression/act-i-awakening|Act I – Awakening]]. In the quest book it is titled **Act II - First Steps**, with "Act I Complete" as its subtitle. Clicking its checkmark gives 5 XP levels and a button into Act II.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/kill-3-zombies|Kill 3 Zombies]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Act II - First Steps**.

## Rewards

- 5 XP levels
- "Move into Act II", which opens [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-real-journey-begins|The Real Journey Begins]].

## Next

- [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-real-journey-begins|The Real Journey Begins]], the first quest of [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II – First Steps]].
- The four seal-shard quests of Act II also list this quest as a requirement.
