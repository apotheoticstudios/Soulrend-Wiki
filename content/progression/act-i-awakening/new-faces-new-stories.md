**New Faces, New Stories** is a checkmark quest on the main line of [[progression/act-i-awakening|Act I – Awakening]]. It points the player toward villages and other settlements, and its rewards are buttons to two side chapters: [[progression/side-chapters/factions|Factions]], about the eight Valarian kingdoms, and The Overworld, a list of Overworld structures (see [[progression/side-chapters/fabled-realms|Fabled Realms]]).

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/find-a-place-to-call-home|Find a Place To Call Home]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **New Faces, New Stories**.

> [!LORE] New Faces, New Stories (Act I)
> The Realms of Soulrendia are far from empty. Beyond the wilderness lie settlements, travelers, people... and dangerous monsters...
>
> Set off on a jorney, and seek signs of civilization. You may find new allies, valuable resources, forgotten knowledge and more!

## Rewards

- "Move to Factions Chapter", which opens the Factions quest.
- "Move to The Overworld Chapter", which opens the Adventurer's Refuges quest.
