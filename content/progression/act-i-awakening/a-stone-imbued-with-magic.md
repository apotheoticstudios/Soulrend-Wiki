**A Stone Imbued with Magic** is an Act I checkmark quest about gems: the magic stones that monsters and quests drop. Its reward is a button to the [[progression/side-chapters/reforging|Reforging]] chapter, which teaches gem sockets, sigils and the reforging tables.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/kill-3-zombies|Kill 3 Zombies]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **A Stone Imbued with Magic**.

> [!LORE] A Stone Imbued with Magic (Act I)
> While searching through the remains of the undead, you discovered something unusual.
>
> This strange gemstone appears to be infused with magical energy. Its purpose is unclear, but one thing is certain, such an object should not exist naturally. Perhaps the creatures of the Realms of Soulrendia carry more than just rotten flesh.

## Rewards

- "Move to Reforging Chapter", which opens the Affix Enchanting quest.

See [[features/gear-and-crafting/gems-and-sigils|Gems and Sigils]] and [[features/gear-and-crafting/reforging-and-affixes|Reforging and Affixes]].
