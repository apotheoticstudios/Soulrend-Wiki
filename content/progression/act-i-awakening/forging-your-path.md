**Forging Your Path** is the Act I hub for gear. It is a checkmark that links to the armor and enchanting chapters, and it opens four more checkmarks, one for each playstyle: magic, archery, Epic Fight skill books and Epic Knights armor. None of them is required for the main line; they exist to point new players at the right side chapters.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/find-a-place-to-call-home|Find a Place To Call Home]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Forging Your Path**.

> [!LORE] Forging Your Path (Act I)
> A simple traveler can only go so far. Sooner or later, every adventurer must sharpen their blade and strengthen their defenses.
>
> The challenges ahead will test both your skill and your equipment. Make sure you're prepared.

## Rewards

- "Move to Rise Of The Champion Chapter": [[progression/side-chapters/rise-of-the-champion|Rise of the Champion]], the armor tiers.
- "Move to Enchanting Module Chapter": [[progression/side-chapters/enchanting|Enchanting Module]], Apotheosis enchanting.

## The four paths

All four depend on Forging Your Path and are checkmarks.

### The Arcane Path

Opens the Magic Academy and [[progression/side-chapters/magic-equipment|Magic Equipment]] chapters (see [[progression/side-chapters/pursuit-of-mastery|Pursuit of Mastery]] for Magic Academy).

> [!LORE] The Arcane Path (Act I)
> If the life of a warrior does not appeal to you, there are other ways to grow stronger.
>
> Magic is a force woven into the very fabric of the Realms of Soulrendia. Those willing to study its secrets may wield powers far greater than any sword could offer. The choice is yours.

### The Ranger's Path

Opens the Archery Armory chapter, about bows (see [[progression/side-chapters/pursuit-of-mastery|Pursuit of Mastery]]).

> [!LORE] The Ranger's Path (Act I)
> Why risk your life in close combat when your enemies can be defeated from a distance?
>
> The bow rewards those with a steady hand and a keen eye. In the Realms of Soulrendia, a well placed arrow can mean the difference between life and death.

### SkillBooks

Opens the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] chapter of skill books.

> [!LORE] SkillBooks (Act I)
> Skill Books allow you to develop your abilities and become more than an ordinary adventurer. Choose your strengths wisely, for every decision will shape your journey.

### Epic Knights

Its "Move to Epic Knight Chapter" button points at a chapter that isn't in the quest book, so it does nothing.

> [!LORE] Epic Knights (Act I)
> For those who do not hide in the shadows, nor rely on unstable magic. The true test of a warrior lies in the weight of steel and the cold embrace of heavy armor.
>
> If you seek to master the art of medieval warfare, forge indestructible armor, and wield legendary weapons, your journey begins here.
