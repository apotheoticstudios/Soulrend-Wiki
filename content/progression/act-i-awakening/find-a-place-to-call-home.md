**Find a Place To Call Home** ("A Nomad's Life") is the first quest of [[progression/act-i-awakening|Act I – Awakening]]. It is a checkmark that gives a set of iron tools, and it opens the rest of the act.

## Requirements

- [[progression/prologue/welcome-to-soulrend|Welcome to Soulrend]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Awoken But Lost**.

> [!LORE] Find a Place To Call Home (Act I)
> You've found yourself in The Realms of Soulrendia. Filled with many treasures and adventures!
>
> First things first though, make sure you have a home to come back to after a long day of adventure. Or become a wanderer that travels without a permenant place to call home.

## Rewards

- [[item:minecraft:iron_pickaxe]]
- [[item:minecraft:iron_axe]]
- [[item:minecraft:iron_shovel]]

## Walkthrough

1. Claim the tools. The quest doesn't check for a base, so there is nothing else to do.
2. Choose a base site, or keep moving. A [[progression/prologue/waystones-and-warp-items|Waystone]] at the base makes it easy to come back.
