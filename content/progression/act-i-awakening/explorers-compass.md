The **Explorer's Compass** quest ("Never be lost again!") asks for an [[item:explorerscompass:explorerscompass]], a compass that can search for any structure by name. Many later quests start with "Find Structure", so the compass is one of the most useful items in the pack. The quest comes after a short **Diamond Pickaxe** quest.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/your-first-backpack|Your First Backpack!]], then Diamond Pickaxe

## Diamond Pickaxe

**Diamond Pickaxe** ("A trusty tool") asks for a [[item:minecraft:diamond_pickaxe]] and gives 2 [[item:minecraft:diamond]]s and 1 [[item:minecraft:quartz]].

> [!LORE] Diamond Pickaxe (Act I)
> Of course you'll end up needing a Diamond Pickaxe. How else are you gonna mine obsidian for your Nether Portal. You'll have to earn the right to enter the Nether though!

The Nether is closed until the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-seal-of-the-underworld|Seal of the Underworld]] in Act II.

## Objectives

Have an Explorer's Compass.

> [!LORE] Explorer's Compass (Act I)
> This compass with be a great help in us finding structures. Perfect for adventure and perfect to help us find hidden secrets.

## Rewards

- 10 XP levels

## Walkthrough

1. Craft a Diamond Pickaxe, then claim its two diamonds.
2. Craft the compass:

   {{recipes explorerscompass:explorerscompass}}

3. Right-click the compass to pick a structure, and it points the way.

## Next

- No quests depend on this one, but the compass is the fastest way to finish Act II's "Find Structure" quests.
