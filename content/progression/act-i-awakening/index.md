**Act I – Awakening** is the first chapter of The Exalted Path, Soulrend's main quest line. It starts as soon as [[progression/prologue/welcome-to-soulrend|Welcome to Soulrend]] in the Prologue is done. The act is short: one main line ends with killing three zombies, and most other quests are signposts that open side chapters such as [[progression/side-chapters/rise-of-the-champion|Rise of the Champion]], [[progression/side-chapters/factions|Factions]] and the weapon-path chapters.

## Main line

1. [[progression/act-i-awakening/find-a-place-to-call-home|Find a Place To Call Home]]: a checkmark that gives iron tools.
2. [[progression/act-i-awakening/new-faces-new-stories|New Faces, New Stories]]: a checkmark that opens Factions and The Overworld.
3. [[progression/act-i-awakening/kill-3-zombies|Kill 3 Zombies]]: the only fight in the act.
4. [[progression/act-i-awakening/act-i-complete|Act II – First Steps]]: the act's closing quest, which opens [[progression/act-ii-first-steps|Act II]].
