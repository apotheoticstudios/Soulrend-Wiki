**Kill 3 Zombies** ("What in the world is that thing?") is the only combat quest in [[progression/act-i-awakening|Act I – Awakening]]. Killing three zombies finishes the act's main line: the quest opens the Act II link quest and two side-chapter links.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/new-faces-new-stories|New Faces, New Stories]]

## Objectives

Kill 3 [[enemies/act-i/zombie|Zombies]] (`minecraft:zombie`). Other zombie types, such as husks and drowned, don't count.

> [!LORE] Kill 3 Zombies (Act I)
> You've never seen a creature like this before. It walks like a human, yet there's no life left in its eyes. Whatever curse befell these poor souls...

## Rewards

- 3 XP levels
- A roll on the [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Cracked Gems]] table (one common gem)

## Walkthrough

1. Wait for night, or explore caves, and kill three zombies.
2. Hold onto the gem: [[progression/act-i-awakening/a-stone-imbued-with-magic|A Stone Imbued with Magic]] is about gems like it.
