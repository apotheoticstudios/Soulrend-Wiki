**A Hunter's Beginning** is an Act I checkmark quest. Its reward is a button to the [[progression/side-chapters/hunters-journal|Hunter's Journal]], a side chapter of kill-count quests for common mobs.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/kill-3-zombies|Kill 3 Zombies]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **A Hunter's Beginning**.

> [!LORE] A Hunter's Beginning (Act I)
> No one becomes a legendary monster hunter overnight.
>
> Every foe defeated brings valuable experience. Keep fighting, keep learning, and your name may one day be remembered among the greatest.

## Rewards

- A chapter button that opens The Hunter's Journal quest.

> [!NOTE]
> The button is labeled "Move to Reforging Chapter", but it opens the Hunter's Journal.
