**Fallen Myths** ("Explore and Exile") is the side chapter for the bosses and structures of Mowzie's Mobs. It matters for the main line: its first checkmark is one of the two requirements of Act II's [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/frostmaw-shard|Top-Right Shard]] quest, and its Frostmaw quest hands out that shard.

## Requirements

- **Fallen Myth**, a link quest in Act II's [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/dungeons-and-combat-bosses#Side-chapter links|Dungeons and Combat]] branch, opens the chapter's **Fallen Myths** checkmark. The other quests depend on that checkmark.

> [!LORE] The Ferrous Wroughtnaut (Fallen Myths)
> Only the bravest of adventurers would dare to challenge the Ferrous Wroughtnaut. Upon defeat, the Wroughtnaut will drop its Wrought Helm and its Axe of a Thousand Metals for the victor.
>
> Right clicking with the axe attacks in a large arc, and shift-right clicking will create a shockwave that brings targets closer to you. Neither tool can break.

> [!LORE] The Sculptor's Challenge (Fallen Myths)
> Tongbi waits in the monastery's courtyard, challenging visitors to a parkour test. Never before has a disciple reached the top of this arduous climb, but the one who does will be worthy of the power of the Earthrend Monks.
>
> The Sculptor will grant the completer a powerful artifact called the Earthrend Gauntlet, which gives its wielder control over earth magic.

## Tips

- The structure descriptions give their biomes: the Monastery is in snowy mountains, the Wrought Chamber deep underground, and the Umvuthana Grove in savannas and dry grasslands.
- The Ferrous Wroughtnaut (level 25) is the easiest fight here; Frostmaw and Umvuthi are both level 60.
