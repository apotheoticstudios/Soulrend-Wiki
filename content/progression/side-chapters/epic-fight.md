**Epic Fight** is the Legendary Armoury chapter for combat skills. Every quest asks for one Epic Fight [[item:epicfight:skillbook]] with a specific skill, or one of the enchanted books that teach the YAMATO's skills, and pays out XP levels. The chapter is a checklist: no quest depends on another except the opening checkmark.

## Requirements

- The **SkillBooks** checkmark under [[progression/act-i-awakening/forging-your-path|Forging Your Path]] links to **EpicFight SkillBooks**, whose subtitle reads: "While the skillbooks listed here can be crafted, many rarer ones must be found in dangerous structures or obtained as drops from powerful enemies". It gives 20 experience points.

Soulrend adds crafting recipes for 52 skill books; JEI shows them in game.

## Skill books

Mods: Epic Fight (EF), Weapons of Miracle (WoM), EpicFight Nightfall (EFN), EpicFight-Resurrection (EFR) and Epic Fight Extra (EFX).

| Reward | Skills |
| --- | --- |
| 3 XP levels | Berserker, Catharsis, Demolition Leap, Forbidden Strength, Meteor Slam, Technician (EF); Aqua Maneuver, Avatar of Might, Back and... FORTH!, Dodge Master, Dopamine, Natural Sprinter, Punishment Kick, Shooting Style, Spider Techniques (WoM) |
| 5 XP levels | Adrenaline Fiend, Emergency Escape, Endurance, Hyper Vitality, Parrying, Revelation (EF); Arrow Tenacity, Counter Attack, Dread Full Buster, Ender Step, Lunatic Vivacity, Manipulator, Perfect Bulwark, Precise Roll, Ravanger Force, Shulker Cloak, Soul Protection (WoM); Gravity Stomp, Parry, Parry Master, Soul Hunt (EFN); Fatal Flash, Phantom (EFR); Combat Mastery I (EFX) |
| 10 XP levels | Vengeance (EF); All Eyes on Me, All Eyes on You, Critical Knowledge, Dancing Blade, Ender Obscuris, Essence Extortion, Heart Shield, Latent Retribution, Lethal Focus, Mindset, Time Travel, Voodoo Magic (WoM); Precise Parry (EFN) |

## Weapon skill books

These quests ask for an enchanted book from EpicFight Nightfall that gives a weapon its skill:

| Quest | Enchantment | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yamato-Summoned Sword | Yamato Summoned Sword | 10 XP levels |
| Summoned Sword End | Yamato Summoned Sword End | 10 XP levels |
| Yamato-Judgment Cut End | Yamato Judgement Cut End | 10 XP levels |
| Yamato-Guard | Yamato Guard | 10 XP levels |
| Yamato-Doppelganger | Yamato Doppelganger | 10 XP levels |
| Yamato-Heavy Rain | Yamato Heavy Rain | 10 XP levels |
| Crimson Moon - Demonized | Scythe Enhance | 5 XP levels |

> [!NOTE]
> A second quest is also titled "Yamato-Heavy Rain", but it asks for the Broad Blade Enhance book. It gives 10 XP levels.

The YAMATO itself is a reward of [[progression/act-v-duties/defeat-the-scarlet-hunter|Defeat The Scarlet Hunter]] in Act V.
