**Soulslike Champions** is the side chapter for the bosses of Soulslike Weaponry. Most quests ask for the boss's **Lord Soul**, the unique soul each boss drops, and some also ask for the kill or the summoning items. Four boss quests together open the Chaos Orb, which summons the last two bosses at once.

## Requirements

- The **Soulslike Champions** link quest in Act II (after Fallen Myth) opens **The Guiding Moon** ("Craft a moonstone compass, revealing the location of key structures"). Its Moonstone Compass task is optional; the checkmark alone completes it and gives 2 [[item:soulsweapons:lost_soul]]s.

The [[item:soulsweapons:boss_compass]] points to this mod's structures.

## Summoning

The quest descriptions say how the altar bosses are called:

- The [[Returning Knight]] is summoned with a Lost Soul in an Old Moon Altar.
- The [[Fallen Icon]] is summoned with an Essence of Eventide in an Old Moon Altar.
- The [[Monarch of Chaos]] is summoned with a Shard of Uncertainty in a Blackstone Pedestal.
- Throwing a Chaos Orb summons the [[Day Stalker]] and the [[Night Prowler]] together.

## Levels

Soulrend sets level floors for these bosses: the Returning Knight, Fallen Icon, Day Stalker and Night Prowler are at least level 50, the Frenzied Shade at least 60, the Old Champion's Remains at least 100, and [[The Decaying King]] and the Monarch of Chaos level 200. The Decaying Kingdom generates in the Nether, so that line waits for [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames|Act III]].

## Tips

- The Decaying King's [[item:soulsweapons:withered_demon_heart]] is also part of the [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-otherside|Heart of the Deep]] recipe. Craft the heart only after this quest has counted the Withered Demon Heart.
- The Wither quest asks for a Nether Star, which the [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/confront-the-wither|Wither]] drops.
