**Sky Arena** is a side chapter for the Sky Arena mod's battle arenas. It has two parts: a ladder of trophy quests for clearing the arenas at higher difficulties, and a shop of repeatable quests that trade [[item:skyarena:ice_key]]s for rewards.

## Requirements

- The **Sky Arena** checkmark in Act II, which opens after [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-warden|The Warden]], links to **Challenge yourself on SkyArena!** (1 XP level). Every quest in the chapter starts there.

> [!LORE] Challenge yourself on SkyArena!
> Defeat enemies in the Ice Arenas and Sky Arenas to obtain keys. Trade them in the shop above, and conquer the highest difficulty to claim the ultimate trophy.
>
> All available arenas drop only Ice Keys
> (Don't use keys on chest)

## Arenas and trophies

| Quest | Task | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find Ice Arena | Find `skyarena:ice_arena` | 3 XP levels |
| Find Sky Arena | Find `skyarena:sky_arena` | 3 XP levels |
| Oak Trophy | [[item:skyarena:oak_trophy]] | 3 XP levels |
| Stone Trophy | [[item:skyarena:stone_trophy]] | 5 XP levels, Chipped Gems |
| Iron Trophy | [[item:skyarena:iron_trophy]] | 10 XP levels, two rolls on Chipped Gems |
| Gold Trophy | [[item:skyarena:gold_trophy]] | 15 XP levels, Flawed Gems |
| Diamond Trophy | [[item:skyarena:diamond_trophy]] | 25 XP levels, Flawed Gems and a Flawed Gem of your choice |
| Netherite Trophy | [[item:skyarena:netherite_trophy]] | 50 XP levels, a Perfect Gem of your choice |

Each trophy quest needs the one before it. The Oak Trophy is for the lowest difficulty and the Netherite Trophy for the highest.

## The Ice Key shop

Every shop quest takes **one** Ice Key and can be repeated. Click the quest's reward to trade.

## Tips

- Besides the arenas, the Act II **Heart of Deepdark** quest trades one [[item:efn:deepdark_heart]] for 8 Ice Keys, and it repeats. Every [[Warden]] drops a heart.
- "Choose your Simply Sword" costs the same single key as the random draw, so it is always the better deal once it opens.
