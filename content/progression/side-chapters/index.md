The **side chapters** are every chapter of the quest book that isn't an act. Most are opened by a link quest in one of the acts: a checkmark whose reward is a button to the chapter. None of them is needed to finish the main line, but three feed into it: [[progression/side-chapters/fallen-myths|Fallen Myths]] gives the Top-Right seal shard, [[progression/side-chapters/journey-to-the-end|Journey To The End]] opens Act IV, and [[progression/side-chapters/rise-of-the-champion|Rise of the Champion]] is the armor guide.

## Links that go nowhere

Four link buttons in the acts point at chapters that aren't in the quest book: Epic Knights (Act I), Dungeons and Combat and Soulslike Weaponry (Act II), and Simply NightFall (Act III).
