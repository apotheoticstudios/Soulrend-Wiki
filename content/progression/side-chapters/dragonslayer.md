**Dragonslayer** ("Legendary Lizards") is the side chapter for Ice and Fire's dragons. It asks for one kill of each dragon type, then leads to the dragon radars, the Dragonslayer armor pieces, a dragon egg and the three Dragonforges.

## Requirements

- Act II's **Dragon Bone** quest (find an [[item:iceandfire:dragonbone]], usually from a dragon skeleton) links here. Its **Dragons** checkmark gives a [[item:iceandfire:bestiary]].

> [!LORE] Dragons (Dragonslayer)
> Dragons crawl the lands, feirce elementals with ancient lineage. You will encounter numerous through your journeys, slay them to gain rewards and grow in immense power.
>
> To start questline, find dragon bone from death dragons

## Quests

### Dragon kills

| Quest | Task | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire Dragon ("Slay a Flame Wyvern") | Kill a [[Fire Dragon]] | An epic Solar gem, 5 XP levels |
| Ice Dragon ("Slay a Frost Wyvern") | Kill an [[Ice Dragon]] | An epic Lunar gem, 5 XP levels |
| Lightning Dragon ("Slay a Thunder Wyvern") | Kill a [[Lightning Dragon]] | An epic Lightning gem, 5 XP levels |
| Dragonslayer | All three kills | [[item:fantasy_armor:dragonslayer_chestplate]] (one socket), a Chipped Gem of your choice |

### Radars

| Quest | Requires | Task | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dormant Radar | Dragons | [[item:dragonradarmod:dormant_radar]] | None |
| Fire Radar | Fire Dragon | [[item:dragonradarmod:fire_radar]] | 2 red Dragon Scales, 1 XP level |
| Ice Radar | Ice Dragon | [[item:dragonradarmod:ice_radar]] | 2 blue Dragon Scales, 1 XP level |
| Lightning Radar | Lightning Dragon | [[item:dragonradarmod:lightning_radar]] | 2 electric Dragon Scales, 1 XP level |
| Master Radar | All four radars | [[item:dragonradarmod:master_radar]] | [[item:fantasy_armor:dragonslayer_helmet]] (one socket), 2 XP levels |

Each radar finds dragons of its type; the Master Radar finds any type. See [[items/dragons/dragon-utility#Using a radar|Dragon Utility]] for how the radars work.

> [!LORE] Your First Dragon Egg (Dragonslayer)
> To obtain a dragon egg, one must seek out a female 4+ stage dragon, which can be found sleeping in treasure-filled caverns deep underground. Once you've found one, kill it and right-click the body with an empty hand to collect loot, and it will drop a dragon egg of random color depending on the species

Your First Dragon Forge explains that a Dragonforge is a 3 × 3 × 3 build: 1 Dragonforge Core, 1 Aperture, 8 Dragon Bone Blocks and 17 Dragonforge Bricks. The forge quests only check for one of each block.
