**Backpacks** is the Legendary Armoury chapter for Traveler's Backpacks. Every quest asks for one backpack item: an upgrade, a tier upgrade, or one of the decorated backpack types. All of them hang off the first quest, so they can be done in any order.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/your-first-backpack|Your First Backpack!]] in Act I links to **Personalizing Your Bag** ("A fresh start"), which asks for a [[item:travelersbackpack:blank_upgrade]] and gives 24 Leather.

## Tier upgrades

Each tier upgrade raises the backpack's storage and upgrade slots. The quest text says they are applied in a smithing table.

| Quest | Rewards |
| --- | --- |
| [[item:travelersbackpack:iron_tier_upgrade]] | A Block of Iron |
| [[item:travelersbackpack:gold_tier_upgrade]] (after Iron) | A Block of Gold |
| [[item:travelersbackpack:diamond_tier_upgrade]] (after Gold) | A Block of Diamond |
| [[item:travelersbackpack:netherite_tier_upgrade]] (after Diamond) | 3 smithing templates |

## Decorated backpacks

Twenty-four quests, one per backpack type, each with a small themed reward:

The quest subtitles hint at each backpack's ability, for example "No More Fall Damage!" for the Blaze backpack and "Hearty As A Warden!" for the Warden backpack, whose quest gives a [[item:deeperdarker:heart_of_the_deep]]. The Emerald and Villager quests give a roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Other tables|Lucky]].
