The **Hunter's Journal** is a side chapter of kill-count quests in the Tales of Soulrend group. Each mob has a ladder of quests, and each rung needs more kills than the last. Kills count from the moment the rung is open, so the ladders fill up naturally while playing the acts.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/a-hunters-beginning|A Hunter's Beginning]] in Act I opens **The Hunter's Journal** checkmark (reward: 1 Gold Ingot). Every ladder starts from it.

> [!LORE] The Hunter's Journal
> Every great adventurer starts somewhere.
>
> The creatures you defeat and the battles you survive become part of your story. This is merely the first entry in a much larger chronicle.

## Quests

### Zombies, skeletons and spiders

[[enemies/act-i/zombie|Zombies]], [[enemies/act-i/skeleton|Skeletons]] and [[enemies/act-i/spider|Spiders]] each have the same four-step ladder:

| Kills | Rewards |
| --- | --- |
| 50 | 24 [[item:minecraft:gold_ingot]] |
| 200 | A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables\|Chipped Gems]], a roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Rare Loot\|Rare Loot]] |
| 500 | A roll on Flawed Gems, 5 [[item:dungeons_and_combat:estus_flask]] |
| 1,000 | Two rolls on Flawed Gems |

### Other mobs

| Mob | Kills | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[enemies/act-ii/necromancer\|Necromancer]] | 5, then 25 | 16, then 64 [[item:irons_spellbooks:arcane_essence]] |
| [[enemies/act-ii/hollow\|Hollow]] | 5, then 25 | 16 Iron and 16 Steel Ingots, then 32 of each |
| Enderman | 5, then 25 | 5, then 10 [[item:minecraft:ender_pearl]] |
| [[enemies/act-iii/piglin-and-piglin-brute\|Piglin]] | 50, then 250 | 4, then 16 [[item:minecraft:gold_block]] |
| [[enemies/act-iii/piglin-and-piglin-brute\|Piglin Brute]] | 50, then 250 | 4, then 16 Blocks of Gold |
| [[enemies/act-iii/wither-skeleton\|Wither Skeleton]] | 25, then 75 | 1, then 5 [[item:minecraft:wither_skeleton_skull]] |

Only the exact mob counts: husks don't count as zombies, and strays don't count as skeletons.

## Tips

- The Wither Skeleton ladder gives six skulls in total, enough for two Withers ([[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/confront-the-wither|Confront The Wither]]).
- Damage dealt to Hollows never counts toward Soulrend levels, so the Hollow ladder is for its ingots only.
