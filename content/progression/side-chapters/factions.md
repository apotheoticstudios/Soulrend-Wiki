**Factions** is a side chapter about Valarian Conquest's eight factions, each with its own castle, gear and units. Defeating a faction's soldiers earns its citizenship item; consuming a citizenship makes that faction's units neutral or willing to follow your commands.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-i-awakening/new-faces-new-stories|New Faces, New Stories]] in Act I opens the **Factions** checkmark ("Many Houses Scatter the Realm"), which gives a [[item:valarian_conquest:nomad_citizenship]].

> [!LORE] Factions
> There are 8 factions in Soulrend, each with their own unique gear, units, and citizenship.
>
> To earn citizenship to any faction, you must defeat the Soldiers of said faction and claim citizenship. Once obtaining citizenship you may consume it to make all units of that faction neutral or follow your commands.

## Quests

Each faction quest has two tasks: find the faction's castle, and kill a number of Valarian soldiers (`valarian_conquest:soldier`, any faction).

| Faction | Structure | Soldiers | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Valarian | Valarian Castle | 10 | [[item:valarian_conquest:valarian_citizenship]], a roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Other tables\|Lucky]] |
| Burgundian | Burgundian Castle | 20 | [[item:valarian_conquest:burgundian_citizenship]], [[item:ironchests:copper_chest_upgrade]] |
| Orleanian | Orleanian Castle | 30 | [[item:valarian_conquest:orleanian_citizenship]], [[item:ironchests:iron_chest_upgrade]] |
| Hospitaller | Hospitaller Castle | 40 | [[item:valarian_conquest:hospitaller_swordsman_item]], [[item:ironchests:gold_chest_upgrade]] |
| Templar | Templar Castle | 50 | [[item:valarian_conquest:templar_citizenship]], [[item:ironchests:diamond_chest_upgrade]] |
| Bohemian | Bohemian Outpost | 60 | [[item:valarian_conquest:bohemian_citizenship]], [[item:ironchests:crystal_chest_upgrade]] |
| Visgothian | Visgothian Castle | 70 | [[item:valarian_conquest:visigothian_citizenship]], [[item:ironchests:obsidian_chest_upgrade]] |
| Barathian | Barathian Castle | 80 | [[item:valarian_conquest:barathian_citizenship]], [[item:ironchests:netherite_chest_upgrade]] |

> [!NOTE]
> The Hospitaller quest gives a Hospitaller Swordsman unit item, not a Hospitaller citizenship.

## Tips

- Soldier kills count toward every faction quest that is open at the time, so open several at once.
