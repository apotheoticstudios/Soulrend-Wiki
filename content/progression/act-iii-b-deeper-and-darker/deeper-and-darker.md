**Deeper and Darker** ("This act is optional") is the first quest of [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker|Act III-b]]. It is a checkmark that gives an [[item:minecraft:echo_shard]] and opens the [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/ancient-temple|Ancient Temple]] quest.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-otherside|The Otherside]] (Act II)

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Deeper and Darker**.

> [!LORE] Deeper and Darker (Act III-b)
> Curiosity has led many adventurers underground in search of answers.
>
> Beyond the ancient portal lies a world of endless gloom, mysterious echoes, and horrors lurking just beyond sight. The Depths conceal powerful relics and terrifying foes alike.
>
> If you seek the truth hidden beneath the world, prepare to embrace the darkness.
