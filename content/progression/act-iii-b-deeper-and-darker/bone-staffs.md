The **Bone Staffs** are five [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker|Act III-b]] quests, one for each staff the [[Corrupted Champion]] can drop. Each kill drops one staff, chosen at random with equal odds, so collecting all five takes at least five kills and usually more.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/defeat-corrupted-champion|Defeat Corrupted Champion]]

## Objectives

| Quest | Item | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staff of Zatraq'gul | [[item:graveyard:white_bone_staff]] | 3 XP levels |
| Staff of Nortoq'gul | [[item:graveyard:black_bone_staff]] | 3 XP levels |
| Staff of Azrata'gul | [[item:graveyard:red_bone_staff]] | 3 XP levels |
| Staff of Mynakta'gul | [[item:graveyard:cyan_bone_staff]] | 3 XP levels |
| Staff of Xaphnok'gul | [[item:graveyard:purple_bone_staff]] | 3 XP levels |

## Walkthrough

1. Kill the Corrupted Champion and pick up its staff.
2. For the other staffs, summon it again. Each fight needs a new [[item:deeperdarker:sculk_transmitter]] from an Ancient Temple.

## Next
