**Act III-b – Deeper and Darker** is an optional chapter of The Exalted Path. In the quest book it is titled "Act III - Deeper and Darker"; the wiki calls it Act III-b to tell it apart from [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames|Act III – Into The Flames]]. It opens from Act II's [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-otherside|The Otherside]] quest, takes place in and around the Otherside dimension, and ends with the [[Corrupted Champion]]. Nothing in the main line depends on it.
