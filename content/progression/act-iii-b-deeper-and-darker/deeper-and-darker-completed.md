**Act III Deeper and Darker - Completed** closes [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker|Act III-b]]. It is a checkmark that opens after the Corrupted Champion kill and gives one random Perfect Gem. Unlike the other acts' closing quests, it doesn't lead anywhere: its subtitle sends the player back to the main line.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/defeat-corrupted-champion|Defeat Corrupted Champion]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Act III Deeper and Darker - Completed**.

## Rewards

- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Perfect Gems]] (one ancient-rarity gem)
