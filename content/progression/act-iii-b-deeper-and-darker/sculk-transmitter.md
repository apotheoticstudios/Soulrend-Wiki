**Sculk Transmitter** ("Used to summon...") is an [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker|Act III-b]] quest for a [[item:deeperdarker:sculk_transmitter]]. In Soulrend the transmitter is the summoning item for the [[Corrupted Champion]]: using it on the altar in a Lich Prison wakes the boss.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/ancient-temple|Ancient Temple]]

## Objectives

Have a Sculk Transmitter. It has no crafting recipe; the secret chest of an [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/ancient-temple|Ancient Temple]] always holds one.

## Rewards

- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Rare Loot|Rare Loot]]
- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Chipped Gems]]

## Walkthrough

1. Loot the Ancient Temple's secret chest.
2. Keep the transmitter for the Lich Prison. Using it on the altar uses it up, so a second fight needs a second transmitter.
