**Defeat Corrupted Champion** is the boss quest of [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker|Act III-b]]. The [[Corrupted Champion]] (`graveyard:lich`) is level 300 to 310, higher than any Act III Sin. It is summoned by using a [[item:deeperdarker:sculk_transmitter]] on the altar in a [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/lich-prison|Lich Prison]].

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/lich-prison|Lich Prison]]
- A [[item:deeperdarker:sculk_transmitter]]

## Objectives

Kill the Corrupted Champion.

> [!LORE] Defeat Corrupted Champion (Act III-b)
> Legends speak of a champion who stood above all others.
>
> Now, only a corrupted shell remains. Driven by madness and empowered by darkness, the fallen warrior commands an endless tide of lesser foes to fight in its stead.
>
> Cut through the horde, stand your ground, and bring an end to the Champion's torment.

## Rewards

- 5 XP levels
- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Flawed Gems]]
- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Rare Loot|Rare Loot]]

## Walkthrough

1. Stand at the altar and use the Sculk Transmitter on it. The boss appears above the altar with the message "The dead stir...". The transmitter is used up.
2. Fight it. See [[Corrupted Champion#Strategy]] for its summons and attacks.
3. It always drops one of five bone staffs, which open the [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/bone-staffs|Bone Staffs]] quests.
