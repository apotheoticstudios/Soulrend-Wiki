**Ancient Temple** is an [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker|Act III-b]] quest to find an Ancient Temple, a Deeper and Darker structure in the Otherside. Its secret chest always contains a [[item:deeperdarker:sculk_transmitter]], the item the next quests revolve around.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iii-b-deeper-and-darker/deeper-and-darker|Deeper and Darker]]

## Objectives

Find an Ancient Temple (`deeperdarker:ancient_temple`).

> [!LORE] Ancient Temple (Act III-b)
> Buried beneath the darkness of the Depths stands a monument to a civilization long forgotten.
>
> Though time has worn away its grandeur, the Ancient Temple still guards the secrets of those who came before. Its halls are filled with hidden dangers, valuable relics, and mysteries waiting to be uncovered.
>
> Tread carefully. Some knowledge was buried for a reason.

## Rewards

- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Chipped Gems]]

## Walkthrough

1. Enter the Otherside through its portal (see [[progression/act-ii-first-steps/the-otherside|The Otherside]]).
2. Search for an Ancient Temple with an Explorer's Compass.
3. Find the temple's secret chest. Its loot table has a single entry, the Sculk Transmitter.
