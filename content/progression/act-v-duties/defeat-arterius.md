**Defeat Arterius** is the other boss line of [[progression/act-v-duties|Act V]]. The player enters the Gate of Disaster, a NightFall: Invade structure that Soulrend places only in the Past, and kills [[Arterius, the Last Watcher]]. Together with the [[progression/act-v-duties/defeat-the-scarlet-hunter|Scarlet Hunter]] kill, it completes [[progression/act-v-duties/blessing-of-mortusest|Blessing of Mortusest]].

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-v-duties/visit-the-past|Visit the Past]]

## Objectives

| Quest | Task | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter Gate Of Disaster | Find `nightfall_invade:gate_of_disaster` | 5 XP levels |
| Defeat The Last Watcher Arterius | Kill Arterius | 50 XP levels, a roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables\|Perfect Gems]], [[item:efn:hf_murasama]] |

## Walkthrough

1. Search the Dead Wasteland biome of the Past for the Gate of Disaster. See [[Arterius, the Last Watcher#Location]].
2. At level 200 Arterius has 728,400 HP. A single hit can take at most 8% of it, and after 4% of its health is gone it starts reducing damage, up to 90%. Sustained damage works better than burst; see [[Arterius, the Last Watcher#Strategy]].
