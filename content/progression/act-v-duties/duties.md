**Duties** is the first quest of [[progression/act-v-duties|Act V]]. It is a checkmark with no reward that opens the two ingot quests.

## Requirements

- **Act V - Duties**, the closing quest of Act IV (see [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/end-highlands#Act V - Duties|End Highlands]])

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Duties**.

> Yet one final journey remains. Beyond the veil lies a forgotten realm where ancient horrors and fallen champions still endure.
