**Defeat The Scarlet Hunter** is one of the two boss lines of [[progression/act-v-duties|Act V]]. The player first enters the Ancient Church, a NightFall: Invade structure that Soulrend places only in the Past, then kills its master, [[Ó Brolacháin, the Scarlet Hunter]]. The kill is half of what [[progression/act-v-duties/blessing-of-mortusest|Blessing of Mortusest]] needs.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-v-duties/visit-the-past|Visit the Past]]

## Objectives

| Quest | Task | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter Ancient Church | Find `nightfall_invade:ancient_church` | 5 XP levels |
| Defeat Scarlet Hunter Ó Brolacháin | Kill the Scarlet Hunter | 50 XP levels, a roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables\|Perfect Gems]], [[item:efn:yamato_dmc_in_sheath]] |

## Walkthrough

1. Search the Dead Wasteland biome of the Past for the Ancient Church. Fallen Knights guard it. See [[Ó Brolacháin, the Scarlet Hunter#Location]].
2. Prepare for a long fight: at level 200 the Scarlet Hunter has 728,400 HP, a single hit can take at most 4% of it, and its damage reduction builds up as it takes damage. See [[Ó Brolacháin, the Scarlet Hunter#Strategy]].
3. The YAMATO reward has its own skill books in the [[progression/side-chapters/epic-fight|Epic Fight]] chapter.
