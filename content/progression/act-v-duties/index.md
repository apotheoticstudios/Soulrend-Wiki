**Act V – Duties** is the last chapter of The Exalted Path. It opens from the end of [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]] and leads to the Past (`graveyard:past`), a dimension of endless war. Soulrend raises its three bosses (Arterius, the Scarlet Hunter and the Nightlord) to 12,000 base health. The act asks for two kinds of Universium-based ingots, the [[item:kubejs:emblem_of_the_past]] made from them, and two boss kills in the Past.

## After Act V

Blessing of Mortusest is the last quest of the main line. Its subtitle reads: "Mortusest will come to congratulate you. Now all you can do is wait." The Galactic Core from the Decaying King also opens the Esoteric Reforging Table in [[progression/side-chapters/reforging|Reforging]].
