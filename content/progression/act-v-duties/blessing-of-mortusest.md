**Blessing of Mortusest** is the final quest of The Exalted Path. It opens once both [[Arterius, the Last Watcher]] and [[Ó Brolacháin, the Scarlet Hunter]] are dead, and its checkmark gives the [[item:cataclysm:zweiender]]. Mortusest is named once more in the quest book: Act II's Dead King quest calls the Dead King "the crazed servant of Mortusest".

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-v-duties/defeat-arterius|Defeat The Last Watcher Arterius]]
- [[progression/act-v-duties/defeat-the-scarlet-hunter|Defeat Scarlet Hunter Ó Brolacháin]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Blessing of Mortusest**.

> [!LORE] Blessing of Mortusest (Act V)
> Mortusest will come to congratulate you. Now all you can do is wait.

## Rewards

- [[item:cataclysm:zweiender]]

## Next

- The Zweiender quest in Path of the Samurai (see [[progression/side-chapters/pursuit-of-mastery|Pursuit of Mastery]]) depends on this quest.
- No main-line quest comes after it.
