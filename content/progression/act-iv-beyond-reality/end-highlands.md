**End Highlands** is the [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]] quest that completes on entering the End Highlands, the tall biome of the outer End islands. It is the act's last required step: the **Act V - Duties** quest that opens [[progression/act-v-duties|Act V]] depends on it, and so do the Void Cathedral quests.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/slay-the-ender-dragon|Slay The Ender Dragon]]

## Objectives

Enter the End Highlands biome (`minecraft:end_highlands`).

The quest subtitle warns: "Prepare yourself before exploring the End Highlands, monsters there can be very powerful." See [[enemies/act-iv/end-highlands-mobs|End Highlands Mobs]].

## Rewards

- [[item:waystones:warp_scroll]]
- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Chipped Gems]]

## Walkthrough

1. After the dragon dies, an End Gateway opens at the edge of the main island. Throw an Ender Pearl into it to reach the outer islands.
2. Walk until the biome changes to End Highlands. Place a Waystone nearby for later trips.

## Act V - Duties

**Act V - Duties** (subtitle "Act IV - Completed") is the octagon checkmark that closes the act. It needs only End Highlands, not the Nightwarden. Its reward, "Move into Act V", opens [[progression/act-v-duties/duties|Duties]].

## Manifestation of Infinity

**Manifestation of Infinity** ("...And the soldiers march for all of eternity.") also opens after End Highlands. It asks for an [[item:apotheosis:ancient_material]], the ancient-rarity Apotheosis material, and gives a roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Flawed Gems]]. The material comes from salvaging ancient gear (see [[features/gear-and-crafting/scrapping|Scrapping]]); the [[progression/side-chapters/reforging|Reforging]] chapter says the End is where most ancient gear is found. The Emblem of the Past in Act V needs one.
