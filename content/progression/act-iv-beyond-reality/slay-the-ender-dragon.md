**Slay The Ender Dragon** ("Free The End") is the boss quest of [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]]. It completes with the vanilla "Free the End" advancement for killing the [[Ender Dragon]], which Soulrend pins at level 467. It opens the [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/end-highlands|End Highlands]] quest and so the way to Act V.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/beyond-reality|Beyond Reality]]

## Objectives

Kill the Ender Dragon.

> [!LORE] Slay The Ender Dragon (Act IV)
> At the edge of reality, where only darkness remains, an ancient ruler watches over the Void.
>
> The Ender Dragon is more than a beast, it is a relic of a forgotten age, a guardian standing between you and the truth hidden beyond.
>
> Face the dragon and prove that even legends can fall.

## Rewards

- [[item:fantasy_armor:dragonslayer_helmet]], with one gem socket
- [[item:cataclysm:storm_eye]], the key to [[Scylla]]'s dimension (see [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/the-eight-sins|The Eight Sins]])
- [[item:minecraft:dragon_head]]

The [[progression/side-chapters/dragonslayer|Dragonslayer]] chapter gives the matching Dragonslayer Chestplate.

## Walkthrough

1. Read [[Ender Dragon#Strategy]]. At level 467 it has 28,160 HP.
2. Destroy the End Crystals on the pillars, then fight the dragon.
3. After the kill, take the [[item:minecraft:dragon_egg]] from the exit portal for the [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/dragon-egg|Dragon Egg]] quest.
