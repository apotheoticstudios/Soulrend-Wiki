The **Cradle Of Will** quest is the last, optional quest of [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]]. It opens after [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/defeat-the-nightwarden|the Nightwarden]] and asks for a [[item:traveloptics:cradle_of_will]]; its reward is a [[item:traveloptics:void_manuscript]].

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/defeat-the-nightwarden|Defeat The Nightwarden]]

## Objectives

Have a Cradle Of Will. The item doesn't need to be handed in.

## Rewards

- [[item:traveloptics:void_manuscript]]

## Usage

Both items lead to the [[bosses/optional-and-world/fading-mage|Fading Mage]]:

- The Cradle Of Will locates the Fading Mage when right-clicked, and it is a weapon upgrade item.
- The Void Manuscript is mainly used as currency for trading with the Fading Mage.
