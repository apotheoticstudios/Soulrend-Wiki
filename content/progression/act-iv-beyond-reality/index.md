**Act IV – Beyond Reality** is the fourth chapter of The Exalted Path, set in the End. It opens from the [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/act-iv-beyond-reality-gate|Act IV gate]] at the end of Act III. The main line is short: kill the [[Ender Dragon]], then step into the End Highlands, which opens [[progression/act-v-duties|Act V]]. The act's best rewards are on an optional line in the Void Cathedral, ending with [[The Nightwarden]].

## Main line

1. [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/beyond-reality|Beyond Reality]]: a checkmark.
2. [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/slay-the-ender-dragon|Slay The Ender Dragon]]
3. [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/end-highlands|End Highlands]]: visit the biome.
4. **Act V - Duties**: a checkmark that opens [[progression/act-v-duties|Act V]] (see [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/end-highlands#Act V - Duties|End Highlands]]).
