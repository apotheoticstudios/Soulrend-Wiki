**Your test begins.** is the optional boss quest of [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]]: kill [[The Nightwarden]], a level 500 Traveloptics boss in the [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/void-cathedral|Void Cathedral]]. It has the biggest reward in the act, 250 XP levels and two Universium Ingots, but nothing requires it: Act V opens from [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/end-highlands|End Highlands]] alone.

## Requirements

1. [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/void-cathedral|Find Void Cathedral]]
2. The **Counterspell scroll** quest (below)

## Objectives

Kill the Nightwarden.

> [!LORE] Your test begins. (Act IV)
> A silent sentinel of the void, bound to shadows older than time. Face the Nightwarden, and prove your strength against the final darkness.

### Counterspell scroll

The quest before it ("Useful on black holes", optional) asks for a level 1 **Counterspell** scroll from Iron's Spellbooks and gives 2 XP levels. Its subtitle hints that the scroll is meant for this fight.

## Rewards

- 250 XP levels
- 2 [[item:astral_calamity_extras:universium_ingot]]
- Two picks from [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Perfect Gems]]
- [[item:skilltree:wisdom_scroll]]

## Walkthrough

1. Get a Counterspell scroll and claim the scroll quest.
2. Read [[The Nightwarden#Strategy]]. At level 500 it has 90,420 HP.
3. Fight it in the Void Cathedral with the Counterspell scroll ready.
