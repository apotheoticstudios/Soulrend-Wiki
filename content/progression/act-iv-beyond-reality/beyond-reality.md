**Beyond Reality** is the first quest of [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]]. It is a checkmark, and its only reward is a [[item:minecraft:carved_pumpkin]]: worn as a helmet, it stops Endermen from getting angry when you look at them.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/act-iv-beyond-reality-gate|Act IV – Beyond Reality Gate]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Beyond Reality**.

> [!LORE] Beyond Reality (Act IV)
> The flames of the Inferno are behind you.
>
> Ahead lies a realm untouched by time, suspended between existence and oblivion. Countless heroes have sought the answers hidden within the Void, yet few returned to tell their tale.
>
> Beyond Reality, powerful foes guard the path forward. The Dragon watches from above, while something far older stirs in the darkness.
