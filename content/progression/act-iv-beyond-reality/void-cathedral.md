**Find Void Cathedral** ("A forgotten cathedral drifting beyond reality, where silence reigns eternal") is an [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality|Act IV]] quest for the Void Cathedral, a Traveloptics structure in the End Highlands. The cathedral is home to [[The Nightwarden]] and a [[bosses/optional-and-world/voidshelf-golem|Voidshelf Golem]]. The quest opens the Obsidian Bookshelf quest and the optional Nightwarden line.

## Requirements

- [[progression/act-iv-beyond-reality/end-highlands|End Highlands]]

## Objectives

Find the Void Cathedral. It generates in the End Highlands, at most once per 90 × 90 chunk area.

## Rewards

- 3 XP levels
- A roll on [[progression/reward-tables#Gem tables|Chipped Gems]]

## Obsidian Bookshelf

**Obsidian Bookshelf** ("Maximum POWER!") asks for a [[item:traveloptics:obsidian_bookshelf]] and gives 2 XP levels. Its tooltip calls it "A very rare bookshelf": it adds 15 Eterna (up to 50) and 8.5% Rectification to an enchanting table. See [[progression/side-chapters/enchanting|Enchanting Module]].
