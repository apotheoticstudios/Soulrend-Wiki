**Questing** ("Using the Quest Book") is a Prologue quest that explains how the quest book is organized. The book's chapters sit in five groups, and the group a chapter is in says what kind of content it covers.

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Questing**.

> [!LORE] Questing (Prologue)
> In Soulrend you will use quests to progress through your adventure. From earning rewards to slaying powerful bosses, and learning how to use mechanics in the game, the Quest Book will be your player's companion.
>
> - The Exalted Path The Exalted Path is the main storyline of Soulrend, guiding you through every major stage of your adventure.
> - Rise of the Champion will walk you through basic gear progression.
> - Tales of Soulrend contains the many stories, legends, and challenges scattered throughout the world.
> - Legendary Armoury has information on specialized equipment that will aid your along your path.
> - The Fabled Realms will teach you about the realms of Soulrend, and will help you settle these hostile lands.
