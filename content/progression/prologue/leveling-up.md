**Leveling Up** ("Grow in Power") is a Prologue quest that introduces the leveling screen. Soulrend levels come from damage dealt: once a player passes a damage threshold they gain a level, which gives one attribute point and one skill point. The quest is a checkmark and unlocks [[progression/prologue/the-skill-tree|The Skill Tree]].

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Leveling Up**.

> [!LORE] Leveling Up (Prologue)
> You can reach the leveling screen through a tab in your inventory menu, there you will find different attributes you can level up. You have to pass a damage threshold to Level Up.

## Rewards

- 1 XP level

## Walkthrough

1. Open the inventory (I with Soulrend's default key bindings) and find the leveling tab.
2. Spend attribute points there as they come in.

See [[features/character/levelling-stats|Leveling Stats]] for what each attribute does and [[features/character/skill-points-from-damage|Skill Points from Damage]] for how the damage threshold works.
