The **Prologue** is the first chapter of the quest book. Its quests explain Soulrend's main systems: Epic Fight combat, leveling, the skill tree, origins, the quest book itself, waystones and storage. Almost every Prologue quest is a checkmark that completes when clicked, and most give a small reward. Only the first quest, [[progression/prologue/welcome-to-soulrend|Welcome to Soulrend]], is needed to start [[progression/act-i-awakening|Act I – Awakening]]; the rest are optional.
