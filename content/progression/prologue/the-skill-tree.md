**The Skill Tree** ("Unlock Unique Perks") is a Prologue quest that introduces Soulrend's second progression system, the passive [[skill-trees|skill tree]]. Skill points come from leveling up, which comes from dealing damage. The quest is a checkmark and gives an [[item:skilltree:amnesia_scroll]], which resets the tree so its points can be spent again.

## Requirements

- [[progression/prologue/leveling-up|Leveling Up]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **The Skill Tree**.

> [!LORE] The Skill Tree (Prologue)
> The second system of progression is the Skill Trees, you can access the Skill Trees by pressing [O] (default) or with the tab in your inventory menu, there, you will find hundreds of skills and perks to unlock.
>
> To gain skill points you must deal damage, tracked in the levelling menu. When you Level Up you gain 1 skill and 1 attribute point.

## Rewards

- [[item:skilltree:amnesia_scroll]] (resets your passive skill tree)

## Walkthrough

1. Press O, or use the skill tree tab in the inventory.
2. Spend points from the root outward. Keep the Amnesia Scroll for when a build needs to change.

> [!TIP]
> Quests later in the book give [[item:skilltree:wisdom_scroll]]s, which grant one passive skill point each. The Cataclysm boss kills in [[progression/act-iii-into-the-flames/the-eight-sins|The Eight Sins]] give one per boss.
