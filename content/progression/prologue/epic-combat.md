**Epic Combat** is a Prologue quest that introduces Epic Fight, the combat mod that gives Soulrend's weapons their movesets, dodges and guards. Clicking its checkmark gives four Epic Fight skill books: two guard skills and two dodge skills.

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Combat Mode [R]**.

> [!LORE] Epic Combat (Prologue)
> Press [Middle-Button] (mouse) to lock-on to enemies
>
> Press [Left-Alt] to perform a dodge roll or step
>
> Press [Right-Click] (mouse) to guard against attack
>
> With the special combat mode you will harness special weapon skills and use epic fighting movesets unique to gear, as well as being able to mine.

## Rewards

Four [[item:epicfight:skillbook]]s, one of each:

| Skill | Mod |
| --- | --- |
| Parry | EpicFight Nightfall (`efn:efn_parry`) |
| Guard | Epic Fight (`epicfight:guard`) |
| Soul-like Dodge | EpicFight Nightfall (`efn:efn_dodge`) |
| Step | Epic Fight (`epicfight:step`) |

Right-click a skill book to learn its skill. See [[features/combat/combat-skill-books|Combat Skill Books]] for the skill slots.

## Walkthrough

1. Click the checkmark and collect the four books.
2. Right-click the books to learn the skills you want to use.
3. Practice the keys from the quest text: middle mouse to lock on, Left Alt to dodge, right mouse to guard.

> [!NOTE]
> The quest names **R** as the combat mode key, but Soulrend's default key bindings leave Epic Fight's "switch mode" key unbound. Bind it in the Controls menu if it doesn't respond.
