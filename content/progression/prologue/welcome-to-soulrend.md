**Welcome to Soulrend** ("The Realms Need A Hero") is the first quest in the quest book. It is a checkmark: clicking it in the quest book completes it. It gives no reward, but every other Prologue quest and the first quest of [[progression/act-i-awakening|Act I – Awakening]] depend on it.

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **The Realms**.

> [!LORE] Welcome to Soulrend (Prologue)
> These lands have countless to conquer, forge your way through Soulrend with thousands of quests to be unlocked.

## Walkthrough

1. Open the quest book and select the Prologue chapter.
2. Click Welcome to Soulrend, then click its checkmark.

The quest book uses linear progression: a quest's tasks can't be completed until the quests it depends on are done. Item tasks check the inventory, so items picked up early still count once the quest opens.
