**New Quest UI** is a Prologue quest about Soulrend's custom quest screen. Pressing **J** opens a redesigned view of the same quests. The standard FTB Quests window still works and shows the same progress.

## Requirements

- [[progression/prologue/jei-menu|JEI Menu]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **New Quest UI**.

> [!LORE] New Quest UI (Prologue)
> Soulrend comes with a custom Quest UI, designed to make your adventure feel more immersive and rewarding.
>
> Although the standard FTB Quests menu remains available, pressing J will open a completely redesigned quest interface, offering a fresh way to follow your journey through the world.

## Walkthrough

- Press J for the new interface.
- For the standard FTB Quests window, right-click a [[item:ftbquests:book]]. Soulrend's default key bindings leave the FTB Quests key unbound, so set one in Controls to open it without the book.
