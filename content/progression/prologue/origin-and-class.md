**Origin and Class** is a Prologue quest about the race and class every player picks when they first join. Its reward is one [[item:origins:orb_of_origin]], which opens the origin picker again so both choices can be made over.

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Origins and Classes**.

> [!LORE] Origin and Class (Prologue)
> Congratulations, you've started your journey with a selected Race and Class, but what if you don't actually want what you chose?
>
> Use this Orb of Origin to reselect, but be careful, you can only use it once.

## Rewards

- [[item:origins:orb_of_origin]]

## Walkthrough

1. Read the [[origins|Origins]] pages for the races and classes before using the orb.
2. Right-click the orb to pick again. The quest gives only one orb.
