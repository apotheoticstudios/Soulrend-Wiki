**Looting and Pickup Mechanics** is a Prologue quest that explains Soulrend's item pickup. Items on the ground are not collected by walking over them. They are picked up one at a time by right-clicking them, or all at once by crouching.

## Requirements

- [[progression/prologue/origin-and-class|Origin and Class]]

## Objectives

Click the checkmark task, **Complete**.

> [!LORE] Looting and Pickup Mechanics (Prologue)
> Pickup mechanics have been changed to fit a looting aspect, now items will drop on the ground and can be picked up by Right Clicking.
>
> Alternatively, you can pick up all drops around yourself like vanilla by Crouching, useful for items amongst grass.

## Rewards

- 1 XP level
