**Black Frost Dragon** (in game, only a name: **Black Frost**) is a name in Ice and Fire's language file with no entity behind it. Version 2.1.13 doesn't register a Black Frost entity, so it can't spawn and can't be summoned in Soulrend.

The [[Dread Queen]] is unfinished in the same way. The [[Dread Lich]] is the Dread caster that does spawn. For the dragons that exist, see the [[Fire Dragon]], [[Ice Dragon]] and [[Lightning Dragon]].
